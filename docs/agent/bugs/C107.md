### C107 — Dry Farming skips the Feeding the Future plant farms (P3) `[cand 2026-09-18: source-verified; control not run]`

#### Report

Steam comment, 2026-09-18 (-=Maure=-): the breakthrough *"is supposed to reduce water consumption
of all farms by half"* but applies only to Farm, Hydroponic Farm and Open Farm by label; they propose
the `AllFarms` label, *"the same way the Agronomist Commander effect applies"*. The gap is real; the
proposed scope is not (see Intent).

#### Source (1.1.0.403908 unless stated)

- `Data/Tech.lua:769-802`, tech `DryFarming`. Description and short description say *"Water
  requirements of crops are reduced by <percent(param1)>"* (`:770`, `:782`), with no qualifier.
  Effects: three `Effect_ModifyLabel`, `water_consumption` −50%, on labels `Farm`, `HydroponicFarm`,
  `OpenFarm` (`:787-801`). 1.0.7.396349 carries the same three in `Data/TechPreset.lua`.
- `Building:AddToCityLabels` adds a building to `self.class` only (`Lua/Buildings/Building.lua:460`),
  and the class is the template id, so `FarmUnderground` never joins `Farm` despite
  `object_class = "FarmConventional"` and `prg_class = "Farm"`.
- Crop farms set their base water from the crop: `FarmBase:PlantNextCrop` →
  `SetBase("water_consumption", cropdef.WaterDemand)` (`Lua/Buildings/Farm.lua:619`); the getter
  applies modifiers (`:825`).
- Farm templates and whether each inherits `FarmBase` (script over the archived tree, template
  `object_class` walked through `DefineClass` parents):

| template | class | FarmBase | Dry Farming |
|---|---|---|---|
| Farm, HydroponicFarm | FarmConventional, FarmHydroponic | yes | paid |
| OpenFarm | OpenFarmBase (VegetationPlant) | no | paid |
| FungalFarm, FungalFarm_Asteroid | FungalFarmBase (ResourceProducer) | no | not paid, fixed 500 water |
| **FarmSmall, FarmSmallUnderground** (norman) | FarmSmallBase → FarmConventional | yes | **not paid** |
| **FarmUnderground** (norman) | FarmConventional | yes | **not paid** |
| **AutomatedFarm** (norman) | AutomatedFarmBase → FarmBase | yes | **not paid** |
| FarmInsect (norman) | FarmInsectBase → FarmBase | yes | not paid |

  Crop water demand in `DLC/norman/Presets/CropPreset.lua`, by `FarmClass`: FarmSmall 11 crops,
  800–1500; FarmUnderground 6, 500–2400; FarmSmallUnderground 5, 250–1200; AutomatedFarm 5, 3000
  each (coffee, basil, chili, sorghum…); FarmInsect 6, 0–500.

#### Intent

- **Tell 4 (self-contradiction):** the tech's own text promises every crop and its enumeration pays
  three buildings. Same shape as [F95](F95.md).
- **The curation rule is recoverable, and it is not `AllFarms`.** In base 1.0.7 the `AllFarms`
  label already existed (`Data/Label.lua:8`) and held exactly Farm, HydroponicFarm, OpenFarm,
  FungalFarm and FungalFarm_Asteroid (1.0.7 `Data/BuildingTemplate/*Farm*.lua`, `label3`/`label4`).
  The author enumerated that set minus the two Fungal Farms, which already drew 500 water. So the
  rule is *plant-growing farms*: the fungi were left out on purpose. The four norman plant farms fit
  that rule and are missed; FarmInsect follows the fungi and stays out. The reporter's `AllFarms`
  swap would also pay the Fungal and Insect Farms, contrary to the base author's choice (OpenFarm
  carries `label3 = "AllFarms"`, so it would not be lost).
- **Counter-reading, recorded:** the DLC authors could have left their farms out for balance, since
  the small farms already draw little water. Nothing in the source says so, and the same DLC wrote
  its Agronomist bonus on `AllFarms` (`DLC/norman/Presets/CommanderProfilePreset.lua:21`), which
  shows it expected farm-wide bonuses to reach its new farms. Veto point if the owner reads it the
  other way.

#### Repair (not built)

A §1.1 additive preset patch as F95: four `Effect_ModifyLabel` entries (labels `FarmSmall`,
`FarmSmallUnderground`, `FarmUnderground`, `AutomatedFarm`; `water_consumption`, −50) appended to
`Presets.TechPreset`'s `DryFarming`, plus a load-time heal for saves where the tech is already
researched, as `Code/Fix_AstrogeologistExtractors.lua` does.

#### Control — not yet run

On a Feeding the Future save, build a Farm and an Underground Farm, each with a crop planted, and
note each panel's water consumption. Grant Dry Farming. **The claim holds** if the Farm's figure
halves and the Underground Farm's does not. **It is refuted** if both halve (some other route
covers the DLC farms) or neither does (the modifier path is not what this entry says).

#### Reach

R1 for any owner of Feeding the Future (norman DLC, EF-078) who researches the breakthrough: all
four templates are in `build_category = "Farms"` or `"LifeSupport"` with no `hide_from_build_menu`.

#### Not opened

The norman `DLC/.../Code/BuildingTemplate/*.generated.lua` bodies beyond the lines cited; any
`OnResearched` or later code that might add the modifiers another way (the control covers it);
Fungal Farm's norman crop presets, which its non-FarmBase class does not appear to consume.
