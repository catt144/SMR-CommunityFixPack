### F127 — OUR OWN DEFECT: the C83 arrival reroute leaves the old bed reserved (P3) `[cand 2026-09-24: mechanism confirmed; harm unvalidated]`

#### Must_Read_Header

A defect in our shipped `Fix_ArrivalDeaths`, not in vanilla. Migration audit P2, confirmed by the
independent [cross-check](../reports/MIGRATION_CROSSCHECK_2026-09-24.md). Not built. The owner added it
to the hub release set on 2026-09-24.

#### Source

- Vanilla reserves a bed for each applicant in the dome the rocket chose
  (`Lua/Buildings/RocketBase.lua:2072-2073`, archived 1.1.1.405907).
- Our C83 path (`Code/Fix_ArrivalDeaths.lua:485-486`, pack at `21a16ec`) moves an arrival away from an
  unwelcoming dome by rewriting `emigration_dome` and `emigration_elevator`. It leaves
  `reserved_residence` in the rejected dome.
- On arrival, `UpdateResidence` (`Colonist.lua:3123-3130`) sees the reservation is in another dome and
  asks the new dome for a home. Settling into one cancels the old hold (`Residence.lua:111`).
- If the new dome is full, no home is found. `Colonist.lua:3095` then keeps the colonist off the
  Homeless list because its old reservation is still valid, and the bed in the rejected dome stays
  held until the stale-reservation sweep (`Fix_StaleReservations`) releases it.

#### Harm

HYPOTHESIS. A rerouted arrival in a full dome is homeless but not counted as homeless, so the
player's homeless figure and housing alerts undercount it, and a bed elsewhere is blocked for a
while. This is exactly the C83 fallback case: the working destination C83 picks may be full. Early
to mid game, when domes fill and rockets keep landing.

#### Control (desk first)

- **Supports:** after the reroute into a full dome, `reserved_residence.parent_dome` is the rejected
  dome and the colonist is missing from the Homeless label.
- **Refutes:** the reservation already moves or clears with the reroute, or the colonist is labelled
  Homeless anyway.
- The cross-check asks for the full-destination branch to be validated before any repair.

#### Fix shape

Cancel or move the reservation together with the reroute, inside the same C83 branch. Keep the
unrerouted path byte for byte native.

#### Release (owner ruling, 2026-09-24)

Joins the hub release set (C114, C115, C116, C117, C42, C111, F127 and the P3 pins in
`Fix_VacuumWalks`). The owner: "if p3 is just makeing something better we already ship or correcting
it and its ready to go we shoul,d just add it same with p2." The set ships in one release, never
alone; the ruling's words are in [C114](C114.md).
