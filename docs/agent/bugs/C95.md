#### ⭐⭐ THE PRECEDENT — vanilla ALREADY carves habitat residents out of the labour pool, with the exact predicate this repair needs

⭐ **This is the strongest single argument for the owner's shape, and it is vanilla's own code.**
`IsSuitableWorkplace` (`Lua/Buildings/Workplace.lua:1446-1462`) contains a dedicated habitat branch:

```lua
if IsKindOf(unit.residence, "MicroGHabitatBase") then
    if IsValid(bld.parent_dome) then
        return false
    end
    if HexAxialDistance(bld, unit.residence) > unit.residence:GetOutsideWorkplacesDist() then
        return false
    end
end
```

⇒ The game **already** asks "does this colonist live in a habitat", **already** answers it as
`IsKindOf(unit.residence, "MicroGHabitatBase")`, and **already** uses that answer to keep them out of
the colony's ordinary labour pool. ⛔ **So the repair is not a new rule invented by this pack — it is
the same rule the employment system enforces, applied to the one recruiter that ignores it.**
⭐ **And it hands the builder the predicate:** key on `unit.residence`, not `unit.dome`. That is the
field vanilla itself tests, and it is the durable one (`dome` is cleared the moment the colonist
steps outside; a resident's `residence` is not). Both habitat classes descend from
`MicroGHabitatBase`, so one test covers Naturalist and Micro-G alike.

⚠️ **CORRECTION to a claim made in conversation: habitat residents are NOT unemployable.** The branch
above rejects workplaces **inside a dome** and workplaces **beyond `GetOutsideWorkplacesDist()` of the
habitat** — it does not reject work as such. An outside workplace sited near the habitat is a legal
job for them. ⇒ Anywhere this entry or its conversation implied "permanently unemployed by design",
read "excluded from dome work and from distant work". The practical consequence is unchanged: with no
qualifying workplace nearby they are unemployed, which is what puts them at the top of the draft.

⚖️ **Stall risk CONSIDERED AND JUDGED NEGLIGIBLE, with its reason — ⛔ do not re-raise it as new.**
`CargoTransporter:Load` retries forever (`:126-130`, `while not succeed do Sleep(1000)`), so an
unfillable manifest waits indefinitely. Removing 5 candidates could in principle reach that.
⭐ **It mostly evaporates on inspection:** the draft only struggles when a *specialisation* is
demanded, and habitat residents are typically unspecialised (both of the owner's were "No
specialization"), so in exactly that case they were never eligible anyway. Where they do count is
when the requirement is "Any", which is when the colony has the most candidates. Owner's scale
argument: a habitat is 5 slots against domes of hundreds.
⭐ **And a stall is VISIBLE, not silent** — an unfillable crew sets `colonist_summon_fail`
(`RocketExpedition.lua:498`) and the rocket's own UI status reads **"Not enough Colonists"**
(`T(11473)`, `:1151`), beside the sibling rover and drone messages. The player is told, and the

#### ⭐⭐ The game ALREADY holds their bed, and then throws the reservation away (read 2026-09-16)

⛔ **This does NOT reopen the repair shape** — it is the mechanism, recorded because it makes the
defect sharper and because nobody would reconstruct it. 1.1.0 added a home-reservation system for
expeditions (`expedition_residence`, **zero hits** in the archived 1.0.7 tree):

1. `Colonist:EnterTransporter` (`Colonist.lua:5024-5044`) saves `self.expedition_residence =
   self.residence` **before** `SetDome(false)` clears it — for a habitat resident that is **the
   habitat**.
2. `Colonist:OnDisappear` (`:5003-5007`) then calls `expedition_residence:ReserveResidence(self)`,
   so they leave with **their own habitat bed held for them.** ⭐ `EF-103` records that a habitat
   implements `ReserveResidence`, so this works for a habitat exactly as for a dome.
3. `Colonist:GetExpeditionReturnDome` (`:5047-5060`) does `table.find(domes, residence.parent_dome
   or residence)`. A standalone habitat has no `parent_dome`, so it looks for **the habitat itself**
   in the reachable list — and `EF-103`'s gate ladder says a far habitat is not in it.
4. `Colonist:ReturnFromExpedition` (`:5075-5082`) then **cancels the held reservation** because the
   dome they were re-homed into is not the reserved one.

⇒ ⭐ **The machinery to bring them home exists, ran, and reserved the right bed. The reachability
list is what never contained the habitat** — so the bed is released and the colonist is re-homed at
random. That is why the owner's Dermot could simply walk back into a slot that was, moments earlier,
being held for him.

⚠️ **It also narrows item 1 below.** Of the two candidate gates, `CanVisit` capacity was very likely
never reached: `GetDomesReachableByColonists` tests **walking distance** before anything about the
rail sweep matters, and the rail sweep is `labels.Dome`-only so it cannot carry a community at all
(`EF-103`). A ≈2 h train ride fails the walking gate outright. ⛔ Still not a reason to delay — see
item 1.

#### The mechanism (SOURCE, game 1.1.0.403908)

Boarding: `Colonist:EnterTransporter` stashes the colonist's home before they
leave — `self.expedition_residence = self.residence` and, if it's a real
`Workplace`, `self.expedition_workplace` too (`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:5027-5037`).

Return: `RocketBase:Disembark` (`Lua/Buildings/RocketBase.lua:1969-1988`)
computes every community reachable **from the rocket's own current position**:

```lua
local domes, safety_dome, _, dome_elevators = GetDomesReachableByColonists(self.city, self:GetPos())
for _,unit in ipairs(crew) do
    unit:Appear(self)
    local dome, elevator = unit:GetExpeditionReturnDome(domes)
    if dome then
        elevator = dome_elevators and dome_elevators[dome]
    else
        dome, elevator = ChooseDome(unit, domes, safety_dome, dome_elevators)
    end
    ...
    unit:SetCommand("ReturnFromExpedition", self, dome)
```

`Colonist:GetExpeditionReturnDome` (`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:5047-5059`):

```lua
function Colonist:GetExpeditionReturnDome(domes)
    local residence = self.expedition_residence
    if IsValid(residence) then
        local dome = residence.parent_dome or residence
        if table.find(domes, dome) then
            return dome
        end
    end
    local workplace = self.expedition_workplace
    if IsValid(workplace) and IsValid(workplace.parent_dome) and table.find(domes, workplace.parent_dome) then
        return workplace.parent_dome
    end
end
```

A Naturalist/MicroG habitat has no `parent_dome` (`dome_forbidden = true`), so
`dome = residence` — the habitat itself — and the function needs it to appear
literally inside `domes`, the list `GetDomesReachableByColonists` built by
walking/train/elevator distance **from the rocket**. If the habitat sits off
that network (parked at a remote mine, as reported), it is absent from the
list, `GetExpeditionReturnDome` returns nil, and the code falls through to
`ChooseDome(unit, domes, safety_dome, dome_elevators)` — plain best-score
matching among whatever *is* reachable, with zero notion that this colonist
used to live somewhere specific. That is an instant, silent re-home to a
different residence (or none, if nothing scores positively), matching "become
instantly unassigned" exactly.

#### ⭐ AMENDED 2026-09-15 — the habitat can only ever enter `domes` ON FOOT; the train leg cannot carry it at all

**MEASURED** (1.1.0.403908, installed `ModTools\Src`), and it sharpens the entry
rather than refuting it. `GetDomesReachableByColonists` builds its list in two
sweeps with **different membership rules**:

- **Walking sweep** (`_GameUtils.lua:398-412`) iterates `city.labels.Community`.
  A habitat qualifies: `MicroGHabitatBase.__parents` carries `"Community"`
  (`MicroGHabitat.lua:4`) and `Community:GameInit` does
  `self.city:AddToLabel("Community", self)` (`Community.lua:47`). ⇒ **the sniff
  test in this entry is sound — an in-walking-distance habitat IS in `domes`,
  so 1.1.0's homing really does return it directly.**
- **Train sweep** (`:450-478`) harvests only `s.labels.Dome` from each reachable
  station. A habitat is a `Community`, **not** a `Dome`, so no station ever
  contributes one. ⇒ **a habitat sited beside its own passenger station, with
  the rocket landing elsewhere on the rail network, is skipped exactly as a
  trackless one is.** Train adjacency buys the player nothing here.

⇒ The reachable set for a standalone habitat is **walking distance from the
disembark point, full stop.** That is a narrower and more testable trigger than
"walk/train/elevator range" as written above, and it is a better fit for the
reporter's *"near one of my mines"* than the distance-only reading. This source
read preceded the 2026-09-15 reproduction above; the exact gate
remains unisolated and is not required for the chosen repair.

⛔ **A claim made to the owner in conversation and RETRACTED here before it could reach a builder:**

|---|---|---|

| `RocketExpeditionBase:…` (`Buildings/RocketExpedition.lua:496`) | calls the base explicitly at `:497` | ✅ **yes** |
| `LanderRocketBase:…` (`Buildings/LanderRocket.lua:1149`) | `self.cargo_passengers` — **a full reimplementation that never calls the base** | ❌ **no** |
| `CargoTransporterNew:…` (`CargoTransporterNew.lua:235`) | separate class; trade / supply / colony transfer | ❌ no, and out of scope |

⭐ **And the asteroid case needs no protection anyway: there is no auto-draft there.** The lander's
pool is `cargo_passengers`, which is written from the player's own cargo dialog
(`LanderRocketCargoRequest:SetPassengers` → `:ApplyPassengers`, `LanderRocketCargoRequest.lua:253-283`;
the pool the dialog offers is filtered by the `g_AsteroidLanderPassengerPoolFilter` GameVar).
⇒ **A habitat resident only goes to an asteroid because the player put them there.** Excluding them
would remove a player choice rather than close an automatic hole, which is a materially weaker case
and a different decision. ⛔ **The repair scopes to the EXPEDITION draft only.**

⚠️ **The asteroid return remains memoryless and that is NOT filed as a defect** — an asteroid trip
never sets `expedition_residence` at all (the save is gated on `transporter.expedition or
transporter.category == "expedition"`, `Colonist.lua:5026`, and `LanderRocketBase` carries neither),
so every asteroid returnee is re-homed by score. ⚖️ **Owner's reading, adopted:** an asteroid stay is
open-ended and player-ended, and the saved home is a **hold** on a bed and a work shift (the engine's
own comments call it *"the expedition hold"*, `Residence.lua:94`, `:394`), so not holding housing
indefinitely is a coherent design choice rather than an oversight.

**Our exposure re-checked:** `grep -rliE "GatherAvailableColonists|MicroGHabitat|NaturalHabitat|labels\.Colonist" Code/`
hits four modules (`Fix_DomeOverviewHighlight`, `Fix_FactionDomeSizeGate`, `Fix_FreedHousingNotice`,
`Fix_ShelterReflex`) and **every hit is `labels.Colonist` in an unrelated context** — a dome stat
average, C89's dome-size gate, notices. None touches the gather, the habitat classes, or the return
path. Still 100% vanilla, still not ours.

#### Comparison to 1.0.7: new machinery, not a regression

`grep -rn "GetExpeditionReturnDome\|expedition_residence" 1.0.7.396349/Src/Lua`
returns **zero hits**. 1.0.7's `RocketBase:Disembark` (`RocketBase.lua:1980-1991`
there) went straight to `ChooseDome(unit.traits, domes, safety_dome, dome_elevators)`
for every returnee, unconditionally — no memory of the original residence
existed at all, reachable or not. So 1.1.0 **added** the homing attempt this
entry is about; it did not remove or break one that used to work.

**Sniff test, run against both trees:** land the rocket right beside the
habitat, fully reachable, no train needed.
- **1.0.7:** still meaningless — `ChooseDome` scores every reachable
  community and picks the best regardless of prior residence; if a bigger
  dome scores higher for that colonist, they'd board a train to it and walk
  right past their own habitat with an open bed. Locality/familiarity is not
  a term in `Community:GetScoreFor` (`Community.lua:442-459`) on either
  branch.
- **1.1.0:** `GetExpeditionReturnDome` finds the habitat in `domes` and
  returns it **directly**, bypassing `ChooseDome`'s scoring entirely — fixed,
  relative to 1.0.7, for the reachable case.

So the gap this entry files is narrower than "expedition return is broken" —
it is specifically **"the new 1.1.0 homing logic still funnels into the old
1.0.7 fallback whenever the saved residence isn't within the disembark
point's walk/train/elevator range,"** and that fallback lands hardest on
exactly the residence type (`dome_forbidden`, self-sufficient, meant to sit
away from the dome network) the feature was clearly written to cover.

#### Why this reads as a genuine home, not a temporary outpost

`NaturalHabitatBase` → `MicroGHabitatBase`, `__parents = {"LivingBase",
"LifeSupportConsumer", "WaypointsObj", "Community", "MicroGHabitatAutoResolve",
"FoodBuilding"}` (`Lua/Buildings/MicroGHabitat.lua:3-4`) — a complete,
self-contained community in one building (own life support, own food
service, its own `birth_policy` field), never a satellite of a parent dome.
`can_be_safety_dome = false` (`:14`) explicitly excludes it from generic
homeless overflow — it's an opt-in residence for colonists who specifically
belong there, not a bunkhouse. `allow_work_in_connected = false` /
`allow_service_in_connected = false` (`:11-12`) — residents aren't expected
to commute to a dome network for anything.

The game's own text agrees: Preservation Movement faction copy calls it *"not
just a structure — it's a statement of humility"* (`Data/FactionDef/PreservationMovement.lua:164`),
weighed against "populated surface domes" at `-300` to `-900`; the "A Piece
of Mars" achievement is *"Had 5 Naturalist habitats around a Martian
reservation"* (`Data/Achievement.lua:713`) — permanent-settlement language,
not a rotating waypoint. Nothing anywhere frames it as temporary or
shift-limited. The expedition-homing gap is landing on the one building type
explicitly designed to be a real house sited off the beaten path.

`workplace.parent_dome` fallback in `GetExpeditionReturnDome` rescues some
cases this entry doesn't cover (a habitat resident whose *workplace* is
inside a real dome) · reporter's mod list — not held.
