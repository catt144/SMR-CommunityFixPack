### C57 — disabled delicacies still enter meals while residual stock remains (P3) `[cand 2026-09-10: source-read; desk control only]`

**DIFF-CAUSED. SOURCE intent tells:** the toggle advertises use, and the menu/stat
readers honor it while the consuming sibling does not. This is a data/consumer
contradiction, not a claim about hit-testing or the visual toggle animation.

**SOURCE:** base 1.1.0 `Lua/Buildings/FoodServiceBuilding.lua:625-649`
`IsIngredientEnabled`/`SetIngredientEnabled` store the disabled bit.
`GetAvailableIngredientsMask :541-550` and `GetMealEffectStats :562-570`
exclude disabled ingredients. `ConsumeMealIngredientsMask :521-539` walks ALL
stored ingredients and checks only ingredient-bit existence and the colonist's
vegan gate; it never checks the disabled bit. `:339-371` deliberately posts
supply requests to drain disabled stock, so that stock can remain pending haulage.
Nothing synchronously clears it when switched off.

**SOURCE ROUTE — R2:** `Lua/XDef/sectionIngredientRow.generated.lua:10-11`
says "Toggle use of this delicacy"; its mouse/gamepad handlers (`:39`, `:46`,
`:51`) call `ToggleIngredient` (`FoodServiceBuilding.lua:657-669`), which calls
the setter. A subsequent food visit executes `Colonist:VisitService`
(`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:2431-2517`), its `:2493-2494` consumption/stat call.
The other base consumer is `Lua/Buildings/MicroGHabitat.lua:141-144`
(`DeductDirectFood` followed by ingredient consumption and stat application).
These are the two explicit base consumption callers; the setters are also
used by same-building-type broadcast, so broadcast retains the discrepancy.

**SOURCE old counterpart:** `FoodServiceBuilding.lua` and `Meal.lua` do not
exist in 1.0.7. Base Diner/Grocery used `ServiceWorkplace` (their old/new class
files start at `:1`); food service formerly consumed generic Food through the
`HasConsumption` path and `Colonist:VisitService :1956-1996`. There is no old
delicacy-toggle contract to execute. The harness does NOT pretend otherwise.

**MEASURED at the desk, 2026-09-10:** `tools/desk_seam_food.py` loads the new
shipped setter, availability reader, vegan gate and consumer. A synthetic
registered ingredient with 300 units, 100 per visit and disabled=true gives
advertised mask 0, consumed mask 1, remaining stock 200. Enabled control
advertises/consumes mask 1; empty-registry control consumes nothing; vegan-mask
control consumes nothing. `reports/vanillahunt/SEAM_DESK.txt` holds output.
Request scheduling, actual DLC preset loading, and the game UI are not executed.

**SOURCE non-owner answer:** no trigger from normal base food alone.
`Lua/Resources.lua:13` initializes `MealIngredientIds`; `:50-52` fills it
only from resources in `group == "MealIngredients"`. `Meal.lua:14-22` derives
the consuming bit map from those IDs. The separate `FoodResources` registry
also includes ordinary Food (`Resources.lua:53-55`). Base `Data/Resource.lua`
defines ordinary Food at `:138`, not a delicacy. The chaser enumerated eight
ingredient definition locations under `DLC/norman/Presets/Resource.lua`; those
locations are not a DLC-wide behavior audit (scope drift recorded for 99).
**INFERRED severity:** DLC owners may lose disabled delicacy stock and receive
meal effects omitted by the menu until drones drain that stock. No vanilla
non-owner trigger was found; this is conditional on an ingredient being registered.

**Independent recipe [NEVER RUN]:** stock a supported delicacy in an ordinary
food service, pause, disable that delicacy, ensure residual stock remains, then
observe the next non-Vegan colonist's meal and ingredient stock while hauling is
not the cause of stock change. Vacuous if no ingredient is registered, the stock
already drained, the colonist's consume mask forbids it, or no meal completes.

**Falsifier:** with residual disabled stock and a completed eligible meal, the
game leaves that stock and its ingredient mask untouched, or a runtime override
changes the base consumer. TAKEABLE WHEN a fresh DLC food-service fixture and
attended stock observation are available (checklist seam rider). No module filed.
