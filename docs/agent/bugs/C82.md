of C75 (`reports/vanillahunt/HUNT_AUDIT.md` §3), not by a chain link; it has had ONE
source read, not the chain's two.** SOURCE tell: a reply whose outcome text promises "no
negative effects" has no outcome block, and no later member of the chain undoes the

`Data/StoryBit/TheIncident_3_Aftermath_NoExplosion.lua:4-13` and
