### C108 — The Wildfire cure never reaches colonists served from home (P2) `[built 2026-09-18: Code/Fix_WildfireCureVisit.lua; desk harness 20/20 with 7 falsifying variants; boots clean in game, cure not yet watched]`

#### Report

r/SurvivingMars, 2026-09-18, "Wildfire Crisis impossible to end with DLC": infected colonists never
visit a medical building, so the mystery cannot end; *"colonists are gaining health bonuses from
medical centres without ever visiting them."* Players work around it by shutting hospitals or
draining Sanity. The reporter blamed the DLC; the stall needs none (see Control).

#### Source (1.1.0.403908 unless stated)

- **The cure is a visit.** `MedicalBuilding:Service` removes `Infected` once `g_StartVaccinating`
  is set (`Lua/Buildings/MedicalCenter.lua:35-46`, the check at :37). The flag is set by
  `OnMsg.Mystery8_BeginHealing` (`Lua/Traits.lua:1227-1229`). The mystery says Infected colonists
  *"will be cured permanently after their first visit in a Medical Building"*
  (`Data/Scenario/Mystery 8.lua:204`) and ends only when `CountDomeLabel("TraitInfected") == 0`
  (:1045-1051). `Service` is reached only from a visit (`Colonist:VisitService`,
  `Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:2482`).
- **A colonist sends itself only when unwell.** `Colonist:Idle` visits `needMedical` on low Sanity,
  on Health < `LowStatLevel` (30), or on Health < `HighStatLevel` (70) past a 16-hour cooldown
  (`Colonist.lua:2206-2209`, `:2313-2333`; `Lua/__const.lua:1592-1595`).
- **Infected costs 12 Health a sol** (`Data/TraitPreset.lua:144-157`), and stops spreading once
  vaccination starts (`InfectedDailyUpdate`, `Lua/Traits.lua:1207-1225`).
- **1.1.0 pays medical care at home.** On every rest, `ApplyResidenceAdditiveStats`
  (`Lua/Stats.lua:623-648`, called at `Colonist.lua:2605`) adds each category in `dome.serviced`
  through `AccumulateCategoryServiceStats` (:568-582). `RecomputeDomeServiceStats` (:219-241) fills a
  category's Health as the sum of `service.Health × people / population × performance`.
  `ReassignServiceCategory` admits any working service with `service_impact > 0` (:498-505), which
  is the sum of its positive stats (`Lua/Buildings/Service.lua:73-82`). Every medical template sits
  in the `Infirmary` category and carries Health: Infirmary and MedicalPostCCP1 10000, MedicalCenter
  and HospitalCCP1 20000 (`Data/BuildingTemplate/*.lua`, `same_category_as = "Infirmary"`). Neither
  name exists anywhere in 1.0.7 (`grep -rn` over the archived tree: 0 lines).
- **1.1.0 dropped the rest recovery.** `DailyHealthRecover = 0` (`Colonist.lua:164`); in 1.0.7 it
  was a +5 default paid on every rest (1.0.7 `Colonist.lua:27`, :2026).
- **The visit route that 1.1.0 left in place.** A colonist's daily interest is picked by
  `PickInterest` (`Lua/Interests.lua:112-114`, called at `Colonist.lua:294` and `:722`) and visited
  by `Idle` (:2382-2386, commented *"Cosmetic service visit"*). Every medical template has
  `interest1 = "needMedical"`, and the Hypochondriac trait already adds that interest
  (`Data/TraitPreset.lua:116-126`), so the game already sends colonists to a medical building
  this way while they are healthy.

#### Intent

- **Tell 4 (self-contradiction):** the mystery's text names a visit as the cure and waits for zero
  infected, but in 1.1.0 medical coverage paid at home is what removes the need to visit. The more
  medical care a dome has, the less its infected are sent to it, and a Medical Center removes the
  visit entirely.
- **Tell 1:** player-reported harm, with the workaround (closing hospitals) that confirms the mechanism.
- **Counter-reading, recorded:** the at-home payment could be read as the 1.1.0 form of a visit,
  so the cure should follow it. It is not taken. It would cure with no visit, against the text,
  and it would still miss a colonist held above 70 by non-medical Health. The same category route
  pays an Open Air Gym and a Tai Chi Garden (+10 each), a Smart Home pays +5, and Feeding the Future
  delicacies +5 a meal (`DLC/norman/Presets/Resource.lua:240`). The delicacy balance question is out
  of scope and not touched.

#### Repair (built 2026-09-18, not run in game)

`Code/Fix_WildfireCureVisit.lua` (Register id `WildfireCureVisit`), listed in `items.lua` and
`metadata.lua` `code` before `90_SaveSanitizer`.

- **What it does:** once `g_StartVaccinating` is set, an Infected colonist's daily interest is
  `needMedical`. `Idle` then walks them to a medical building on the game's own cosmetic-visit
  route, and `MedicalBuilding:Service` cures them. The next sol they pick as usual. They give up
  one sol's cosmetic visit, once.
- **Technique:** FIX_POLICY §1.4b chained post-wrapper on the global `PickInterest`. The shipped
  body always runs first, so the colonist's random stream draws exactly what vanilla draws; only
  the answer is replaced, and only for an Infected colonist while vaccination is on. §3a layer 3:
  synchronous, nothing of ours in the save, and a save loads without the pack.
- **Guards:** `Require` on `PickInterest` and on `MedicalBuilding:Service` (declared there). A
  `test` that `AccumulateCategoryServiceStats` exists: absent on 1.0.7, where colonists still reach
  the threshold, so the module declines there without `update_suspect`. A §2a probe calls the
  shipped `AccumulateCategoryServiceStats` on stub tables and requires it to pay a category's Health.
  Manifest pins `MedicalBuilding:Service` (DEFECT: the cure line), `ApplyResidenceAdditiveStats`
  (DEFECT: the category payment) and `PickInterest` (class b only).
- **Not changed:** how colonists are housed or serviced. No other colonist, interest or service is
  touched.
- **Side effect, recorded:** an infected colonist with no reachable medical building fails that
  visit, so the dome records a medical service failure (`RecordServiceFail`, `Colonist.lua:2058`).
  An infected Hypochondriac in that position also takes the trait's own Sanity penalty for a missed
  medical interest (`Data/TraitPreset.lua:117-121`). Both require a colony with no medical care at all.

**The claim holds** if, in game, an Infected colonist in a dome whose Medical Center covers everyone
keeps Health at 88 or above after the cure is found and is still Infected after several sols, with
the pack disabled. **It is refuted** if that colonist visits the Medical Center and loses the trait
without the pack, or if their Health log shows no `Services` Health line on rest. With the pack,
the same colonist must visit and lose the trait within a sol.

The stall is vanilla and needs no DLC: a Medical Center is base game. Feeding the Future adds
another way to keep Health up; it is not the cause.

#### Reach

R1 on every platform: Wildfire is a base-game mystery and the Medical Center a base-game spire.
Any colony whose infected colonists live under a Medical Center or Hospital, or under an Infirmary
plus another +Health service, cannot finish the mystery. Once they are served from home it cannot
finish in ordinary play. The reported workarounds (closing the medical buildings, draining Sanity)
still end it. An owner check needs a Wildfire save at the cure stage, which is a provisioning job.
The console route (`g_StartVaccinating = true` plus an added `Infected` trait) proves the fix, not
the path.
