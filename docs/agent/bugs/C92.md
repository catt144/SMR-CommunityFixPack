#### Why the original cause was wrong

**SOURCE:** Completion is retained in `UIPlayer.tech_researched`
(`CommonLua/Libs/Research/Research.lua:147-164`). `GetTechState` gives researched
flags priority over lock state (`:46-54`). `Player:UIResearch` records completion
before emitting `TechResearched` (`Lua/TechTree.lua:1200-1221`), and permits
subsequent repeat research without clearing the flag. The UI explicitly displays
the repeat icon in its **researched** branch instead of a checkmark
(`Lua/XDef/XTechNode.generated.lua:176-185`). It is not evidence of non-completion.

**SOURCE + desk control:** [desk_c92_achievement.py](../../../tools/desk_c92_achievement.py)
executes those shipped bodies and the achievement listener with a recording-only
unlock sink. It confirms vanilla can request the achievement with completed
repeatables; an unresearched repeatable legitimately blocks it; exempting only
the orphan preserves that first-completion requirement and ordinary incomplete
techs still block. The original filing is retained in git `1b7d695`.

**SOURCE:** `IsTechRepeatable` delegates to `Research:IsTechRepeatable`, which
honors `g_ForceTechRepeatable` overrides (`Research.lua:150-159`). It can disagree
with `tech.Repeatable`; neither requires a C92 repeatable exemption.

#### Solution options - investigation handoff, no module built

**INFERRED recommendation:** Exempt only the verified orphan from this
achievement's counted population, preserving other tech requirements and the
vanilla tracked-group scope. The live comparison establishes the proposed
predicate's effect. The local vanilla filter is not directly assignable by a mod;
a focused additional achievement listener is a possible runtime seam. Do not
change shared `IsTechResearched` semantics or silently mark the tech researched.

**SOURCE recovery requirement:** The vanilla listener returns immediately on
repeat completions (`Achievements.lua:21-23`). This completed save has no remaining
ordinary first completion to trigger a corrected listener. The fix agent must
include an eligible repeat-event recheck and/or an ordered post-load recheck,
using normal engine achievement restrictions. Simply changing the predicate
while retaining the first-time-only trigger does not recover this reporter.

**INFERRED alternative:** Expose the tech through a normal tree connection.
That adds its authored production benefit and requires a gameplay placement
decision; it is broader than achievement bookkeeping. Exempting every hidden
tech is also broader and can waive valid future requirements. Options and
acceptance demands are in [the investigation](../reports/C92_INVESTIGATION.md#solution-options-for-the-fix-agent).

**SOURCE separate scope:** Terraforming is absent from the vanilla tracked list.
Adding it makes the requirement stricter and does not repair this hidden-tech
barrier. Keep that change separate. No player reply or owner scope call is
requested by this investigation.

#### 2026-09-13 addendum - why it is hidden, and what is still missing

**SOURCE verdict: the hidden/`Unknown` flags are an AUTHORING LEFTOVER, not a
deliberate bench.** Five controls, all in the shipped 1.1.0 data: (1) the devs'
retirement idiom is `Obsolete = true` and they used it three times in this same
change - on the `LawDef`, on the `PolicyDef`, and on `SelfSufficientLighting`, a
tech in this very `Underground_1` group; this preset carries none of it. (2) Of
441 Tech presets, 10 obsolete techs are hidden **zero** times and 218 hidden
techs are obsolete **zero** times - the idioms never overlap. (3) They rewired a
live consumer onto it (`BuildingComponents.lua:1358-1364`), a line-for-line port
of the 1.0.7 law check, tightened with an `Extractors` label test. (4) They
migrated saves off the old law with no replacement grant. (5) The four sibling
laws retired in the same batch got **no** replacement Tech at all - this one
alone was promoted.

**SOURCE second defect, larger than the achievement:** the +20% underground
extractor bonus shipped and worked in 1.0.7 via the law. On 1.1.0 the law is
obsolete and stripped from saves and the replacement is unreachable, so **no one
can obtain that bonus**, and players who carried the enacted law across the patch
lost it silently. Dev-report material stronger than the achievement alone.

**SOURCE the wiring can be finished with ONE call:** `UnlockTech(...)`
(`CommonLua/Libs/Research/Research.lua:31-38`) clears both `hidden` and `locked`
reasons, giving state `enabled`, which `IsVisibleOnMap` and `Player:CanResearch`
both accept. It cannot self-heal because `CheckUnlockPrerequisites` needs
`IsTechUnlocked` when `RequireTech` is empty, which being hidden prevents.

**MEASURED the content is ship-ready:** all four of its loc strings - ids new for
1.1.0, absent from the 1.0.7 tree - are translated in **all eight** shipped
languages, flavour prose included (`Local/*.fpk`, presence control passed).
