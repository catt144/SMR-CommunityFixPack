### C83 — the arrival fallback dome ignores power, life support and quarantine (P1) `[tested-attended 2026-09-10]`

**Field report:** a Steam discussion comment (2026-09-10): *"Rocket lands and colonists go
to nearest dome with no life support and is turned off and quarantined."* The reporter asked
whether a listed fix covers it. None does (§Our pack).

**OBSERVED, attended (owner, 2026-09-10, 1.1.0.403908, pack on — the rig's normal config,
cheats on).** A purpose-built test: two domes in walkable distance of the landing site (the
landing-site panel read "Domes in walkable distance: 2"); `Fuller #1` switched off
("This building has been turned off"), **Quarantined**, no power or life-support connections,
0/0 colonists, and the nearer of the two to the pad; the other dome powered and working. A
passenger rocket landed. **The arrivals split: some walked into the powered dome, the rest
into Fuller #1**, and the game raised **Suffocation!**. Evidence: the owner's screenshots in
conversation (not saved to the repo). ⚠️ Not observed: whether the colonists in Fuller #1
then died (not waited out), and the per-colonist housing count in the powered dome.

**SOURCE (1.1.0 `Lua/_GameUtils.lua`).** A community is *welcoming* only if it accepts
colonists, is switched on and has life support (`:382-384`). Real candidates must be
walkable AND welcoming (`:408-411`). The fallback `safety_dome` is the NEAREST community with a
foot route and `can_be_safety_dome` — **no welcoming test** (`:398`, `:403-407`).
`ChooseDome` starts from `safety_dome` and keeps it unless a candidate scores AND has free
living space for this colonist (`:486-501`; `Community:HasFreeLivingSpaceFor`,
`Lua/Buildings/Community.lua:402-418`). It is decided per colonist, so once the working
dome's free housing is used up, the rest of the rocket falls back — **the observed split**.
`Community:CanVisit` excludes only destroyed/demolishing communities (`Community.lua:420-422`);
`can_be_safety_dome` is `true` for every dome (`Community.lua:18`; only MicroG habitats opt
out, `MicroGHabitat.lua:14`).

**Intent tell:** the same function DOES prefer a welcoming safety dome in its elevator
branch — `if not safety_dome or (not is_welcoming_community(safety_dome) and
is_welcoming_community(elevator_safety_dome))` (`:437`). The authors' comment on the field:
"domes shelter any homeless colonist" (`Community.lua:18`) — a dome with no life support
shelters no one.

**Quarantine traps them.** Quarantine is the `accept_colonists` switch (dome panel text,
`Lua/XDef/sectionDome.generated.lua:205-209`: "Colonists are not allowed to enter or leave
quarantined Domes"), yet arrivals were admitted (observed). Once it is their dome,
`Colonist:FindEmigrationDome` returns nothing — `-- quarantine, no one enters or leaves`
(`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:3510-3512`) — so they do not look for a better dome by themselves
(source-read; the escape paths beyond this function are not enumerated).

**SOURCE old counterpart (1.0.7 `Lua/_GameUtils.lua`):** the same shape — `is_welcoming`
`:342-344`, fallback by distance alone `:359-362` (1.0.7 also lacks the foot-route test — F53's
subject), candidates `:363-366`, the elevator preference `:386`. PASSING, not diff-caused.

**SOURCE ROUTE — R2, base/non-owner:** a passenger rocket landing where the walkable working
domes lack free housing for all arrivals and a switched-off / quarantined / unsupplied dome is
the nearest. Base Lua only.

**Pre-fix attribution: not ours.** Before C83, `Fix_ArrivalDeaths` (F53 half b) re-chose only
when the assigned dome was NOT reachable on foot or by its paired elevator; Fuller #1 was
walkable, so that wrapper stood down and vanilla's destination held. The store card's
"Rocket loads of new arrivals died on their way to a dome" is F53 — the walk, not the
destination. C83 now adds the separate destination-safety half described below.

**Challenge re-derivation before build (2026-09-10, game 1.1.0.403908 + archived
1.0.7.396349): 5/5 PASS.**

1. **PASS — fallback omission.** Both trees' safety fallback is distance-only, with no
   welcoming test (1.1.0 `_GameUtils.lua:403-407`; 1.0.7 `:359-362`); both define welcoming
   as `accept_colonists and ui_working and HasLifeSupport()` (`:382-384` / `:342-344`).
2. **PASS — observed split.** `ChooseDome` is called once per applicant and keeps the safety
   fallback when no scored candidate has free suitable housing (`:486-500` / `:426-440`);
   rockets and landers reserve the chosen residence before the next applicant
   (`RocketBase.lua:2064-2073`; `CargoTransporterNew.lua:996-1006`).
3. **PASS — life support.** Both branches define it as water plus either breathable
   atmosphere or power and air (`Community.lua:462-464` / `:396-398`).
4. **PASS — seam covers true arrivals.** Rocket and `CargoTransporterNew` passenger
   generation set `applicant.arriving` before constructing the colonist
   (`RocketBase.lua:2064-2070`; `CargoTransporterNew.lua:996-1003`). Every such colonist
   reaches `Colonist:Idle`'s sole `self.arriving` branch (`Colonist.lua:2233-2235` /
   `:1791-1793`) before `Arrive` snapshots `emigration_dome` into a local (`:1592` /
   `:1260`). `ReturnFromExpedition_TransportDestination` also sets `arriving`, but uses the
   return command's own destination; this module's destination half only acts when
   `emigration_dome` is present.
5. **PASS — quarantine is the bypassed gate.** `accept_colonists` is the quarantine switch;
   the owner observed the arrivals enter it, and only later emigration enforces “no one
   enters or leaves” (`Colonist.lua:3510-3512`).

**Homeless follow-through challenge.** There is no second safety fallback in the later
resettlement path: `FindEmigrationDome` builds a reachable graph and
`GetBestReachableCommunities` scores explicit candidates. It *can* select a switched-on,
accepting dome without life support because `CanAcceptNewColonists` omits life support
(`Community.lua:96-98`) and a must-have filter may override the no-life-support tier.
That is explicitly intentional in the shipped 1.1.0 constant comment (“so the player can
still force colonists into an unpowered dome”, `Community.lua:436-440`), not C83's
unguarded fallback. Sibling [C84](C84.md) records and closes that distinct route; C83 was
not widened.

**Built 2026-09-10 — `Fix_ArrivalDeaths` half (c), same `ArrivalDeaths` veto.** The existing
arrival-only `Colonist:Idle` layer-2 pre-wrapper now re-chooses when the assigned destination
is reachable but fails vanilla's welcoming rule. It passes the nearest welcoming candidate
(the minimum returned `dome_dist`, not `domes[1]`, because the station sweep appends after
sorting) as `ChooseDome`'s safety fallback. A full working dome therefore receives overflow
homeless; if no welcoming candidate exists, or F117's argument probe is UNKNOWN, vanilla's
assignment is preserved. The paired `dome_elevators` value follows the chosen dome. The
module's only one-shot diagnostic names colonist, old dome and new dome on the first C83
reroute in a session.

**D03 composition.** Residency Control filters `ChooseDome` candidates but deliberately
does not filter its safety fallback. C83 therefore applies the public
`Community:CanAcceptNewColonists` gate while choosing its non-tourist fallback; a D03-closed
dome cannot leak back through safety. Tourists retain D03's documented exemption. The fix
reads no opt-in-owned field or global.

**Desk control:** `python tools/desk_c83_arrivals.py` — **12/12 held** on the shipped
`_GameUtils.lua:382-501` span plus the whole module: dead→working with space; dead→full

(layer 2). It writes only vanilla's `emigration_dome` / `emigration_elevator` fields with
vanilla objects. Uninstall restores the shipped fallback; no colonist or function is stranded.
