### C83 — the arrival fallback dome ignores power, life support and quarantine (P1) `[filed 2026-09-10: OBSERVED attended + source-read]`

**Field report:** a Steam discussion comment (2026-09-10): *"Rocket lands and colonists go
to nearest dome with no life support and is turned off and quarantined."* The reporter asked
whether a listed fix covers it. None does (§Our pack).

**OBSERVED, attended (owner, 2026-09-10, 1.1.0.403908, pack on — the rig's normal config,
cheats on).** A purpose-built test: two domes in walkable distance of the landing site (the
landing-site panel read "Domes in walkable distance: 2"); `Fuller #1` switched off
("This building has been turned off"), **Quarantined**, no power or life-support connections,
0/0 colonists, and the nearer of the two to the pad; the other dome powered and working. A
passenger rocket landed. **The arrivals split: some walked into the powered dome, the rest
into Fuller #1**, and the game raised **Suffocation!**. Evidence: the owner's screenshots in
conversation (not saved to the repo). ⚠️ Not observed: whether the colonists in Fuller #1
then died (not waited out), and the per-colonist housing count in the powered dome.

**SOURCE (1.1.0 `Lua/_GameUtils.lua`).** A community is *welcoming* only if it accepts
colonists, is switched on and has life support (`:382-384`). Real candidates must be
walkable AND welcoming (`:408-411`). The fallback `safety_dome` is the NEAREST community with a
foot route and `can_be_safety_dome` — **no welcoming test** (`:398`, `:403-407`).
`ChooseDome` starts from `safety_dome` and keeps it unless a candidate scores AND has free
living space for this colonist (`:486-501`; `Community:HasFreeLivingSpaceFor`,
`Lua/Buildings/Community.lua:402-418`). It is decided per colonist, so once the working
dome's free housing is used up, the rest of the rocket falls back — **the observed split**.
`Community:CanVisit` excludes only destroyed/demolishing communities (`Community.lua:420-422`);
`can_be_safety_dome` is `true` for every dome (`Community.lua:18`; only MicroG habitats opt
out, `MicroGHabitat.lua:14`).

**Intent tell:** the same function DOES prefer a welcoming safety dome in its elevator
branch — `if not safety_dome or (not is_welcoming_community(safety_dome) and
is_welcoming_community(elevator_safety_dome))` (`:437`). The authors' comment on the field:
"domes shelter any homeless colonist" (`Community.lua:18`) — a dome with no life support
shelters no one.

**Quarantine traps them.** Quarantine is the `accept_colonists` switch (dome panel text,
`Lua/XDef/sectionDome.generated.lua:205-209`: "Colonists are not allowed to enter or leave
quarantined Domes"), yet arrivals were admitted (observed). Once it is their dome,
`Colonist:FindEmigrationDome` returns nothing — `-- quarantine, no one enters or leaves`
(`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:3510-3512`) — so they do not look for a better dome by themselves
(source-read; the escape paths beyond this function are not enumerated).

**SOURCE old counterpart (1.0.7 `Lua/_GameUtils.lua`):** the same shape — `is_welcoming`
`:342-344`, fallback by distance alone `:359-362` (1.0.7 also lacks the foot-route test — F53's
subject), candidates `:363-366`, the elevator preference `:386`. PASSING, not diff-caused.

**SOURCE ROUTE — R2, base/non-owner:** a passenger rocket landing where the walkable working
domes lack free housing for all arrivals and a switched-off / quarantined / unsupplied dome is
the nearest. Base Lua only.

**Our pack — attribution: not ours, and not fixed by us.** `Fix_ArrivalDeaths` (F53 half b)
re-chooses only when the assigned dome is NOT reachable on foot or by its paired elevator
(`Code/Fix_ArrivalDeaths.lua:341-345`); Fuller #1 was walkable, so the wrapper stood down and
vanilla's destination held. The store card's "Rocket loads of new arrivals died on their way to
a dome" is F53 — the walk, not the destination.

**Fix shape (NOT built — checklist 143 RULED FIX 2026-09-10; the build prompt is
`prompts/C83_FIX.md`, which chose the seam: extend `Fix_ArrivalDeaths`' arrival-only
`Colonist:Idle` pre-wrapper, not the shared rule).** Make the fallback prefer the
NEAREST WELCOMING foot-reachable community (arrivals land homeless in a working dome —
survivable, and the homeless-resettle path takes over), falling back to any dome only when
none is welcoming — the rule `:437` already applies to elevator safety domes. ⛔ Before
choosing the seam: `GetDomesReachableByColonists` and `ChooseDome` have many callers (F117
lists nine `ChooseDome` sites in 1.1.0; F53 declined a global `ChooseDome` wrap for exactly
this reason) — enumerate them, decide whether the change is arrival-only (keyed like F53 on
`self.arriving`) or global, and state who else it moves (homeless resettling, the "Abandoned"
path, android spawns). FIX_POLICY §4a: a player LOSS, not a rebalance.

colony and layout).** That makes the fix's A/B a reload, not a rebuild: land from it unfixed
(overflow enters Fuller #1) and fixed (nobody enters it). Record the save's name here at first
