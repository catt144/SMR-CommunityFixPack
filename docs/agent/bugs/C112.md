### C112 — a migration re-reserve drops the player's assigned residence (P3) `[cand 2026-10-02: recheck possible-unconfirmed]`

#### Report

No field report.

#### Source (archived 1.1.1.405907)

- Assigning a colonist to a residence in another dome reserves that residence and sets a forced
  dome (`Lua/Buildings/Residence.lua:351-352`, seat; `Colonist:SetForcedDome :4357-4361`). While
  the lock holds (`ForcedByUserLockTimeout`), Idle calls `TryToEmigrate` every pass (`:2550-2551`).
- The journey start reserves again: `StartMigration` (`:2099-2101`, seat) and the walk, retarget
  and booking branches of `TryToEmigrateToDome` (`:1927-1934`, `:1954-1961`, `:1972-1980`) all call
  `dest_dome:ReserveResidence(self)`.
- `Dome:ReserveResidence` (`Lua/Buildings/Dome.lua:3482-3493`, cleared) returns early only when
  the colonist already holds a slot **in this dome**; otherwise it reserves the first residence in
  `self.labels.Residence` that `CanReserveResidence(unit)`. The assigned residence is in this dome,
  so the early return would protect it, except that the journey cancels the hold first:
  `StartMigration` calls `ClearTransportRequest` (`:2097`, cleared) and the walk branch calls it at
  `:1925`, and `ClearTransportRequest` begins with `CancelResidenceReservation()` (`:2238-2239`,
  cleared). By the time `Dome:ReserveResidence` runs (`:2099-2101`) the colonist holds nothing, and
  the first free residence is taken (which may still be the selected one). Direct shuttle booking
  at :1972-1973 does not first clear the reservation and preserves an existing same-dome hold.
- On arrival, `UpdateResidence` runs `CheckForcedResidence` (`:3143-3156`, cleared): while the lock
  holds and the residence is in the colonist's dome, the assigned residence is applied; if it is
  full by then, `UpdateResidence` (:3123-3130) chooses elsewhere. The valid forced choice remains
  until expiry/invalidity; fullness alone does not clear it.

Intent tell (FIX_POLICY §4, tell 3): the assignment path reserves the specific residence; the
journey path in the same file cancels that and reserves generically.

#### Harm

Only players who assign homes by hand, and only when the assigned home fills during the journey.
Then the colonist arrives and may live elsewhere while the forced choice remains live. In the
early game, with few residences and a player placing founders by hand, that is a visible
"I told them to live there" failure; otherwise unnoticed.

#### Reach

R2: the assign-residence action across domes; the loss needs a second colonist to take the slot
during the walk.

#### Control — not yet run

Two domes joined by a passage, both with two-slot residences. Assign colonist X in dome A to a
specific residence in dome B, then have a second colonist claim that residence before X arrives.

- **Supports:** X's hold moves to a different generic home, a competitor takes the selected bed,
  and X arrives elsewhere while its forced choice can remain live.
- **Refutes:** X's reservation in the assigned residence survives `StartMigration`
  (print `reserved_residence` right after the journey starts).

#### Fix shape (owner decides)

A synchronous §1.4 chain on `Dome:ReserveResidence` could reserve a valid, unexpired selected
home before calling orig (whose same-dome early return then preserves it), or move the reservation
after orig. Preserve all original returns; guard foreign units and unavailable homes. The post
variant adds transient generic reserve/cancel effects. An early-return pre-wrapper is a guarded
bypass, not an always-call-original chain. No new persisted field is needed.

#### Not opened

Cross-check read the branches on archived 1.1.1.405907: foot and multi-leg starts cancel;
`TryToEmigrateByTrain` also clears transport at `ColonistTransport.lua:805` before reserving
(:807-808). Same-target shuttle retarget and direct booking preserve the held same-dome slot.
Actual competitor timing and final home in ordinary play remain unobserved.
