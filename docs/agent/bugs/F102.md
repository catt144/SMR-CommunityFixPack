### F102 — Visiting an asteroid with subsurface Exotic Minerals hard-freezes some systems (P2, ext-witnessed)  `[fixed 2026-08-12 — Code/Fix_ExoticDepositSign.lua retargets SubsurfaceDepositPreciousMinerals.entity to the orphaned remaster sign SignRareMineralsDeposit + LoadGame UpdateEntity sweep. CURE UNVERIFIED on affected hardware; ships disclaimered by owner decision 2026-08-12. Safety verified: gameplay is class-keyed, both entities ship in vanilla, save carries nothing of ours]`

## The defect, and how much of it is proven

**Witnessed (external):** VasjaTheOx, Steam General Discussions, May 31 2026 —
Linux Mint, Proton, and (inferred from his own DLSS setting) NVIDIA proprietary
drivers: the game hard-freezes when visiting "Rare (Blue) Metal" asteroids;
deterministic on his machine for the second such asteroid; also reproduced by
~10 planet↔asteroid switches. DrFromage (Aug 2026, 13h before we read the
thread) still steers players to the community workaround, so the population is
live. **The freeze is a hang, not a crash or Lua error** — kill-the-process
territory, no log signature expected or reported.

**Proven by the community mod (extracted and read this session, PDX 154761
"Hotfix 1.0.7 asteroid freeze" by Amiphacelie):** its entire mechanism is a
`DepositMarker:PlaceDeposit` pre-hook rewriting `resource = "PreciousMinerals"`
→ `"PreciousMetals"` for `SubsurfaceDepositMarker`s. That this cures the freeze
on affected machines (the author iterated against a live repro — the header
records failed attempts, `Subs1Count`/cluster-range edits) pins the trigger to
**the subsurface PreciousMinerals deposit object existing on an asteroid map**.
Surface clusters are explicitly fine (the mod keeps them).

**Our forensics — what is unique about that object:**

- `SubsurfaceDepositPreciousMinerals` (`Lua\Buildings\SubsurfaceDeposit.lua:496-507`)
  has no unique logic — only display strings, an encyclopedia id, and its
  entities. Nothing in all of Src special-cases the class beyond
  `Hints.lua:341`, an extractor's `subsurface_deposit_class`, and the
  deep-flag map. Nothing can loop.
- Its sign material (`Materials.fpk:SignPreciousMineralsDeposit_mesh.sub_0.mtljson`)
  is the **only sign material in the game carrying `VertexNoise: "noiseTree"`**
  — 7 of 2,455 materials have VertexNoise at all; the other six are crops
  (sway) and a rover part (`noiseGrass`). A tree-sway vertex shader on a
  static billboard, on exactly the deposit that freezes.
- The material is a visible hand-edit: BaseColor `ResourceRareMinerals_BC.dds`
  (used by no other material), SI `ResourcePM02_EM - Copy.dds` (scratch name,
  though shared by all signs — exonerated as differentiator).
- **The remaster's own clean sign for this resource ships orphaned:**
  `SignRareMineralsDeposit` — mesh, clean numbered-texture material (same
  field shape as every working sign), and a full `entities.dat` record — is
  referenced **nowhere in Lua, not even EntityData**. The wiring the remaster
  apparently intended never landed; the hand-hacked material on the old B&B
  entity is what shipped instead.

**What is NOT proven (and stays labelled a hypothesis):** that the anomalous
material's shader permutation is the freeze. The mechanism is below Lua —
plausibly a shader-permutation miss absent from `ShaderCached3d12.fpk`
(content-hashed, uninspectable) hanging the NVIDIA-under-Proton driver stack.
The fix does not depend on the hypothesis being exact — it replaces the whole
entity+material — but a failure report from an affected player would mean the
defect is below anything entity-shaped and out of our reach.

## Negative repros — two, both recorded as expected under the hypothesis

1. **Windows rig (RTX 4080, D3D12), 2026-08-12.** Console-spawned D-type
   (`UIColony:SpawnAsteroid(Presets.DiscoveryAsteroidPreset.Default["Asteroid_D"], true)`
   — the cap in `Discoveries.lua:160-163` gates only natural discovery, not
   this call), map `BlankAsteroidSlim_02` generated in 527 ms, scan 3 exotic
   deposits, all three signs on screen for ~5 min, zoom/overview/switch legs
   run. Zero `[LUA ERROR]`; log otherwise all known noise (C44 pair, Braze,
   version notice) plus one unrelated legitimate F89 heal.
2. **Steam Deck (RADV, Proton), 2026-08-12.** Same save prepped but asteroid
   NOT yet visited — map generated fresh on-device (`GenerateMap` runs on
   first travel, so this was a full end-to-end trigger on Proton hardware).
   Landed, switched back and forth repeatedly: clean. Caveat: Deck is AMD and
   benefits from Valve's pre-distributed shader caches — a weaker negative
   than it looks, and the witness class (NVIDIA proprietary) is untested and
   untestable on hardware we own (a VM replaces exactly the layer under
   suspicion; ruled out).

## Intent and reachability (§4)

- **Intent tells:** (1) player-reported harm — the Steam thread and the
  existence + iteration of a dedicated community hotfix mod; (3) sibling
  contradiction — every sibling sign material lacks VertexNoise and uses the
  clean texture pipeline, and the sibling asset pair (`SignRareMineralsDeposit`)
  was built and never wired. A hand-hacked one-off material on a shipped
  entity is not a design.
- **Reachability of the trigger: R1.** Subsurface Exotic Minerals deposits
  spawn on B (1), D (3–5), P (2), S (1) and mystery asteroids by preset
  (`Data\ResourcePreset.lua` `Subs1Count`; map presets `Data\RandomMapPreset.lua`);
  only C and M types have `ResEnabled_PreciousMinerals false`. Ordinary play
  reaches the trigger constantly. **The harm is configuration-gated:**
  witnessed only on Linux/NVIDIA; two negatives on our hardware.

## The fix, and its safety case

`Code/Fix_ExoticDepositSign.lua` — FIX_POLICY §1.1 data patch:
`SubsurfaceDepositPreciousMinerals.entity = "SignRareMineralsDeposit"` at mod
load (pre-flattening on both boot paths, so ClassesBuilt bakes it), guarded by:
class present · classdef still declares the shipped entity (a future patch
rewiring it deactivates us) · `IsValidEntity("SignRareMineralsDeposit")`.
Plus an `OnMsg.LoadGame` sweep (`WhenActive`-gated) mirroring vanilla's own
entity-convergence pattern (`SubsurfaceDeposit.lua:471-483`,
`AllMapsForEach` + `UpdateEntity`) for deposits already placed in older saves.

- **Gameplay identity is class-keyed, not entity-keyed** — extractors, reveal,
  exploitation, UI all resolve via class/resource; no Src site reads the
  deposit's entity string. Only the billboard art changes.
- **Save carries nothing of ours** (layer 3): a class default and one
  synchronous OnMsg handler. Whichever entity name a save stores, **both
  entities ship in vanilla packs** (entities.dat records read this session),
  so saves made with the fix load clean after uninstall.
- **If it fails to cure:** affected players are exactly where vanilla left
  them — the community swap mod remains compatible (it removes the deposits
  our fix re-signs; load order irrelevant, their hook fires at PlaceDeposit,
  ours at entity level).
- `disabled_entity` (`SignUnexploitablePreciousMinerals`) left vanilla: clean
  material, no evidence against it. **Residual risk recorded:** if a freeze
  report ever comes from a deep-layer deposit shown unexploitable, that
  entity is the next suspect.

## Platform gating — considered and DECLINED (owner, 2026-08-12: "ok we will keep it as is")

A gate ("apply only on Proton + NVIDIA") is buildable — `os.getenv` survives
the mod sandbox (engine uses it, `lib.lua:897`; `os` absent from
`ModEnvBlacklist`) and Proton leaks `STEAM_COMPAT_DATA_PATH`/`WINELOADER`;
`GetGpuDescription()`/`GetGpuDriverVersion()` are exposed C exports
(`uiXBugReportDlg.lua:529`). `Platform.steamdeck` detects the wrong machine
(the Deck is the clean AMD negative; the witness is desktop Linux, flag
false). Declined because the failure directions are asymmetric: a gate
false-negative (env-var heuristics, undocumented, most variable in exactly
the witness population — Steam/Heroic/Lutris/Wine) turns the fix OFF for its
audience, while the ungated fix has no harmful direction anywhere (safety
case above). A gate would also fork every report four ways and make the
shipped path untested on hardware we own. Do not re-propose without new
evidence of harm on unaffected systems.

## ⛔ What a future session must not conclude

- **Not that the cure is verified.** Two clean local runs verify SAFETY only;
  the freeze never reproduced here, so nothing local can verify the cure.
- **Not that the VertexNoise attribution is established.** It is the only
  anomaly found, not a measured mechanism. Do not cite F102 as "the shader
  bug" — cite it as "the freeze the sign-entity retarget targets".
- **Not that the console-spawn negative repro was a wrong trigger.** The
  trigger condition (subsurface exotic deposits present + signs rendered) was
  verifiably on screen; the spawn path shares `PlaceDeposit`/`SpawnDeposit`
  with natural discovery.
- **Do not remove the fix on a quiet record** — silence from affected players
  is the expected steady state either way. Only a witness-class report moves
  this entry.
