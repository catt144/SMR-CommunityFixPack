### C70 — destroyed experiment tank can be replaced by an old tank `[cand 2026-09-10: source-read; runtime unobserved]`

**PASSING. SOURCE intent tell:** the mystery explicitly says that even a player
who already owns a Large Water Tank “has to build a new one”
(`Lua/Scenario/Mystery 11.generated.lua:271`). It initially waits for a new
`ConstructionComplete` tank (`:285-295`), but if that designated tank is
destroyed, it switches to the entire `MainCity.labels.WaterTankLarge` list and
chooses its last member without requiring new construction (`:297-310`). The
old body carries the same broad rebound (`old :296-317`); the 1.1.0 diff did not
cause it.

**SOURCE ROUTE — R1, base/non-owner:** St. Elmo's Fire mystery progression
reaches the experiment directly. Large Water Tanks and the city label are base
content. No DLC/norman interior is involved.

**INFERRED impact:** destroying the newly built experiment tank while an older
large tank remains can satisfy the script's replacement selection and continue
filling/progress on that old tank, contradicting the stated construction goal.
Label ordering and runtime objective behavior remain unobserved.

**Independent recipe / falsifier:** in a fresh 1.1.0 St. Elmo colony, build one
large tank before the objective. When prompted, build the designated new tank,
then destroy it before 80% fill while the old tank survives. Observe which tank
is renamed/targeted and whether another ConstructionComplete event is required.
Vacuous if the designated tank reaches completion first, no older tank survives,
or destruction is unavailable. If the script remains on “Construct new Large
Water Tank” until another new tank completes, the candidate is false. No fix is
proposed.
