### C48 — vegetation seed offers get no distance brake, so a full depot loses to a trickle  `[filed 2026-08-15 from the C47 attended sitting — mechanism SOURCE-VERIFIED, owner-observed in play, ⛔ the drone PREFERENCE is NOT yet measured, ⛔ nothing built, ⛔ not a release gate]`

## Where this came from, and whose it is

⭐ **The owner found this by watching drones during the `C47` attended sitting**
(2026-08-15). The agent was counting notification events; the owner was watching
the screen, and asked why drones were fetching single seed crates out of the
landscape while a **4,000/4,000 Seeds depot stood next to the farm**. Their own
words, and they turned out to name the mechanism exactly:

> *"they are picking seeds to deliver to it from the environment even though
> there is a massive full seed storage right there… I am guessing there is a
> hidden rate at which vegetation offers seeds."*

There is. It is called `seed_cooldowns`.

## The mechanism, read at `ModTools\Src` on the pinned build

* `Lua/Vegetation.lua:4` — `is_in_seed_cooldown = 128`, a per-hex flag on the
  vegetation grid.
* `Lua/Vegetation.lua:32` — `GameVar("seed_cooldowns", {})`, the per-hex timer
  table, keyed `x + y*hex_width`.
* When a grown plant is off cooldown the game places a **`VegetationTaskRequester`**
  on that hex — an invisible `TaskRequester` whose job is to offer that plant's
  seeds to the drone network (`:1081`, `:1774-1795`). Lichen is excluded
  (`:867`); every other vegetation group participates.
* The farm has the matching reader: `VegetationPlant:GetSeedsInRange()` →
  `GetResourceInRange("Seeds")` (`TerraformingBuilding.lua:428-429`), and
  `GetGrownPlantsWithNoRequestorInRange` (`:405-421`) counts grown, non-withering,
  off-cooldown plants that do not yet carry a requester.

### ⭐⭐ The asymmetry, which is the whole filing

```lua
DefineClass.VegetationTaskRequester = {
    __parents = { "TaskRequester" },
    priority = 1, --seeds have lowest prio
}                                            -- Lua/Vegetation.lua:1774-1783
```

It inherits `TaskRequester.supply_dist_modifier = 100` — *"modifier (percentage)
of the distance when considering this supply (0-255)"*, `Lua/_TaskRequest.lua:96`.
**100 is the default: no penalty at all.**

And there is **exactly one** override of that field in the entire shipped tree:

```lua
supply_dist_modifier = 150, -- surface deposits are considered 50% further than storages
                                      -- Lua/Buildings/SurfaceDeposit.lua:84
```

⇒ **The developers identified this exact problem for surface deposits and applied
a 50% brake. Scattered vegetation seed offers got none.** A single plant's yield
is weighted identically to a 4,000-unit storage depot, so on a heavily terraformed
map hundreds of unbraked trickle sources compete with the depots.

## What the owner observed, and what it would explain

1. **Drones arrive at the farm carrying one crate** when the rig's carry dial is
   at +2 (3,000 per trip). Explained without any carry-capacity bug: *the pile it
   chose only contains one plant's worth*.
2. **They pass a full depot to do it.** Explained by the missing brake — with one
   caveat below.
3. ⭐ **The two farms' difference.** The owner: *"the farm that is complaining is
   in a much more dense area of the map… and the drone extenders in that area give
   the drones a much larger area to look for vegetation seeds."* This is a better
   explanation of the `C47` A/B split than the crop-cost one the agent proposed,
   and the `C47` numbers agree: the two farms **spent almost the same** (9,425 vs
   9,520) and differed in what they **received** (7,320 vs 8,361).
4. **Why it is a terraforming-speed-run symptom.** The offers only exist where
   vegetation is grown. A young colony has few; the owner's map is carpeted.

## ⛔ What is NOT established, stated plainly

* ⛔ **That drones actually PREFER these over a depot.** The mechanism permits it;
  the owner watched it; nobody has measured it. ⚠️ And the owner's own evidence
  contains an anomaly the distance rule does **not** explain: *"these drones are
  going after ground seeds that are further away than the big storage and far
  further away than the smaller depot."* If they are travelling FURTHER, distance
  weighting alone is not the whole story — `priority = 1, --seeds have lowest prio`
  may be involved, and the interaction between request priority and supply
  selection has not been read.
* ⛔ **The offer size and the cooldown rate.** Neither has been read out of the
  source or measured live.
* ⛔ **That this is a defect at all.** "Colonists should sweep the landscape for
  free seeds" is a defensible design, and the trickle is genuinely free resource.
  The complaint is only that it appears to out-compete a bulk source that would
  serve a consumer far better.
* ⛔ **Nothing is built. Not a release gate.**

## The measurement that settles it — designed, not yet run

Read-only, one unattended launch on a copy of `C47FARM`:

1. **Enumerate every live `VegetationTaskRequester`** on the map: count, position,
   the resource and amount each offers, and how many sit inside each farm's
   drone-coverage radius. ⭐ **That count, per farm, is the headline** — if the
   complaining farm's radius holds many and the silent farm's holds few, the
   owner's explanation is measured rather than argued.
2. **Track both Seeds depots' stored amounts** on the same clock. If they sit flat
   while the farm's buffer sawtooths, the farm is being fed from the landscape.
3. **Sample the farm buffer at 200 ms**, not 2 s, so each delivery lands in its
   own sample and the delivery-size distribution is real rather than smeared.
   ⚠️ The `C47` sitting's 2-second polling is why "how much does one delivery
   bring" is still unanswered.
4. Re-read `supply_dist_modifier` and `priority` **live** off the requester
   objects rather than trusting the class declaration.

⚖️ **If confirmed this is bigger than `C47`**: it would affect every consumer
building on a vegetated map, not farms alone, and the repair shape would be a
one-field data change of the kind the game already applies to surface deposits —
rather than `C47`'s balance-changing buffer resize.

### P1 — ⭐⭐ THE HEADLINE. The complaining farm's drones can reach many more seed offers than the silent farm's

The owner's explanation, in their words: *"the farm that is complaining is in a
much more dense area of the map… and the drone extenders in that area give the
drones a much larger area to look for vegetation seeds."* Measured as the
union — deduplicated by handle — of the `VegetationTaskRequester`s inside every
command centre serving the farm and every working extender hanging off those
centres, which is the game's own search (`FindTaskRequesters`,
`DroneControl.lua:315-324`).

⇒ **predicted: `OpenFarm#15779` (A, complains) reaches at least 1.5× as many
offers as `OpenFarm#15974` (B, silent)**, and the same direction holds on the
topology-independent local-density proxy.
*Falsifiers, all three written down now:*
* ⛔ **within ±20% of each other ⇒ the owner's density explanation is REFUTED**
  and the `C47` A/B difference is unexplained again. Say so; do not patch it.
* ⛔ **B > A ⇒ an outright reversal**, stronger than a refutation, and it must be
  reported as one.
* ⛔ **zero offers on the map, or `command_centers` resolving empty ⇒
  `UNSAMPLED`**, never "no offers exist". A zero is only a reading if the
  condition was sampled.
⚠️ **Band, stated honestly:** nobody has ever read this count, so the map-wide
figure is predicted only as *hundreds* (**200–3,000**). A number outside that
band is not a failure of the case, only of this guess.

### P2 — the depots stay flat while the farms eat ~46,000 seeds

If the farm is fed from storage, the Seeds depots must fall roughly in step with
what the farms receive.
⇒ **predicted: each Seeds depot's stored amount moves by less than one drone
trip (3,000) between the census and the close**, while the two farms consume
≈46,000 between them.
*Falsifier:* ⛔ **a depot draining by anything close to the farms' cumulative
receipt (say ≥ half of it) means the farm IS being fed from storage and this
entry's premise is wrong.**
⚠️ Boundary that does not go away whichever way this reads: flat depots are
**circumstantial**. They cannot show that a drone *chose* a plant over a depot.

### P3 — the delivery-size distribution is a wall of small values, not trips of 3,000

A drone on this rig carries **3,000**. A vegetation offer holds **200 / 1000 /
2000** plus a soil bonus, ceiling **280 / 1300 / 2600**. So a depot-fed farm
should show deliveries at or near 3,000 and a landscape-fed one should not.
⇒ **predicted: ≥ 60% of sampled deliveries land below 3,000, with the mode in
the 200–2,600 band**, and few or no exact-3,000 arrivals.
*Falsifier:* ⛔ **a wall at 3,000 refutes the routing story** — the farm is being
bulk-fed and `C48` has no symptom to explain.
⚠️ **Two ways a delivery can read small for reasons that are not `C48`,** named
before the numbers exist: a nearly full buffer caps what the drone can unload
(5,000 − stored), and **at 20x one 200 ms sample spans 4,000 game ms**, so
several arrivals merge. ⇒ **read the distribution off the 1x and 3x rungs**;
the 20x rung's sizes are smeared by construction.

### ⛔ Two defects in our own instruments, found at source BEFORE this launch and disclosed rather than quietly fixed

1. ⛔⛔ **The payload's headline reading could not have worked.** `98_C48Veg.lua`
   asked each farm for `work_radius or service_area_radius` and counted offers in
   a world-distance circle of that size. **A farm has neither field**:
   `work_radius` is declared on `DroneNode` — hubs, rovers and extenders
   (`DroneControl.lua:40`) — and `service_area_radius` appears **nowhere in the
   shipped tree**. Both farms would have logged *"no serving radius resolved,
   comparison NOT SAMPLED"* and the leg would have produced everything except the
   number it exists for. Repaired to walk `command_centers` and their working
   `linked_extenders` and count in **hexes**, mirroring `FindTaskRequesters`
   (`DroneControl.lua:315-324`) — which is also a closer read of the owner's own
   sentence than the farm-centred circle was. ⚠️ Same family as the three
   instrument defects `C47` disclosed: trusting *how* a value is read rather than
   checking *what* it is.
2. ⛔ **The arming script could not disarm this payload.** `C47_ARM.ps1`'s strip
   pattern was `Code/9[78]_C47`, which does not match `98_C48Veg` — the file is
   named after the case it measures. So each `arm-veg` added a `metadata.lua`

   returned to its shipped shape each ADDED a stray line** pointing at a Lua file
   that is not on disk (`a9d5c43`, `f4d3bb0`). Both patterns now match the family
   (`C4[78]`); the strays were stripped by the repaired script itself and the
   gate re-read the result off disk (TestKit `15cb9d4`).

## Relationship to `C47`

`C47` says the Open Farm's buffer is too small for its cadence. This entry says
the supply feeding that buffer may be being routed badly. **They are compatible
and the second may be the cause of the first's symptom** — a bigger buffer would
mask a trickle-feed problem, which is exactly why `C47` must not be repaired
until this is settled. ⛔ **Do not build a `C47` fix while `C48` is open.**
