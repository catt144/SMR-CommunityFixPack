### C91 — Building Codes' maintenance modifier survives the law's repeal (P3) `[cand 2026-09-12: source-read; not reproduced; found by C88's build]`

**Found 2026-09-12** answering the open S1 question in `prompts/C88_PREFAB_BUILD.md`: *what removes the Building
Codes maintenance modifier when the player repeals the law mid-game?* **Answer: nothing does.**

#### The asymmetry inside each law (SOURCE, game 1.1.0.403908)

Both `Policy_BuildingCodesLax` and `Policy_BuildingCodesStrict` (`Data/LawDef/LawDef-Efficiency.lua`) have two
halves, and only one of them is reversible:

| half | how it is applied | reverted on repeal? |
|---|---|---|
| construction **cost** | `LawEffectModifyLabel` in the law's `Effects` | ✅ yes — a `LawEffect` has `OnStart`/`OnStop`, and `OnStop` undoes it |
| **maintenance** | a `ConstructionComplete` `MsgReaction` calling `bld:SetModifier("maintenance_resource_amount", self.id, 0, self:GetParameterValue("maintenance_change"), self.display_name)` (`:696-706` Lax, `:904-914` Strict) | ⛔ **no** |

A `MsgReaction` has no stop hook: it writes a persisted modifier onto each building as that building completes,
and the law's deactivation never revisits them.

#### Every `OnMsg.LawDeactivated` handler in the tree, enumerated — none touches it

- `Lua/Factions/Laws.lua:463` — `RecalcApprovalOnLawChange`; faction approval only.
- `Lua/Factions/Laws.lua:393` / `:884` — `SanityCheckActiveLaws` and `AssertNoPolitics`; debug asserts.
- `Lua/Factions/Legislature.lua:425` — the governing-law thread and the Efficient Assembly preparation queue only.

⇒ Nothing clears `maintenance_resource_amount` by law id on repeal.

#### The only clear-down that exists is a ONE-SHOT savegame fixup

`SavegameFixups.RemoveRepealedBuildingCodesMaintenance` (`Lua/Factions/Laws.lua:465-473`) does exactly the right
thing — `AllMapsForEach(true, "RequiresMaintenance", ...)` and `SetModifier(prop, law_id, 0, 0)` for each law that
is **not** active, and `SetModifier` with both amounts zero takes the `remove` branch
(`Lua/Modifiers.lua:181-204`). But a savegame fixup runs **once per save**, on the first load after the fixup
existed. ⭐ Its existence is itself the tell: the developers found this leak in the field and swept it, rather
than fixing the repeal path. So a repeal **after** that sweep leaks again, with nothing left to clear it.

#### Player-visible effect (INFERRED from the lines above; not observed)

A player who enacts Strict, builds, then repeals it keeps the **−30 %** maintenance discount on every building
completed while it was active. Under Lax they keep the **+50 %** penalty. Either way the law's effect outlives the
law. ⚠️ The favourable direction (keeping Strict's discount) is a GAIN and by the house rule
(`fix-negatives-not-small-positives`, owner 09-08) is not chased on its own; the **Lax** direction is a LOSS and is
the half that would justify a repair.

#### Reachability

**R2 — conditional.** Needs: the Martian Assembly built, Building Codes enacted, at least one building completed
while it was active, and the law then repealed or replaced. All four are ordinary play, and the two laws are new
in 1.1.0 — 1.0.7 had only the old cost-only Building Codes law, now `Obsolete` — so the window is short and the
leak is young, which may be why no field report mentions it.

#### Our exposure: none new (and this is why C88 uses the law's id)

`Code/Fix_BuildingCodesPrefab.lua` (C88, built 2026-09-12) adds the same modifier to prefab-deployed buildings,
**under the law's own id**, which the owner ruled (checklist 150). ⇒ Our modifier leaks on repeal **exactly as
vanilla's does** and is cleared by **exactly the same savegame fixup**: one more building inside an existing leak,
never a new kind of leak. That was the condition under which C88's build was allowed to continue past its S1 stop
(the brief softened the stop to "file and tell" because the id ruling removes the reason to stop). ⛔ Had we used
our own id, we would have created a second, unswept leak — and it would also stack with Paradox's coming patch.

#### Fix sketch (NOT built, and NOT recommended without the owner)

An additive `OnMsg.LawDeactivated` that mirrors the shipped fixup for the two law ids — i.e. run
`RemoveRepealedBuildingCodesMaintenance`'s body on repeal instead of only on a version bump. Cheap and it uses the
game's own sweep. ⚠️ But it is **not** obviously ours to make: it would also revoke the discount from buildings a
player has had for a hundred sols, which is a balance change the player may experience as a loss, and the
gain/loss direction differs per law. ⇒ A **dev report** first (they clearly know about the leak — they wrote the
fixup), and only then an owner decision.

#### Not opened

Whether a law being **replaced** by the other Building Codes option (same `policy = "BuildingCodes"`, so the two
are mutually exclusive) leaves BOTH modifiers on a building that spans the change — the plausible worst case, and
unread · whether any other `LawDef` writes a persisted modifier from a `MsgReaction` with no stop hook (the same
shape, tree-wide, uncounted) · whether the shipped fixup has already run on the owner's saves.
