### C117 — salvaging a busy hub passage drops colonists crossing it outside (P2) `[fixed 2026-09-24: desk control; pending play and audit]`

#### Must_Read_Header

Built on `hubset`, pending attended play and audit. Hub audit S5 was cleared and corrected by the
Codex [resweep](../reports/MIGRATION_CROSSCHECK_HUB_RESWEEP_2026-09-24.md). It ships only as part of
the hub release set below.
The 07 sitting's instrument could not sample it: the watch timed out before the salvage countdown ended. See the dated sitting section.
The 07B pass discriminated the fix: fix off left all 43 hub landings outside, fix on none of 37.

#### Source (archived 1.1.1.405907)

- `WouldStrandHubColonists` lets a demolition proceed at once while another exit to the hub is live
  (`Lua/Passage.lua:1134-1138`).
- `OnDemolish` then calls `DisconnectDomes` (:1181), which removes the tunnel metadata (:1404,
  1742-1750, 710).
- A colonist already crossing reads `element.is_pf_tunnel` only at :1224. With the metadata gone it
  takes the non-hub branch: no hub holder, no marker, and it is outside.
- Corrected by the resweep: the elements are not deleted straight away. :1182-1184 waits for
  traversers before `Demolishable.lua:132-140` calls `DoneObject`. The separate [C42](C42.md)
  stale-list claim needs correction: `PassageGridElement.OnEnterUnit = empty_func` at :819 bypasses
  the `LeadIn` holder registration that C42's current source account requires.

#### Harm

A colonist arrives outside beside the hub with its oxygen timer free to start and no marker, so it
is invisible to [C99](C99.md)'s guard. Priced in the mid game, when players rebuild around hubs (C99's
reporter was replacing a dome).

#### Reach

**R2 conditional, SOURCE:** salvage one of two or more hub passages during shift traffic. The
source's own comment at `Passage.lua:1168-1169` expects arriving colonists until the traverse wait
dries up, yet the sibling shortcut at :1134-1138 skips the pre-disconnect wait. This is a
self-contradiction in the shipped path. The race has not been observed in play.

#### Live control (not run)

Salvage one busy spoke while another stays. Log each crossing colonist's endpoint before and after
the disconnect, its final holder, marker and outside timer, element membership and later removal.

- **Supports:** colonists arriving right after the disconnect have `passage_hub == nil` and stand
  outside.
- **Refutes:** they arrive on the hub with holder and marker set.

#### Fix constraints (owner approved fixing the set, 2026-09-24)

Keep the arriving colonist's endpoint independent of the mutable tunnel metadata, or drain
traversers before the disconnect while refusing new entries. Do not restore a removed tunnel just to
keep its metadata. A plain wait-wrapper on `OnDemolish` can admit fresh traffic forever and adds a
blocking frame; it needs cancellation and save analysis (FIX_POLICY §3a) in the build design.

#### 2026-09-24 — hubset build, desk verified; pending play and audit

- **SOURCE route, archived 1.1.1.405907.** Native `OnDemolish` marks `hub_draining` before its
  pre-disconnect `WouldStrandHubColonists` loop (`Lua/Passage.lua:1165-1181`). The loop can be
  extended synchronously for traversers already in `self.traversing_colonists`; no full copy of
  `TraverseTunnel` or `OnDemolish` and no persisted field is needed. A new `TraverseTunnel` entry
  returns false only while another valid, active, non-demolishing, non-draining sibling remains.
  Native `Unit:TraverseTunnel` clears that rejected unit's path (`Lua/Units/Unit.lua:238-245`).
- **BUILD, branch `hubset` at `38cd46c`.** `Code/Fix_PassageHubSalvageDrain.lua` wraps the native
  predicate and traversal entry. It leaves PF tunnel removal, the C99 last-exit predicate,
  cancellation and the passage destructor in shipped code. The C42 teardown wrapper touches a
  different method; no wrapper order is shared. The module's manifest pins both shipped bodies,
  and its `Require` check declines an unknown hub sibling shortcut.
- **DESK, command `python tools/desk_c117_hub_salvage.py` in `hubset` at `38cd46c`.** The archived
  [output](../../archive/logs/hubset_c117_desk_2026-09-24.txt) records the named demands and
  their total. With the module, an in-flight colonist reaches the hub with holder and marker
  before the PF endpoint is removed; without it, the same interleaving takes the native outside
  branch. The last-exit wait remains native, new entries with a usable sibling are refused and
  clear their path, cancellation reopens entry, and a fresh runtime needs only native drain fields.
  The harness loads archived demolition, traversal and disconnect bodies; its scheduler and movement
  seams are fixtures.
- **SAVE/REMOVAL (§3a).** The predicate is a synchronous layer-3 input to native `OnDemolish`.
  Traversal rejection is synchronous; accepted calls tail-delegate as layer 2. There is no
  mod-owned thread, blocked mod frame or new persisted state. Native cancellation deletes the
  waiting demolition thread and clears `hub_draining` (`Lua/Demolishable.lua:33-50`,
  `Lua/Passage.lua:1104,1154-1160`). A save with the mod still installed retains the native wait
  path; actual serialization and mod removal during that saved wait were not exercised.
- **LIMIT.** Desk controls establish the decision logic and the native `ClearPath` result, not a
  real game's reroute, timing, save serialization or a player's encounter with the race. Link 07
  owns a busy-spoke salvage on and off in game. Simultaneous salvage of every spoke retains native
  C99 behavior; this module does not assert that its existing predicate protects that separate
  case.
