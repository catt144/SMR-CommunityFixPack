### C117 — salvaging a busy hub passage drops colonists crossing it outside (P2) `[cand 2026-09-24: source-verified conditional race; unobserved]`

#### Must_Read_Header

Candidate, not built. Hub audit S5, cleared and corrected by the Codex
[resweep](../reports/MIGRATION_CROSSCHECK_HUB_RESWEEP_2026-09-24.md). It ships only as part of the
hub release set below.

#### Source (archived 1.1.1.405907)

- `WouldStrandHubColonists` lets a demolition proceed at once while another exit to the hub is live
  (`Lua/Passage.lua:1134-1138`).
- `OnDemolish` then calls `DisconnectDomes` (:1181), which removes the tunnel metadata (:1404,
  1742-1750, 710).
- A colonist already crossing reads `element.is_pf_tunnel` only at :1224. With the metadata gone it
  takes the non-hub branch: no hub holder, no marker, and it is outside.
- Corrected by the resweep: the elements are not deleted straight away. :1182-1184 waits for
  traversers before `Demolishable.lua:132-140` calls `DoneObject`. Kicks from stale element lists
  ([C42](C42.md)) are a separate consequence to measure.

#### Harm

A colonist arrives outside beside the hub with its oxygen timer free to start and no marker, so it
is invisible to [C99](C99.md)'s guard. Priced in the mid game, when players rebuild around hubs (C99's
reporter was replacing a dome).

#### Reach

Salvaging one of two or more hub passages during shift traffic. Not observed in play.

#### Control (not run)

Salvage one busy spoke while another stays. Log each crossing colonist's endpoint before and after
the disconnect, its final holder, marker and outside timer, element membership and later removal.

- **Supports:** colonists arriving right after the disconnect have `passage_hub == nil` and stand
  outside.
- **Refutes:** they arrive on the hub with holder and marker set.

#### Fix shape (owner approved fixing the set, 2026-09-24; least invasive complete shape not settled)

Keep the arriving colonist's endpoint independent of the mutable tunnel metadata, or drain
traversers before the disconnect while refusing new entries. Do not restore a removed tunnel just to
keep its metadata. A plain wait-wrapper on `OnDemolish` can admit fresh traffic forever and adds a
blocking frame; it needs cancellation and save analysis (FIX_POLICY §3a) in the build design.

#### Release (owner ruling, 2026-09-24)

Ships in one release with the rest of the hub set (C114, C115, C116, C117, C42, C111, F127 and the P3
pins in `Fix_VacuumWalks`; F127 and P3 added by the owner the same day), never alone.
The owner's words: "If possible and we are going to fix them all I would like to do the fixes and
get them out all at once. They all touch surfaces near the same thing so if we push one part people
read that as fixed, and then if they hit the surface of one of the other parts then they say 'well
you said it was fixed but X is still borken'".

#### Not opened

Whether the stale-element kicks after `DoneObject` teleport colonists into a removed passage in
play · save/reload during the drain.
