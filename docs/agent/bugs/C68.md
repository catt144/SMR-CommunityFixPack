### C68 — failed service entry still consumes the reserved meal `[cand 2026-09-10: source-read; desk control only]`

**DIFF-CAUSED. SOURCE intent tell:** the new reservation comment says food is
consumed “on arrival”, but `Colonist:VisitService` places fulfillment and
`Eat` outside the successful `EnterBuilding` conditional. Only service traits
and animation are guarded (`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:2467-2484`); fulfillment,
eating and ingredient effects execute at `:2485-2503` even after a false entry.
The old body (`old Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:1956-1995`) had no reservation path
and serviced only after successful entry.

**SOURCE ROUTE — R2, base/non-owner:** a normal meal is reserved by
`Dome:GetFoodService :3460-3477`; cross-map/elevator routing supplies concrete
false returns when no elevator reaches the building or travel ends on the
wrong map (`Lua/Units/ColonistTransport.lua:701-735`). Base services and
colonists require no DLC.

**DESK CONTROL:** `tools/desk_caller_seam.py` executes the shipped 1.1.0
`VisitService` body. With identical valid service/meal fixtures,
`EnterBuilding=true` and `false` both call fulfillment once and eat 1000. The
explicit shims cover validity, destructors, service assignment, time/sleep,
traits/laws and the service callbacks. A successful-entry guard is the
counterfactual; scheduler and native movement are not emulated.

**Independent recipe / falsifier:** TAKEABLE WHEN a fresh 1.1.0 multi-map
colony has a reserved meal and its elevator/service route can be invalidated
without harming the campaign. Remove or disable the route after reservation
but before entry; record service Food, the colonist's hunger/meal state, and
arrival. Vacuous if no reservation existed or entry succeeded. If the portion
is returned and hunger is unchanged after a genuine false entry, the candidate
is false. No fix is proposed.
