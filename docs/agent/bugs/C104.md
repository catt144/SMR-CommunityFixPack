### C104 - Political Animal cannot be earned on 1.1.0 (P2) `[cand 2026-09-17: shipped-body desk control, falsified by variants; not reproduced; no fix built]`

**VERDICT.** On 1.1.0 no normal-play order of enactments satisfies the shipped
`CheckPoliticalAnimalAchievement`, for **every** player, because two policies it requires
refuse to coexist. **Norman DLC owners** hit a second such pair. A **Politician** commander hits
a third, independent blocker. All three arrived in 1.1.0. The exclusions are intended, and the
achievement check was never taught about them.

#### Field report

Steam discussion post, 2026-09-16: *"How exactly am I supposed to get the Political animal
achievement (enabling all laws) since last patch some laws are mutually exclusive. Voted
everything I could except the ones I can't for that specific reason, and I didn't get the
achievement."* The report is consistent with every finding below. Its "since last patch" matches
the 1.0.7 diff. Nothing here depends on it.

#### Source route - game 1.1.0.403908

1. **The check.** `CheckPoliticalAnimalAchievement` (`Lua/Factions/Laws.lua:894-926`) walks
   `g_Legislature:RecalcPoliciesState()` and returns early unless **every** walked policy has a law
   active **at that moment**. It runs on `LawActivated` (`Data/Achievement.lua:848-854`) and once as
   a savegame fixup (`Laws.lua:928-930`). Activating a law replaces the active law of the same
   policy (`Lua/Factions/LawDef.lua:51-57`), so the requirement is one law per policy.
2. **Only three exemptions** (`Laws.lua:909-919`): a hidden `PreIndependence` policy after
   independence, a hidden `PostIndependence` policy before it, and `CountInAchievementCalc = false`.
   Any other hidden policy still counts.
3. **Membership.** `Legislature:RecalcPoliciesState` (`Lua/Factions/Legislature.lua:1177-1293`)
   takes every non-obsolete policy with at least one non-obsolete law. `ForEachPreset` skips
   `Obsolete` presets (`CommonLua/Preset.lua:1773`), so the 1.1.0 obsoletions cannot block. That
   refutes the first theory checked. **67 walked, 66 counted** (only `FounderPrivilege` is
   exempt). After independence the 5 `PreIndependence` policies are hidden and exempt, so
   **61 are required**.
4. **Pair 1, every player: Night Shift Compensations and Right to Rest.**
   `Data/PolicyDef.lua:223` (`NightShiftCompensations`) carries the policy disable condition
   *"The 'Right to Rest' law must not be active"* (`:226-230`, `ActiveLaws.Policy_QualityRest`).
   `:776` (`QualityRest`) carries the mirror *"The 'Night Shift Compensations' law must not be
   active"* (`:779-783`). Both are counted, neither is Pre/Post, and each has one law.
5. **Pair 2, Norman DLC: Secondary Produce and GMO.** `DLC/norman/Presets/PolicyDef.lua:14`
   (`SecondaryProduce`, locked while `ActiveLaws.Policy_GMO`, `:17-21`) and `:58` (`GMO`, locked
   while any `SecondaryProduce` law is active, `:65`). The policy description states the intent:
   *"Cannot be active at the same time as the GMO law"* (`:28`).
6. **Politician commander: Efficient Assembly.** The law's `Prerequisite` is
   `not (GetCommanderProfile().id == "politician")` (`Data/LawDef/LawDef-Efficiency.lua:153`). The
   profile already grants the effect: `IsAssemblyEfficient` returns true for a Politician
   (`Legislature.lua:1698`). So the policy is `hidden` (`Legislature.lua:1259`), which none of the
   three exemptions covers, and it stays required.
7. **The locks are enforced wherever a player or AI enacts.** They are checked by the voting card
   (`Data/XDef/LawVotingCard.lua:367-368`; the same policy/law lock pair recurs at seven more sites
   in that file, `:450`-`:683`, not each read), by faction
   session suggestions (`Legislature.lua:311-312`) and by the governing assembly (`:1536`, `:1581`).
   `Legislature:EnactLaw` itself does not re-check (`:629-673`). The only enactors that bypass
   the voting card are: game-start initial laws (`Legislature.lua:8-75`), an Assembly choice's
   `initial_laws` (`Lua/Factions/Factions.lua:1249-1255`), and `Effect_Law`. **None can supply a
   pair half.** A tree-wide grep for the four exclusive law ids outside `LawDef` files finds
   them only in code and the policy definitions, never in any `initial_laws` list, even though
   the same pattern hits `QualityRest` in `Laws.lua` and `Workplace.lua`. The only shipped
   `Effect_Law` enacts `Policy_HeavyWorkload` under the `NoPolitics` rule
   (`Data/GameRuleDef.lua:48-50`). No law effect repeals another law: the one `Deactivate` in the
   law data is `FounderPrivilege` removing itself (`LawDef-Founder Stage.lua:83-90`).
8. **The daily revoke reads law-level conditions only** (`RevokeLawsDueToConditions`,
   `Laws.lua:590-606`). Both pairs are policy-level, so a colony that already held both halves
   keeps them. That is the one route left open, and it needs pre-1.1.0 state (not sampled).

#### What changed in 1.1.0 - the intent tell

Checked against the 1.0.7.396349 archive with the same extractor. In 1.0.7,
`NightShiftCompensations` and `QualityRest` carry **no** disable conditions,
`SecondaryProduce`/`GMO` do **not exist**, and `EfficientAssembly` has **no** prerequisite.
`CheckPoliticalAnimalAchievement` **was edited in 1.1.0**. The whole diff is one added exemption,
for hidden `PostIndependence` policies. The developers maintained this function in the same patch
that introduced the three blockers, taught it one new hidden class, and missed these. Together
with the stated exclusivity (item 5), the requirement is unintended, not the exclusion.

#### Desk control - `python tools/desk_c104_political_animal.py`

It loads all 15 shipped registry files and 22 shipped bodies under their real file names and
lines. The fixture is an independent colony with an Assembly, everything prepared, and every
non-law disable condition already met, so only law-on-law locks remain. **34 of 34 demands hold**
at HEAD `695f34a`:

- Instrument agreement: 182/87/8 presets (the grep count), 67 walked (the static extractor), and
  61 required after independence.
- Honest enactment through the voting-card gate leaves exactly one law of each pair
  un-enactable and never unlocks.
- **Positive control:** force the two locked laws in and the set is revoke-stable (daily revoke
  removes nothing), and the shipped check **unlocks**. Dropping `NightShiftCompensations`,
  `QualityRest` or `GMO` refuses it.
- Both enactment orders of both pairs lock the second law (8 legs, across all three
  `SecondaryProduce` laws).
- **Lock graph, enumerated from the shipped bodies across all 61 required policies**: exactly the
  two mutual pairs plus Dictatorship locking Apportionment and AssemblyType. That last one is
  one-way, because Dictatorship requires their Single Party and Consulting laws, and it resolves
  by enacting Dictatorship last. No other lock exists.
- Politician: `EfficientAssembly` is hidden and still required, and unlock is refused even with
  both pairs forced in. Every other commander sees it unhidden.
- Fix shape, **not a recommendation**: `CountInAchievementCalc = false` on one policy of each pair
  lets honest enactment unlock, and adding it on `EfficientAssembly` does the same for a Politician.
- Named stubs: registry construction, law effects (route item 7 shows none repeal another law),
  the MsgReaction retype, the unlock sink, and `requirement()`, which retypes `Laws.lua:909-919`
  only to choose laws and is held to the shipped check by the positive and drop-one legs.
  `procall` records every error, and every leg demands zero.

**Falsified by scratch variants** (in memory, patching the loader's read of `Data/PolicyDef.lua`;
the tree was not touched). Removing the two Night Shift / Right to Rest disable conditions failed
exactly the 4 pair-specific demands. Adding `CountInAchievementCalc = false` to `EfficientAssembly`
failed exactly the 4 requirement/Politician demands. Every other demand still held.

Side observation, **shipped behaviour and not a defect:** Open Domes can be enacted over Energy
Efficiency *Oxygen*, and the next day revokes Oxygen (its condition reads "the Open Domes law is
repealed"). Power and Water remain, so it does not block (leg B2).

#### Who is affected, and who can test

- **Reach:** any player on any platform who takes politics to the end of a new 1.1.0 game.
  Norman owners and Politician commanders carry extra blockers.
- **A mod-side fix could only help Steam and other non-Store PC players.** On PlayStation, Xbox
  and the Microsoft Store any enabled mod blocks all achievements (`EF-106`).
- **Testing:** enacting 61 laws is not a practical sitting. The achievement test route is
  `EF-094` (move `account.dat` aside). A TestKit read of the requirement set plus this harness is
  the realistic check.
- **The reporter's own non-unlock needs no other cause.** The usual differentials are cheats
  (`EF-095`), a `DisableAchievements` game rule, or an open mod editor. Mods do not block on Steam.

#### Solution options - no module built

Same defect class as [[C92]], a counted requirement a player cannot satisfy. ⚖️ The owner's C92
ruling (build and test allowed, **shipping held until lifted in words**) is the nearest precedent,
but it was made for C92 and does not extend here by inference.

- **(a) Data patch:** `CountInAchievementCalc = false` on one policy of each pair and on
  `EfficientAssembly`. The desk shows it suffices. Which half of each pair to exempt is a design
  call. Exempting `EfficientAssembly` for everyone slightly weakens the requirement for
  non-Politicians.
- **(b) Check-side:** count a policy as satisfied when its own policy-level disable condition is
  currently true, or when it is hidden by a prerequisite. That is closer to "enact every law you
  can", but it replaces a shipped function body.
- **Dev report:** the developers edited this exact function in 1.1.0, so a report naming the
  three blockers is cheap and likely to land.

#### Not opened

- Not reproduced in a game; no player save sampled.
- Whether 1.0.7 was earnable end to end was not run; only the diff was read.
- Other achievements with the same "counted but unsatisfiable" shape (Truly Independent,
  Opportunist and the rest of the `Remaster` group) were not swept. [[C92]] and this entry make
  two.
- Whether a pre-1.1.0 colony holding both Night Shift laws carried into 1.1.0 (route item 8)
  exists in the field.
- Iron Fist's `law.id == "Dictatorship"` was checked in passing and is correct: the law id is
  `Dictatorship`, `LawDef-Efficiency.lua:3`.
