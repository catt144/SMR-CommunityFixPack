### F120 — Tech-point conversion hides previously revealed mystery technologies `[fixed 2026-09-16: affected-save recovery desk-exercised for all 17 legacy mystery techs; retail unexercised]`

Investigation and retained negative results: [Wildfire research report](../reports/WILDFIRE_CURE_RESEARCH.md);
its closing section is the cross-vendor audit (Fable, 2026-09-16) whose verdict was
**SHIP WITH CHANGES** and whose changes are the ones recorded below.

**MEASURED:** the shipped discovery converter leaves the entire Wildfire family
hidden for a synthetic legacy player with a discovered cure and sufficient tech
points. A second PostLoadGame sweep does not recover it. The same converter
successfully restores a discovered breakthrough control. Command and source
hashes: [desk evidence](../../archive/wildfire_cure_desk_before_20260916.txt).

**MEASURED class (audit, 2026-09-16):** the omission is the whole field, not the
cure. The archived 1.0.7 registry places **17** technologies in the `Mysteries`
field (`tools/desk_mystery_tech_migration.py`, `LEGACY` line: regex over
`PlaceObj('TechPreset'` blocks with `group = "Mysteries"` in the 1.0.7
`Data/TechPreset.lua`), and the harness shows conversion hides every one of them
and recovery restores every one's entrance. Three are chains whose entrance is
the `_1` member named by `MysteryTechRevealRemapping` (AlienDiggersDestruction,
AlienDiggersDetection, WildfireCure); fourteen are single nodes that are their
own entrance. Every one of the **55** `group = "Mysteries"` presets in the 1.1.0
`Data/Tech.lua` carries `LockState = "hidden"` (Python regex over `PlaceObj('Tech'`
blocks, audit transcript). The two remaining remapped chains
(AncientArtifactAdaptedMachine, BottomlessPitResearchCenter) sit in the
`BuriedWonders` field in both registries, which the converter preserves; it
unlocks their **final** node directly, leaving `_1`..`_3` hidden, a shortcut in the
player's favour that no scenario waits on and is not filed. Wildfire is the only
member with a partial vanilla rescue: `SA_GrantTechBoost` special-cases exactly
one tech (`Lua/Sequences/SA_Gameplay.lua:824`). The other members have none, and
nine of the eleven mysteries gate on `SA_WaitResearch`, so a legacy colony
mid-mystery is stuck there after conversion.

**INHERITED report:** Steam, Jäger, 2026-09-14, owner-relayed: “I seem to be
having an issue with the Wildfire Mystery, there is no option to research the
cure in the new tech tree.” No save, log, migration history, mod list or mystery
stage was supplied. **This mechanism can produce that symptom; the player's
specific cause remains unconfirmed.** No reporter save is being requested.

**MEASURED platform limit:** retail Steam refuses the archived 1.0.7 save
revision. Non-Steam retail offers **Load anyway**, which can reach conversion.
The harness executes `ValidateSaveMetadata` with shipped configuration
(`Lua/Config/config.lua:174-175`, minimum 402200; block on Steam, warn elsewhere).
**Audit reach verdict:** the Steam news feed for app 3215050 (read 2026-09-16
via the Steam news API) lists public builds 1.0.7 (2026-02-06), a Linux
stability update (2026-02-21) and then 1.1.0 (2026-09-12); the pack's first 1.1.0
boot on 2026-09-08 already recorded 403908 (EF-078), and 1.0.7 saves carry
396349 (EF-080). No public Steam build sits between the save floor and the
research rewrite, so **a Steam reporter's legacy colony cannot reach this
converter and this fix does not answer that report.** The report stays open;
a fresh 1.1.0 colony also lacks the legacy state this fix requires. The fix
repairs a real defect for non-Steam players and for any Steam player who loads
a legacy save by other means.

#### Cause and reachability

**SOURCE, R2 / platform-conditional:** a non-Steam colony that revealed a
mystery technology on 1.0.7 and accepts the old-save warning when crossing to
1.1.0 retains `tech_status.<id>.discovered`. The old creator stores
`field = "Mysteries"` (`Lua/Research.lua:668-682`, archived 1.0.7; every
Mysteries preset at `Data/TechPreset.lua:721-820`). That field is
`discoverable = false` (archived `Data/TechFieldPreset.lua:286-291`), so a
positive marker comes only from a scenario's `SA_RevealTech` (17 placements
across eleven mysteries and two buried wonders in `Lua/Scenario/*.generated.lua`,
identical between the trees) or a cheat. The scenario's `Field = "Special"` is
editor metadata, not this saved field.

The current converter `SavegameFixups.TechPoints_MigrateDiscoveredSpecialTechs`
(`Lua/TechTree.lua:1486-1502`, 1.1.0.403908) accepts only BuriedWonders,
Storybits and Breakthroughs. It drops Mysteries without an error. Newly created
lock state hides the current presets (`CommonLua/Features/LockablePreset.lua`,
`ResetLockablePresetState` and `PreProcessLockablePresets`). A scenario already
past its reveal does not replay that action merely because the game was upgraded.
`SA_WaitResearch:SAExec` continues waiting on the final tech.

**SOURCE + MEASURED, the completed case:** the sibling fixup
`SavegameFixups.TechPoints` (`Lua/TechTree.lua:1453-1484`) calls `UnresearchAll`
and refunds points, and the discovery marker survives it. A 1.0.7 colony that had
already **researched** its mystery technology therefore arrives unresearched
**and** hidden, with no node to buy back; the harness reproduces this for the
cure and shows the recovery restores its entrance too.

**SOURCE intent:** the converter explicitly preserves discovered event research
for sibling groups, and each scenario promises an available research node or
chain. Losing an earned discovery while retaining the waiting scenario violates
both contracts. This benefits ordinary vanilla colonies and needs no other mod.

**MEASURED qualification (Wildfire only):** a remaining medical-building grant
force-researches the first intermediate, which reopens the next node. This is a
recovery route only while the saved SpeedUpResearch loop still has a grant
available. A save that used up that loop before conversion has no remaining
medical grant to rescue its hidden chain. Loop exhaustion is a SOURCE condition,
not sampled in a retail colony.

#### Controls and repair contract

**MEASURED:** fresh reveal enables the chain head; sequential purchases reach
the final tech; zero points leave a revealed node visible but unaffordable;
medical grants advance intermediates without spending points; the final tech
still needs a point. `Field = "Special"` does not prevent the completed-tech
wait from returning. These are shipped-body desk results, not UI observations.

**Falsifier:** run the converter and PostLoadGame processing on an old colony
whose discovery predates conversion. If a shipped path already restores a
researchable family member, this hidden-family defect is absent for that state.
The harness must retain both that healthy control and the omitted-group case.

**Repair built:** [Fix_MysteryTechMigration.lua](../../../Code/Fix_MysteryTechMigration.lua)
(built `d004494` as `Fix_WildfireCureMigration.lua`, gated to the cure in a
Wildfire colony; renamed and widened by the audit). On PostLoadGame it collects
every legacy `tech_status` row with `field = "Mysteries"` and a positive
`discovered`, and for each whose current family (the tech plus each `<id>_N`
member) is entirely hidden it unlocks only the entrance the current scenario
would have revealed (`MysteryTechRevealRemapping[id] or id`, which must itself
belong to the family). No research, points, crops or scenario completion are
awarded. There is no colony-mystery gate: `DefenseTower` carries no `Mystery`
preset property while the other sixteen do, and the marker itself is the
evidence the scenario revealed the tech. Existing affected saves must recover on
load; a prevention-only change is insufficient (owner, 2026-09-16). A second
load must preserve recovery and skip the already healthy state. No new
persisted field or thread is used. This is an additive synchronous PostLoadGame
handler; it initialises any unprocessed presets first, preserving their vanilla
processed markers so a subsequent vanilla load does not hide the entrance
again. A closed-table behaviour probe checks the lock reader; missing modern
APIs, a missing remapping table and unknown probe results decline.

**SOURCE, reach of the repair (audit):** the unlock goes through the shipped
`UnlockTech` (`Lua/TechTree.lua:1275`), so it fires `TechUnlocked` to every
vanilla listener exactly as a scenario reveal does: `Lua/Mysteries/Mystery.lua:65`
discovers and notifies the colony's `Mystery_N` display node (measured in the
harness), `Lua/TechTree.lua:1258` rechecks prerequisites and refreshes the tree,
`PresetLockStateChanged` marks the node new, and the hint and faction task
checks run. Two things a live reveal also does are **not** re-applied: the
reveal's own `NotifyTechDiscovered` popup, and the reveal-time cost boost
(`ChangeResearchCost`, owned by [C79](C79.md)); the converter re-applies neither
for the sibling groups either.

**MEASURED verification:** `python tools/desk_mystery_tech_migration.py` passes
**85/85** named demands on 1.1.0.403908 (`grep -c "  PASS  "` on the
[after evidence](../../archive/mystery_tech_desk_after_20260916.txt) gives 85,
`FAIL` gives 0). The unpatched converter loses every legacy discovery; the
actual module restores each entrance on an already converted fixture; another
PostLoadGame is idempotent; vanilla PostLoadGame with the fix disabled retains
recovery; the first load in vanilla handler order (Tech presets unprocessed,
converter thread pending) recovers and leaves every other Mysteries preset
hidden; a legacy researched-then-unresearched cure recovers; buying the chain
releases an already suspended `SA_WaitResearch`. Controls cover partial and
completed progress, missing, zero and foreign-field markers, an uninitialised
owner, a legacy id absent from the registry, a remapping outside the family,
veto, inactive registry, changed or throwing lock reader, missing research API
and missing remapping table; a remapping changed inside the family is followed.
Two one-guard-reverted scratch variants of the module (hidden-family check
removed; positive-marker check removed) each failed exactly one named leg
(`vanilla or external recovery stands the repair down`; `no mutation: zero
discovery marker`), so the harness can fail. The earlier 37/37 Wildfire-only run
is retained as [d004494 evidence](../../archive/wildfire_cure_desk_after_20260916.txt).
`python tools/parsecheck.py --quiet` and `python -X utf8 tools/bodycheck.py
--module MysteryTechMigration --all` pass. These are desk models of cold/enable
paths, not retail boots; engine serialization itself was not exercised. No claim
of attended testing, retail recovery or crop production is made.

**Recovery use:** on a platform where the old save can be loaded, enable the
updated pack and load the affected save. The previously revealed node or chain
entrance becomes available in the Special/event research section. Research
normally, then save; no fresh colony or scenario restart is required for this
mechanism. This usage follows the exercised source path; its retail UI and
save/write/load round trip remain untested. F120 adds no old-save loading bypass.
