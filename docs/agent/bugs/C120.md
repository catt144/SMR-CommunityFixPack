### C120 — Deport-law rocket never launches: the departure wait refills itself (P2) `[built 2026-09-28: Code/Fix_DeportRocketLaunch.lua; desk-verified on archived 1.1.1 Lua, 27/27 with three falsifying mutants; not run in game]`

#### Report

takes off at all; it simply sits on the launchpad, taking on dozen after dozen of people."

can force launch a rocket"; keep Seniors in one dome "not remotely near a train station".

#### Mechanism

- **Intake never closes.** A landed player rocket whose arrival is Earth or not set runs
  `UpdateDepartureThread` (`UniversalRocket.lua:2360-2383`): `GenerateDepartures` then
  `Sleep(departure_tick)`, one game hour. Only `CmdTakeOff` stops it (`:600`).
- **No cap.** `GenerateDepartures` (`:2241-2276`) takes every colonist passing
  `IsColonistValidForDeparture` (`:2213-2223`: tourists going home, Earthsick, and Seniors,
  Renegades or Banished under their deport laws). Walk or train reach → `LeavingMars`; otherwise
  a shuttle stopover task to a dome near the pad (`:2256-2272`, new in 1.1.0).
- **The gate waits for zero.** `IsCargoReady` → `AreEarthDepartColonistsReady` →
  `GetPendingDepartureCount() == 0` (`:531-546`). The count is `#departures` plus stopover
  colonists on a committed ride or just dropped off (`:518-529`). One second at zero passes it,
  then a 10-minute hold and a second zero check (`:500-512`).
- **Why zero never comes.** Committed stopover rides count through flight, drop-off and the final
  walk; with busy shuttles they run one after another and their spans overlap. Colonists age one
  year per sol and turn Senior at 61 (`Units/Colonist.lua:671`, `:2378-2394`; `_GameConst.lua:405-406`),
  so about (Middle Aged count)/30 new deportees appear per sol. Sufficient condition: Seniors out of
  walk/train reach plus saturated shuttle service, reachable only from 1.1.0. Inflow plus long walk
  legs alone can also do it on 1.0.7.
- **Idle rockets hoard.** `IsRocketLanded` is true in `CmdWaitOrder` (`:1580-1583`), and the
  thread's stop test only fires when `arrival_loc` is set to somewhere other than Earth (`:2362`). A parked rocket with
  no destination drafts deportees into `boarded` and never launches; they return only when it gets
  a non-Earth destination (`ReturnDehydratedColonists`, `:2363`) or the law is repealed
  (`:2341-2348`). The same holds on 1.0.7 (`:1372-1375 @1.0.7`).
- **What the player sees.** "Waiting for colonists to depart" (`:972`) and "Departing colonists
  <count>" (`:3479-3482`). No per-rocket control stops the draft; the export toggles change cargo
  only. Manual Launch works because `waiting_departures` is not a blocker (`:1015-1016`, `:541`).

#### Classification

- **Unbounded count: design.** No build caps Earth-bound departures; `MaxColonistsPerRocket`
  applies to arrivals (`__const.lua:1179`), and the law text promises Seniors go "on the first
  available rocket" (`Data/LawDef/LawDef-Welfare.lua:2091-2094`).
- **Never launching: defect (liveness).** The launch gate waits on a quantity the same rocket keeps
  raising. Latent since 1.0.7; 1.1.0's stopovers made the draft colony-wide and tied the gate to
  shuttle availability.
- **Idle-rocket hoarding: separate oversight**, 1.0.7 and 1.1.x.
- Genre comparison (web survey, 2026-09-28): mature designs bound this with a capacity cap, a
  roster fixed at dispatch (RimWorld caravans, ONI rockets) or a timeout trickling arrivals cannot
  reset (Factorio's time/inactivity conditions); Transport Fever's max-wait resets on each partial
  load, the same trickle failure. This rocket has none of the three.

#### Harm

Mid to late game only: it needs a deport law (or tourists / Earthsick), a colony large enough to
age Seniors steadily, and an automated Earth-bound rocket. It does not touch the early game.
The rocket's other cargo and its schedule stall; the player can always escape with Launch, which is
why this is P2 rather than P1.

#### Fix shape (owner-chosen 2026-09-28)

Owner, 2026-09-28, after the kidnap comparison: *"I would think if the kidnap system can pull people
from trains / shuttles for expidition without breaking them it should also be fine for this? It
doesn't sound like the devs intended there to be a cap for this"*, then *"File it and give me a build
prompt"*. The premise was corrected before filing: the expedition draft does not pull colonists out of trains or shuttles;
it takes only idle or interruptible ones (`CargoTransporterNew:GatherAvailableColonists`,
`CargoTransporterNew.lua:235-285`: `Idle`/`Abandoned` or `CanChangeCommand()`, and skips
`thread_running_destructors`). K2 uses that selectivity.

- **K2.** When an automated Earth-bound rocket is ready apart from departures (`GetLaunchIssue()`
  is nil or `"waiting_departures"`, not `WaitsForManualLaunch()`), board every eligible colonist
  that is safely takeable now, through the game's own boarding path for deportees who cannot reach
  the rocket (`GetDehydratedData` → `insert(rocket.boarded, …)` → `CleanupLeavingColonist`,
  `Units/Colonist.lua:1165-1172`), then let it launch. Colonists still walking or riding are left to
  the release a manual Launch already performs (`rocket_left`, `:1163-1169`; `StopDepartureThread`,
  `UniversalRocket.lua:2385-2402`) and catch the next rocket.
- **E.** A landed rocket with no destination drafts nobody.
- **No cap** (owner). Not chosen: a draft cap (B), a timeout (C), dropping stopovers from the count (D).

#### Open before build (settled 2026-09-28, SOURCE unless tagged)

1. **Manual Launch's release is clean.** Walkers: a colonist in `LeavingMars` checks `rocket_left`
   only after its walk returns; the rocket is no longer landed (`IsRocketLanded`, `:1580-1583`), so
   it pops its destructor, sets `leaving = false`, leaves `departures` and returns to Idle
   (`Units/Colonist.lua:1163-1175`); its transport request and ticket were already cleared at the
   start (`:1129-1130`). One on the ramp during `CmdTakeOff` still boards (`:1179-1205`). Stopover
   riders: `StopDepartureThread` hands a committed ride back (`departure_rocket = false`) and
   clears an unclaimed pickup (`UniversalRocket.lua:2391-2399`); `leaving` was never set for
   them. Train riders hold a ticket whose reason is `LeavingMars`; on arrival `ExitVehicle`
   re-issues the raw command (`Units/ColonistTransport.lua:703`), `IsBoardingAllowed` is false for
   a rocket that left, and they are released like walkers. All three end `CanChangeCommand`,
   ticketless and eligible for the next rocket. The desk control checks the walker and the ride
   (MEASURED on the desk); trains are not modelled. Not settled: whether `GotoBuildingSpot`
   toward a rocket that has flown returns promptly (engine; vanilla's manual Launch has the same
   dependency).
2. **The fallback is safe in bulk, from every state K2 admits.** `CleanupLeavingColonist`
   (`Units/Colonist.lua:1107-1123`): deselects, drops the OverstayingTourists notice, sends
   `ColonistLeavingMars`, rebuilds the infopanel, respawns nobody while the rocket accepts
   boarding, and `DoneObject`s the colonist. `Colonist:Done` (`:301-335`) removes city labels and
   notices, runs `SetDome(false)` (workplace, residence and dome labels, `:399-466`), unapplies
   traits and clears transport. `GetDehydratedData` (`:1059-1088`) copies fields only and
   shares the `traits` and `status_effects` tables. The `LeavingMars` prelude before the branch
   adds `SetDome(false)`, `ClearDetrimentalStatusEffects`, `ClearTransportRequest` (reservations
   and task) and `DiscardTransportTicket` (`:1126-1131`). Every effect runs on the colonist's own
   command thread after its previous command's destructors, as for vanilla's own draftees, so
   `DoneObject` does not re-enter destructors (`CommonLua/Classes/CommandObject.lua:128-142`). K2's filter narrows
   the expedition's: `CanChangeCommand` is always required, as `GenerateDepartures` requires it
   (`UniversalRocket.lua:2250`). Two states are excluded because the route is not safe from
   them. A colonist on another map would be sent to `Abandoned` (`Units/Colonist.lua:1148-1153`);
   unlike `Unit:EnterTransporter` this path has no `TransferToMap`, so K2 takes the rocket's map
   slot only. `ClearTransportRequest` on a committed shuttle orders that shuttle Idle
   (`:2238-2256`) and vanilla never does it (`UniversalRocket.lua:2288`, `:2394`), so a
   colonist whose task has a shuttle is excluded too.
3. Committed stopover ride hanging forever: not settled. K2 does not depend on it, because a
   committed ride no longer holds the launch. Stale stopover task on a second rocket: none found.
   A colonist that one rocket drafts into `LeavingMars` has its other task cleared by the prelude
   (`:1129`), and the first rocket's `PurgeStopoverDepartures` drops the entry on `LeavingMars`
   or on invalidity (`UniversalRocket.lua:2282`). Arriving Seniors: not settled for vanilla's
   draft; `arriving` is cleared only at `OnArrival` (`Units/Colonist.lua:1637-1642`), and K2
   skips any colonist with it set.

#### Build (2026-09-28)

`Code/Fix_DeportRocketLaunch.lua`, Register id `DeportRocketLaunch`, registered in `metadata.lua` and
`items.lua` after `Fix_GhostPowerCells`. Layer 3 (FIX_POLICY §3a): synchronous wrappers, two
module-local weak tables, no thread, GameVar or saved field. The header carries the reasons.

- **K2**, a wrapper on `UniversalRocketBase:IsCargoReady`. It acts only when vanilla answers not
  ready and the same body answers ready with `AreEarthDepartColonistsReady` forced true for that
  one call, so departures are the only thing holding the launch. A manual-launch rocket and the
  `instant` path are untouched. It sends each takeable deportee the raw `LeavingMars` command
  with a one-shot mark keyed to the rocket's load cycle. The mark makes `Colonist:FindExit` answer
  false, so the shipped unreachable branch boards the colonist. The gate waits for those colonists
  and then opens even with walkers and rides pending, which takeoff releases.
- **E**, a wrapper on `UniversalRocketBase:UpdateDepartureThread`. With no destination it takes the
  shipped non-Earth branch: `ReturnDehydratedColonists`, then `StopDepartureThread`.
- **Not in the fix:** colonists on another map, colonists on a committed shuttle ride and
  colonists who are still arriving are left to vanilla, and so are train riders, whose ticket
  excludes them. Walkers already heading to an idle rocket when E stops its draft still board it;
  the next hourly update hands them back. In an existing save an idle rocket's live draft thread
  stops within one game hour. Each launch logs up to two lines per gate pass (boarding count,
  released count).
