### C58 — new spoilage can remove already-reserved meals in piles and depots (P2) `[cand 2026-09-10: source-read; native outcome needs a witness]`

**DIFF-CAUSED. SOURCE intent tell:** the recipe input host explicitly says
"meals already claimed by colonists in transit stay edible"
(`Lua/Buildings/RecipeProductionBuilding.lua:59`) and subtracts reservations
from spoilage input at `:60`. `HasConsumption:BuildingDailyUpdate :129-143`
also subtracts `consumption_reserved`. Piles and depots do not.

**SOURCE:** 1.1.0 `Lua/Spoilage.lua:45-64` passes physical stored amounts to
`CalcResourceSpoilage`; the latter (`:17-34`) knows no reservation total.
`StorageDepot:SpoilStoredResources` (`Lua/Buildings/StorageDepot.lua:120-130`)
uses request `GetActualAmount`, which includes assigned stock, instead of the
unassigned target. `ResourceStockpileBase:TakeMealPortion`
(`ResourceStockpile.lua:375-388`) leaves physical Food in place and stamps
`colonist.meal_assigned_amount`; `FulfillMealPortion :390-402` later releases
the request reservation and debits that recorded amount.

**SOURCE ROUTE — R2; downstream result U:** colonist entering the raw-food fallback ->
`Dome:GetFoodService` (`Dome.lua:3460-3499`) -> raw-food fallback pile/depot ->
`TakeMealPortion`; before arrival, new-sol `OnMsg.NewDay`
(`Spoilage.lua:66-91`) handles labeled non-building piles, while
`StorageDepot:BuildingDailyUpdate :116-118` handles depots on their first
building tick noticing the new day (`Building.lua:809-816`), not necessarily
at the global midnight callback. `Colonist.lua:2489-2496` fulfills the ordinary
pile reservation then eats; direct FoodBuilding fulfillment instead uses its
own protected storage. `RecipeInputStockpile` and
`ConsumptionResourceStockpile` override the ordinary-pile meal methods. Base Food Depot
`Lua/BuildingTemplate/StorageFood.generated.lua:5,30-32` uses
`UniversalStorageDepotBase`, a `StorageDepot` subclass (`StorageDepot.lua:329-330`)
with supply requests (`:357`) and stockpile meal methods.
Its `AddResource :562-569` adds to physical stock without a Lua clamp.
New `Lua/_TaskRequest.lua:391-399` passes the AMOUNT to native AssignUnit,
not a colonist object; native AddAmount/Unassign behavior is not in Src.

**SOURCE old counterpart:** 1.0.7 has no `Lua/Spoilage.lua`, no equivalent
daily decay caller, and `ResourceStockpileBase:Service`
(`ResourceStockpile.lua:375-407`, clamp at `:391`) consumes
the minimum of physical stock and the assigned amount (falling back to the
visit amount when no assignment exists), instead of the new full deferred debit.

**INFERRED severity:** includes players **WITHOUT the DLC**: ordinary Food,
raw piles, Food Depots and the base decay setting exist without norman. A sol
boundary can decay food promised to a colonist; possible later accounting
underflow, clamping, lost service, or a native error remains UNMEASURED.
This entry does not claim negative stock or a crash was observed.

**Independent recipe [NEVER RUN]:** provision a fresh 1.1.0 colony with normal food services unavailable, where a
colonist using the raw-food fallback has reserved most of the remaining Food in a raw pile or Food Depot and
is still traveling when the relevant pile/depot spoilage hook executes. Record physical stock, request actual/
target and meal_assigned_amount before decay, after decay and after the visit.
Use an unreserved matching pile as the decay control and an input-consumption
host as the reservation-protection control. Vacuous if a normal service wins, the fallback flag is absent, the visit finishes first,
decay rounds to zero, food arrives, no assignment occurred, or sufficient
unreserved stock hides the difference. RNG makes a single zero-spoil sol inconclusive.

**Falsifier:** reserved Food survives the decay boundary in both the raw-pile and
depot paths, or native request handling demonstrably maintains coherent physical,
request and colonist state through the later consumption. No synthetic native
request implementation can settle that outcome; no desk pass is claimed.
