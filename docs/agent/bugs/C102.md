### C102 — Expedition returnees are sent to a switched-off dome and suffocate (P2) `[cand 2026-09-16: SOURCE-VERIFIED on both return receivers; NOT reproduced; the reporter's trigger condition NOT isolated]`

⛔ **NOT A REOPENING OF [C95](C95.md).** C95 stops habitat residents being drafted. This is what
happens to **any** returnee — habitat resident or not — when the return path finds no welcoming
community with a free bed in walking range of the rocket. The reporter's words are in C95.

#### The mechanism (SOURCE, game 1.1.0.403908)

1. `CargoTransporterNew:UnloadPassengers` (`Lua/CargoTransporterNew.lua:1080-1094`; legacy twin
   `RocketBase:Disembark`, `Lua/Buildings/RocketBase.lua:1969-1988`) takes
   `domes, safety_dome, _, dome_elevators = GetDomesReachableByColonists(self.city, self:GetPos())`.
2. In `GetDomesReachableByColonists` (`Lua/_GameUtils.lua:390-412`) a community enters `domes` only
   if it is in walking distance **and** `is_welcoming_community` (`:382-384`: `accept_colonists`,
   `ui_working`, `HasLifeSupport`). **`safety_dome` gets neither test**: it is the nearest
   `can_be_safety_dome` community with `CanVisit()` (not destroyed) and a non-negative distance.
   A switched-off dome qualifies; so does one that is not walkable.
3. `unit:GetExpeditionReturnDome(domes)` returns the held home only if it is in `domes`
   (C95, `Colonist.lua:5047-5059`). Otherwise `ChooseDome` (`_GameUtils.lua:486-501`) starts from
   `best_dome = safety_dome` and replaces it only with a listed dome scoring above
   `CommunityEvalNone` that `HasFreeLivingSpaceFor` the colonist.
4. `Colonist:ReturnFromExpedition` (`Colonist.lua:5076-5125`) ends in
   `SetCommand("TransportByFoot", dome)`. Nothing re-checks the destination.

⇒ With no welcoming community offering a free bed in walking range of the rocket, every returnee is
marched to the nearest dome by straight-line distance, switched off or not.

#### Why ours does not catch it

`Fix_ArrivalDeaths` repairs this same fallback for F53 (unreachable safety dome) and C83 (dead
safety dome), in a pre-wrapper on `Colonist:Idle` gated on `local rocket = self.arriving`
(`Code/Fix_ArrivalDeaths.lua:381-383`), because Idle is the only issuer of `"Arrive"`. An expedition
returnee is never `arriving`: the transporter issues `"ReturnFromExpedition"` with the dome already
chosen. **MEASURED:** `grep -n "ReturnFromExpedition\|UnloadPassengers" Code/Fix_ArrivalDeaths.lua`
hits one line, a comment about the disembark snap (`:129`), which is a different repair.

#### Reach

Any colony that runs an expedition while the nearest dome to the pad is switched off or lifeless and
no welcoming community with a free bed is walkable from the pad. An all-habitat colony beside a
mothballed dome (the reporter's) is the natural case: habitats are `can_be_safety_dome = false` and
hold 5. Steam, Paradox and console alike; nothing platform-specific is involved.

#### ⭐ The control

- **Desk:** drive `UnloadPassengers` with `domes = {}` and a `safety_dome` whose `ui_working` is
  false; the unit must receive `ReturnFromExpedition` with that dome. Falsified if any shipped code
  between `ChooseDome` and `TransportByFoot` rejects an unwelcoming destination.
- **Play:** one rocket, one switched-off dome nearest the pad, every other community out of walking
  range or full; send a crew on a short expedition and watch where the returnees walk.
  ⚠️ Vacuous if a welcoming community with a free bed is walkable from the pad.
- **Stale if** `GetDomesReachableByColonists` applies `is_welcoming_community` to `safety_dome`, or
  `ChooseDome` stops seeding `best_dome` with it.

#### Not opened

- **Which condition emptied the reporter's list.** Their habitats were either out of walking range of
  the pad (C95's walking-only rule) or failed `HasFreeLivingSpaceFor` (a habitat needs
  `GetFreeSpace() > 0`; whether a returnee's own held reservation counts as free was not read).
- Whether C83's repair shape transfers: a pre-wrapper on `Colonist:ReturnFromExpedition` would see
  the dome as an argument, but the reservation is already placed at `CargoTransporterNew.lua:1091-1093`. Not designed.
- The repair for a returnee with **no** welcoming destination at all. C83 preserves vanilla's
  assignment in that case; here vanilla's assignment is the lethal one.
- The reporter's game version, pack version and mod list. Not held.
