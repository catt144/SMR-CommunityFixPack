### F128 — OUR OWN DEFECT: the RC Driller's drill-hit sound is out of time with its hammer (P3) `[fixed 2026-09-28: Fix_SilentHitMomentFX.lua Driller moments set to the measured strikes; times heard in sync in game via a live swap; the module itself not yet booted]`

#### Must_Read_Header

A defect in our shipped `Fix_SilentHitMomentFX` (C74/C77, released in v7 on 2026-09-10), not in
vanilla: vanilla plays no drill-hit sound at all (C77, owner: "Confirmed no sound without it"). The
repair is a data change to that module's moment table. The strike times are an in-game
measurement on 1.1.1.405907 plus the owner's ear; source cannot give them, because the times live
only in the compressed animation file (C77's root cause).

#### Report

Steam comment by "Errors", 2026-09-19, relayed by the owner 2026-09-28 as a screenshot:
"Russias drill rovers don't play their 3-stage hammer sound correctly. only the first strike sound
effect, repeated out of tune with the animation."

#### Cause

- `Code/Fix_SilentHitMomentFX.lua` supplied `RoverRussiaDriller`/`workIdle` with `Hit` at 2708 and
  8125 ms of a 10833 ms loop, exactly 1/4 and 3/4 (SOURCE, commit `0cfc53a`). The C77 sitting
  (2026-09-10) proved only that the sound fires with the preset and not without it. The extractor
  hammer and MOXIE pump got the same 1/4-3/4 placement and were judged in sync by ear; the Driller's
  timing was never judged.
- The hit sound is one strike, not three. `Unit RoverDriller DrillHit` has two samples
  (`Data/SoundPreset.lua:19412-19423`, archived 1.1.1.405907); decoded from `Packs/Sounds.fpk`, each
  is about 4.3 s long with a single onset at 0.04 s and a steady decay (MEASURED 2026-09-28, ffmpeg
  plus an RMS envelope). So "3-stage" is the animation: the shipped FX key one moment name, `Hit`,
  for both the sound and the `RCDriller_Steam` particles (`ActionFXSound.lua:5568-5575`,
  `ActionFXParticles.lua`), and a three-strike hammer needs three `Hit` entries per loop.

#### Measurement (2026-09-28, owner attended)

A console SAMPLE line sampled the height of every spot on a drilling Driller every 20 ms through
two loops. No spot rides the hammer. The largest mover was `Autolight#0` (range 4 units), with a lift and a sharp drop three
times per loop, identical on both loops:

| Stroke | Lift | Drop | First sample at the low |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3238-3418 ms | 3418-3511 | 3511 |
| 2 | 6850-7031 | 7031-7171 | 7171 |
| 3 | 9811-10050 | 10050-10144 | 10144 |

The gaps are uneven (3660, 2973, 4200 ms). A TUNE line then rewrote
`Presets.AnimMetadata.RoverRussiaDriller.workIdle.Moments` in place to `Hit` at 3500, 7160 and
10130 (log line 342). The Driller was sent to a new deposit so its tracker restarted
(`Lua/Units/RCDriller.lua:101-105`), and the owner judged the result: "looks pretty good there".
Precision: samples 20 ms apart and heights in whole units, so each time is good to about ±40 ms.

Their output lines say `C121`, a provisional label used before this entry was filed; it is
this defect. The two console lines, each [RAN 2026-09-28, log f128_Mars.exe-20260928-13.15.51], stub-run under

#### Fix

`Code/Fix_SilentHitMomentFX.lua`: the Driller entry now lists three `Hit` moments at 3500, 7160
and 10130 ms, sorted by time as `IterateMoments` requires (C77). Nothing else changes. Existing
saves: a Driller drilling at load keeps its old tracker until its next job (module header), so
the new timing starts with the next deposit.

Desk: `python tools/desk_c74_hit_moment_fx.py` 10/10 after the change (it does not assert the
Driller times); bodycheck for the module unchanged (10 OK, 1 SRC-NONE). A boot with the updated
module and a listen would promote this to `tested-attended`.

#### Open

- `Packs/Animations.fpk` also ships a `RoverFlyingRussiaDriller` animation set, including
  `workIdle`. The module has no moments for it, so a Driller that uses that entity plays no hit
  sound. Whether a player can reach that entity is unasked; the owner was asked on 2026-09-28. The
  sampled Driller reported `entity=RoverRussiaDriller`.

#### Control that would falsify this

A Driller on the updated module, freshly sent to a deposit, whose hit sound lands off the three
hammer strikes at 1x speed. Any other `workIdle` duration than 10833 ms would also void the
times.
