### C100 — A jobless habitat resident emigrates to a dome offering an apartment and a job; the habitat's own isolation is what makes them jobless (P3) `[cand 2026-09-16: mechanism SOURCE-VERIFIED, NARROWED by measurement (EF-107) to habitats with no free listed slot, NOT reproduced as a controlled test; ⚖️ defect-or-design is UNRULED and the owner's]`

⛔ **NOT A REOPENING OF [C95](C95.md).** C95's repair shape is ruled and scoped to the expedition
draft. This is a **second, continuous drain by a different route**, and recording it does not widen
that fix. ⚠️ C95's line *"a habitat is drained one resident per expedition"* is, on this reading,
only half the story — but the fix it names still does exactly what the owner ruled.

⚖️ **UNRULED, AND DELIBERATELY SO: is this a defect at all?** The owner's reading is that habitat
residents are not supposed to want to leave. Against that, habitat residency is **explicitly
score-gated and re-evaluated** — `MicroGHabitatAutoResolve:IsSuitable` is `GetScoreFor(colonist) > 0`
(`MicroGHabitat.lua:173-175`), so the design does contemplate people moving out. ⛔ **Do not author a
repair, and do not classify this, until the owner rules.**

#### ⭐ MEASURED 2026-09-16 — the drain is NARROW: a habitat built beside a dome pins its residents

**Owner at the keyboard**, game 1.1.0.403908 build `6a91a190`, repo `60763b9`, console probes on
`NaturalistHabitat(2735)` (5/5, all "Unemployed") beside `DomeMega`. Mechanism, log line numbers
and controls are in **[EF-107](../facts/EF-107.md)**; not repeated here. What it does to this entry:

- **The owner's control contradicted this entry's prediction, and the game agrees with the owner.**
  Five jobless residents, a walkable dome with free jobs and free Smart Apartments, and
  `FindEmigrationDome` returned nothing for all five. Adding many more jobs and high-comfort homes
  to the dome changed nothing: the scorer reads neither comfort nor counts.
- **Why:** the habitat copied 8 of `DomeMega`'s interior workplaces onto its own list when it was
  built. The scorer counts a free slot in any of them as work at home (+10), which ties the dome at
  110; the job picker refuses every one of them. With all listed slots switched off the scorer
  flipped to `work=false weval=0 best_all=3`. ⇒ **the "stay side" row of the table below is wrong as
  written** and is corrected in place.
- ⇒ **This candidate applies only to a habitat whose list offers no free slot**: one built out of
  range of every dome, or before any workplace stood within 20 hexes, or whose listed buildings are
  all full or off. Dermot's habitat (a ≈2 h train ride from anything) fits; the owner's does not.
- ⚠️ **An alternative reading of Dermot's second departure, INFERRED, untested:** the picker refuses
  dome jobs only while `residence` is a habitat, so a resident displaced by an expedition can be
  hired in a dome, and the scorer counts a colonist's own workplace
  (`Workforce.lua:83-113`), which pulls them back to that dome after they re-enter the habitat.
  On that reading his departure is a C95 after-effect that C95's fix already prevents.
- ⛔ **A repair trap for whoever rules this:** adding the missing dome guard to the habitat's sweep
  removes the anchor and drains every dome-adjacent habitat. See EF-107's last bullet.

#### The mechanism, SOURCE-VERIFIED on 1.1.0.403908

`Colonist:TryToEmigrate` runs for every colonist on the heavy-update tick (`Colonist.lua:2349`,
gated by `next_heavy_update`), so this is **continuous**, not event-driven. It calls
`FindEmigrationDome` (`:3497`) → `GetBestReachableCommunities` (`:3425`).

That function builds a **stay-score** for the colonist's current community and then walks every
reachable one. The acceptance test is the part that matters:

```lua
local better_work = not work_available and new_work_available
local better_home_work = better_home or (need_work and better_work)
local better_eval = beat_threshold and new_eval >= eval or new_eval > eval
if better_eval or better_home_work then
```

with `need_work = can_work and not IsValid(self.workplace) and not self.user_forced_workplace`.

⭐ **`better_home_work` is an OVERRIDE, not a tiebreaker.** A destination needs only
`new_eval >= eval` (the outer guard) plus a free job the current community does not offer. **No
score margin is required.**

⇒ For a habitat resident:

| term | value, and why |
|---|---|
| `need_work` | **true** whenever they hold no workplace |
| `work_available` (stay side) | ⚠️ **CORRECTED 2026-09-16 (EF-107, measured):** **true** whenever any building on the habitat's own workplace list is on, has a free slot and passes `CanWorkHere` — and that list holds every workplace within 20 hexes at build time, **a neighbouring dome's interior included**. The `allow_work_in_connected = false` / `IsSuitableWorkplace` bar (`EF-103`, `Workplace.lua:1446-1462`) governs the job PICKER only; the scorer never consults it. As first written this row said "false unless a free outside workplace is in range", which was inferred and is wrong |
| `new_work_available` (dome side) | **true** for any dome with a free job |
| `new_home_available` | **true** when that dome has a free apartment |

⇒ ⭐⭐ **The habitat's defining rule is the thing that supplies the trigger.** It forbids connected
work in order to keep its residents apart from the colony; that keeps them jobless; and joblessness
is precisely what the emigration scorer treats as grounds to move them. ⚠️ **Whether that is an
oversight or the intended cost of the building is the question above, and it is not answered here.**

#### ⭐ Control — what would falsify this, in play

⛔ These are discriminators, not confirmations. Run them before treating the mechanism as live.

| observation | verdict |
|---|---|
| a habitat resident **with** a job (an outside workplace near the habitat) stays put, while a jobless one leaves | ⭐ the mechanism holds: `need_work` is the driver |
| a **working** habitat resident also leaves | ⛔ **refuted** — `better_work` is not what moved them; look at `better_eval` (the score margin) instead, and this entry's headline is wrong |
| a jobless resident stays while the only reachable dome has an apartment but **no free job** | ⭐ consistent: `better_work` needs a free job at the destination |
| a jobless resident leaves for a dome with **no** free apartment | ⛔ refuted — `new_home_available` gates the branch, so something else is moving them |

**Desk falsifier.** `GetBestReachableCommunities` (`Colonist.lua:3425-3494`) must still contain the
`better_home_work = better_home or (need_work and better_work)` short-circuit. **Stale if** a patch
makes the branch require a score margin, or gives `MicroGHabitatBase` its own override.

⚠️ **A fixture note that decides the test:** the habitat must be reachable from the dome for the
colonist to have come back at all, and the test colony must have a free apartment **and** a free job
in that dome — the owner's did. ⛔ A forced setup that intersects the mechanism (mass employment
reassignment) invalidates the reading; see `EF-104`.
