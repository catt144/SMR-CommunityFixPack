### C118 — Prosperity for Mars's store task hides a 10-free-hex requirement in its success check (P3) `[cand 2026-09-25: source-verified on 1.1.1.405907; not run in game]`

#### Must_Read_Header

Candidate, not built. Source-verified on the archived 1.1.1.405907 tree; nothing here was run in the
game. Before any build, the owner decides whether "keep 10 hexes free" is intended Prosperity for
Mars flavour (see Open question). No pack module touches faction tasks.

#### Report

Steam, Surviving Mars: Relaunched General Discussions, 2026-09-25, "Build a 'Store service
building'": a player built an Electronics Store for an unhappy faction's opportunity and it did not
complete. A reply claimed "None of the stores qualify". That claim is wrong: see Source. The faction
was not named, so this entry does not claim that the reporter hit this defect.

#### Source (archived 1.1.1.405907)

- **What counts as a store.** All four "Build a Store service building in any Dome" tasks (New Sol,
  Workers Party, Heritage Trust, Prosperity for Mars) test
  `dome:AreServiceBuildingsAvailable("ShopsElectronics", false)` (`Lua/Buildings/Dome.lua:4344`). It
  scans the dome's cluster (`GetClusterDomes`, passage-connected domes) for a service whose
  `GetServiceCategory()` is `ShopsElectronics` and that `WillWorkAnyShift()`
  (`Lua/ServiceBase.lua:98`, `Lua/Buildings/Service.lua:319`). The Electronics Store has that class.
  The Art Store, the small Art Store and the MegaMall map to it through `same_category_as`
  (`Data/BuildingTemplate/ShopsJewelry.lua:52`, `ShopsJewelry_Small.lua:40`, `MegaMall.lua:54`,
  carried into their generated classes).
- **The defect.** `PfMBuildServiceLuxury` (`Data/FactionDef/ProsperityForMars.lua:362-391`):
  `SuccessCondition` is store access **and** `GetFreeBuildableHexes() >= 10` (`Dome.lua:4497`;
  `ScriptCheckDomeFreeBuildableHexes` defaults to `>= 10`). `DomeFilter` is "no store access".
- **Intent tells.**
  - Of the 67 `FactionTaskDome` definitions whose conditions call `GetFreeBuildableHexes`, 66 do it
    in `DomeFilter`, which decides where a task is offered. This task is the only one that does it
    in `SuccessCondition`. (Counted with a Python regex over `Data/FactionDef/*.lua` and
    `DLC/**/FactionDef/*.lua` on the archive, splitting each block at `'SuccessCondition'` and
    `'DomeFilter'`.)
  - The sibling task directly above, `PfMBuildServiceGambling` (:322-361), succeeds on "has a
    CasinoComplex" and filters on "no casino **and** ≥ 10 free hexes".
  - The other three store tasks succeed on store access alone.
  - The task text never mentions space.
- **Longstanding.** 1.0.7.396349 had the same shape as "Build a Luxury service building in
  `<target.DisplayName>`", with `interestLuxury` and `>= 10`. 1.1.0.403908 has it too, with the text
  "in `<target.DisplayName>`".
- **What 1.1.1 changed** (`Lua/Factions/FactionDef.lua`):
  - The task is no longer tied to one target dome. `GenerateQuest` (:1254) records
    `GetDomeCompletionState` (:1222) for every dome.
  - `IsDomeFulfilling` (:1229) counts a dome only if it did **not** pass the success condition when
    the task was issued and passes it now. `EvalQuestSuccess` (:1238) checks every dome.
  - Domes built later get their state recorded at `OnMsg.BuildingInit` (:1311).
  - In-flight quests are migrated by `SavegameFixups.ZZZ_FactionTaskDomeCompletableInAnyDome`
    (:1322).
- **The camera points at the wrong domes.** Clicking the notification cycles through
  `GetCandidateDomes` (:1273; `Lua/Factions/Legislature.lua:1043`). Those are domes that did not pass
  at issue time and do pass the filter, meaning domes with no store access. Cramped ones are included.

#### Harm

Two effects, both from the misplaced check:

1. **A store built in a busy dome never counts.** If building the store leaves the dome with fewer
   than 10 free buildable hexes, the task cannot complete there. The notification camera sends the
   player to exactly those domes.
2. **New in 1.1.1: completion with no store built.** A dome that already had store access but fewer
   than 10 free hexes at issue time is recorded as not passing. Freeing space later (demolishing
   anything) completes the task on the spot.

The same definitions feed two paths (`Legislature.lua:1166`, `:1374`):

| Path | Duration | Stakes |
|---|---|---|
| Unhappy-faction opportunity (`FactionTask`) | 5 sols (:1333) | Success grants +0.6 approval for 15 sols (:1335). A timeout counts toward `FactionTaskTimedOutLimit` = 2 (:1337), after which the task is never offered again. |
| Vote-time promise (`FactionQuest`) | 10 sols (:990) | Failure costs −1.5 approval, "Did not uphold a faction promise" (:993, `FactionQuest:Eval` :1104), against +0.1 for success. |

The promise path is the costly one: a player who promises a store to win Prosperity for Mars votes,
then builds it in a busy dome, is punished for doing what the text said. Effect 2 favours the player
and is a correctness issue, not harm.

**Game phase:** mid-to-late colonies, where domes fill up and politics runs. Early domes usually
have room. The regime is dense established domes.

#### Reach

R1 on every platform with politics on. Prosperity for Mars is a base-game faction
(`Data/FactionDef/`). The task is offered once the faction is unhappy or a vote is negotiated, and
at least one dome lacks store access. Politics-off rules skip it.

#### Control

- **Desk:** evaluate the shipped `SuccessCondition` against stub domes: store access with 9 free
  hexes (expected false; the claim holds) and with 10 (true). Then run the issue-then-free sequence
  through `GenerateQuest`, `IsDomeFulfilling` and `EvalQuestSuccess` loaded from the archive: a dome
  with store access and 5 free hexes at issue, then 10, must read fulfilled. The claim is refuted if
  the shipped eval ignores free hexes, or if a store-access dome recorded `false` cannot fulfil later.
- **In game (pack off), if wanted:** give Prosperity for Mars the task by console while a dome
  without store access has fewer than 13 free hexes. Build an Electronics Store there, staffed and
  powered, and read `EvalQuestSuccess`: expected `false`. Then demolish a building to free 10 hexes:
  expected `true`.

#### Fix shape (not approved; owner's question first)

A load-time data correction on `FactionDefs.ProsperityForMars`, matching the 66 other tasks. Move
the free-hex test from `SuccessCondition` into `DomeFilter` (offer only where there is room), and
make success "store access" like the other three store tasks. Rebuild both `eval` functions, not
only the editor subobjects: the runtime calls `.eval`.

- **§3a:** there is nothing to persist; the presets are rebuilt at every load.
- **In-flight quests:** their `dome_states` were recorded under the old condition. Under the new
  one, a recorded store-access dome that failed on space would pass at once (effect 2 again).
  Decide whether to re-record the states of pending quests for this task id at load, or accept a
  one-time early completion. Report the choice.

#### Open question for the owner

Could "keep 10 hexes free" be intended Prosperity for Mars flavour, a store in a dome with room to
grow? Every intent tell says it was meant as the offer filter. But this is a threshold, and the
filing rules leave the question of what it protects to you.

#### Not opened

- Each dome type's total buildable area. A small dome that cannot host a store and still keep 10
  hexes free would make the task impossible there by construction, which would widen the reach.
- Whether a construction site counts as occupying a hex for `GetFreeBuildableHexes` (it reads
  `object_hex_grid:GetBuildingNoDome`).
- The cluster-wide store access that all four store tasks share. Connecting a passage to a dome
  that has a store completes the task with no store built. That is shared design and is not
  claimed here.
