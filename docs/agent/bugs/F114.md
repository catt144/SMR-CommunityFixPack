### F114 — trains do not move between stations with the pack loaded on 1.1.0 (field report; cause PINNED 16:25 to `Fix_TrainCargoDumping`'s `UnloadAll` body copy) (P1, measured — filed)  `[filed 2026-09-08: reporter A/B; confirmed live same day; gate landed same day (ck106), boot read owed]`

**⭐ THE REPORT, VERBATIM** (Steam comment, 2026-09-08, reporter *Ranger
Dimitri*) — recorded word-for-word because a paraphrase would smuggle in a
diagnosis:

> "Think something broke in the update when it came to the Train's with this mod.
> Using this mod cause them to not move between stations. When I turn it off they
> work as normal."

**What the report is worth.** The reporter ran the control themselves: pack on ⇒
trains stall, pack off ⇒ normal. That is a **player-run A/B naming our mod as the
difference**, which is the strongest evidence class the project accepts short of
its own repro. ⛔ What it is **not** is a module-level bisection — the pack ships
80 **default-active** modules and `SMRFixPack.OptionEnabled` gates only *optional*
ones (`Code/00_Core.lua:54-58`; `doccheck`: "0 optional-gated files"). So the
reporter **cannot** narrow this and must not be asked to.

⛔ **NO CAUSE IS CLAIMED HERE.** The candidate set below is a search space, not a
diagnosis. The project has twice had a stated root cause overturned by the owner
for exactly this reason.

**⚠️ WHY THIS ENTRY MATTERS BEYOND ITS OWN REPAIR.** Every desk instrument the
2026-09-08 sweeps built came back **CLEAN on every train and track module**:

- `EF-076` existence sweep — all their declared targets resolve.
- The call-site sweep — no dead global, const or method call in any of them.
- `Fix_TrainMinors`' `recompute_max_vehicles` was suspected and **cleared**: its
  formula is byte-identical to 1.1.0's own (`Lua/Buildings/Track.lua:65`).
- `Fix_TrackConnectorPingPong`'s body was suspected and **cleared as a copy**: it
  matches 1.1.0's `TrackConnectedObjBase:CreateConnectorElements`
  (`Lua/TrainTransport.lua`) line for line apart from its own F66 guard.

**A player found a breakage that every one of our sweeps passed.** That is the
limit those sweeps always had — they measure *existence* and *call validity*, never
*behaviour* — now demonstrated rather than argued. Treat "the sweeps are clean"
as meaning nothing about correctness from here on.

**Candidate set (search space, unranked, NOT a claim).** The pack's train/track
modules, all of which apply on 1.1.0:
`Fix_TrainMinors` · `Fix_TrackConnectorPingPong` · `Fix_TrackTunnelPowerBridge` ·
`Fix_TrackSalvageWipe` · `Fix_TrackSalvageRefund` · `Fix_TrainCargoDumping` ·
`Fix_TrainWaitTime` · `Fix_TrainsToVoid` · `Fix_BrokenTrackSalvage` ·
`Fix_TrainPlatformWedge` · `Fix_DestroyedTunnels`.

Two 1.1.0 changes intersect this surface and are worth carrying into the
investigation **as context, not as suspects**:
- The patch note "**Train Stations now connect to Domes that have at least one
  entrance inside the Station's work radius**" — station connection logic moved.
- `TrackBase:GameInit` ends with `self:Notify("TryConnectStations")`
  (`Track.lua:62-67`), so station connection is re-run at track init.
- ⚠️ One asymmetry worth a look because it changes *whether a connector exists*:
  our F66 guard declines to take a hex owned by a live other station, and in that
  branch `el` stays valid, so the `if not IsValid(el)` creation block is skipped
  and **that station gets no connector element at that spot**. Vanilla always
  destroys and recreates. Whether 1.1.0 reaches that branch more often is
  **unmeasured** — this is the first thing to instrument, not the answer.

**What would actually narrow it, in order of value:**
1. **The reporter's log.** The pack writes a `[CommunityFixPack]` boot block
   listing every fix applied plus any self-disable reason, and `EF-065`(a) puts a
   blame line on any throw under a wrapped target. One log tells us whether
   anything is raising at all. ⛔ Ask via the tracker route
   (`api.github.com/.../issues/<n>/comments`), never the rendered page.
2. **Their save + mod list.** `EF-051`'s falsifier applies; and both-mods-loaded
   is our rig's normal config, so a second mod is not excluded.
3. **Our own repro on 1.1.0** — build two stations and a track, run the pack on
   and off. This is cheap and does not need the reporter.
4. **A bisect harness.** Because there is no player toggle, narrowing means an
   agent-side harness that disables train/track modules in groups. ⚠️ `H-08`:
   never do this by pulling the junction.

⛔ **Do not ship a speculative repair.** ⛔ Do not tell the reporter a cause. The
honest reply is that we have reproduced nothing yet and are asking for a log.

### 2026-09-08 (later, SAFETY_FIRST_FIXES session) — a SOURCE-PINNED CANDIDATE, runtime-unconfirmed: `Fix_TrainCargoDumping`'s body copy of `Train:UnloadAll`

⚠️ **Status of this section: a candidate with a mechanism and a 10-second
control, NOT a pinned cause.** No repro has run; no log has shown the throw.
Everything below is read from the shipped 1.1.0 Lua and from our own module.
It outranks the F66-guard thread above because every link is a cited line
rather than an "unmeasured" frequency question.

**The module.** `Fix_TrainCargoDumping` (F46) is a **full body replacement** of
`Train:UnloadAll` copied from 1.0.7 (`Code/Fix_TrainCargoDumping.lua:76-104`),
the same class of module as [[F113]]. Its `Require` list survives 1.1.0 and the
first-launch log confirms `TrainCargoDumping: applied` (`EF-078`).

**What 1.1.0 changed in the function we replace.** 1.1.0's own
`Train:UnloadAll` (`Lua/Units/Train.lua:779-805`) gained two nil-guards our
copy does not have:

```lua
-- 1.1.0, :785-787                      -- ours, :82
if dest.demand and dest.demand[res] then   RequestUnassignUnit(dest.demand[res], self, amount, false)
    RequestUnassignUnit(dest.demand[res], self, amount, false)
end
-- 1.1.0, :794-795                      -- ours, :89
local demand = station.demand and station.demand[res]   local station_cap = station.demand[res]:GetTargetAmount()
local station_cap = demand and demand:GetTargetAmount() or 0
```

(plus a `BlackCube` bookkeeping call at `:800-802` that our copy also lacks —
a silent revert of the F113 kind, minor here).

**Why `station.demand[res]` can be nil on 1.1.0 for a `res` that IS in
`station.storable_resources` — every link cited:**
1. `Station` no longer inherits `UniversalStorageDepotBase`; its depot parent is
   the new **`MultiResourceDepotBase`** (`Lua/Buildings/Station.lua:48-56`,
   `Lua/Buildings/MultiResourceDepot.lua:8-9`).
2. `Station:Init` fills `storable_resources` with **every** transportable
   resource (`Station.lua:110-111`, `table.copy(TransportableResourceIds)`),
   removing only `Seeds` under the `NoTerraforming` rule (`:112-116`).
3. Demand requests are created per resource by
   `MultiResourceCubeVisuals:RegisterResourceRequest`
   (`Lua/Buildings/MultiResourceCubeVisuals.lua:372-392`), which **returns
   before creating any request when the resource's lock state is not
   "enabled"** (`:378`, dev comment: *"Keep the bookkeeping but create no
   requests while the resource isn't enabled"*). The same gate is in
   `RecalculateAfterResourceListChange` (`MultiResourceDepot.lua:386-387`), and
   `ReevaluateResourceLockState` (`:414-467`) **removes** `self.demand[resource]`
   when a resource becomes locked while leaving it in `storable_resources`.
4. Resources shipped with **`LockState = "hidden"`**: `BlackCube`
   (`Data/Resource.lua:32`), `MysteryResource` (`:49`), `Seeds` (`:309`). A
   preset whose `LockState ~= "enabled"` gets that state applied at new-game
   reset (`CommonLua/Features/LockablePreset.lua:402-410`). ⇒ On an ordinary
   1.1.0 colony (no Black Cubes mystery, Seeds not yet unlocked) **every
   station lists `BlackCube` and `Seeds` as storable with `demand[res] == nil`.**
   1.1.0's own `UnloadAll` handling `res == "BlackCube"` inside the
   `storable_resources` loop (`Train.lua:800`) is vanilla's own statement that
   BlackCube reaches that loop.

**What our copy would then do.** `station.demand["BlackCube"]:GetTargetAmount()`
at `Code/Fix_TrainCargoDumping.lua:89` ⇒ **`attempt to index a nil value`** on
the first unload at any station.

**Why that reads as "trains do not move between stations."** `UnloadAll` runs
from `Train:TransferCargo` (`Train.lua:846`, `:992`), which `Train:LoadTrain`
calls on every service cycle (`:254`, `:260`) — i.e. before the train ever
decides where to go next. A command function that raises ends the command
(`CommonLua/Classes/CommandObject.lua:250-277`, `sprocall` → falls to `Idle`),
so the train never reaches `SetCommand("GotoStation", …)` (`:287`).
`OnMsg.NewHour` restarts idle trains (`Train.lua:58-71`) ⇒ the same throw every
hour, forever, and the train sits at its platform. With the pack off, vanilla's
guarded body runs and the train moves. That is the reporter's A/B exactly —
but it is a *prediction*, and the two below are what make it a finding or not.

**The two controls, either takes seconds — run order matters:**
- **0. Disarm any self-check OVERRIDE first.** The rig is gaining a diagnostic
  force surface (`SMRFixPack_Force` in `00_Core.lua`, armed from the TestKit's
  `Code/97_ForceInactive.lua`, sitting of 2026-09-08) that makes inactive
  modules install anyway. A forced boot changes what the pack does, so it
  **cannot** answer "does the shipped pack stop the trains" — comment the
  arming line out of the TestKit `metadata.lua` code list and restart before
  either control below, or the result is uninterpretable. (The console control
  itself reads vanilla state and is unaffected; the LOG control is not.)
- **Console, any 1.1.0 colony, a station selected** (reads vanilla state, pack
  on or off):
  `local st = SelectedObj; for _, r in ipairs(st.storable_resources) do if not st.demand[r] then print("no demand:", r) end end`
  — predicts `BlackCube` (and `Seeds`). **Prints nothing ⇒ candidate dead.**
- **Log, pack on, after a train reaches a station:** a `[LUA ERROR]` /
  `attempt to index a nil value` line citing `Fix_TrainCargoDumping.lua:89`,
  repeating hourly. `EF-065`(a) may add a blame line. **The reporter's log would
  carry this same signature**, which is what the draft reply below asks for.

**What this does NOT settle.** Whether the reporter's colony is in this state;
whether anything ELSE also stalls trains (`Fix_TrackSalvageWipe`'s
`DemolishAndSplitTrack` and `Fix_TrackSalvageRefund`'s `GetRefundResources` are
the other two full body copies among the 11 and were **not** diffed against
1.1.0 in this pass — demolish-path, not movement-path, but unchecked). The F66
guard thread above stays open, demoted.

**The repair shape, if the control confirms it — for the owner, decision
item 106, NOT taken here:** gate the module so it declines on 1.1.0 (the
[[F113]] FF-1 shape). The cleanest discriminator is the class change itself:
`{ test = function() return rawget(_G, "MultiResourceDepotBase") == nil end,
reason = "Station's depot base changed to MultiResourceDepotBase (game update) — the 1.0.7 UnloadAll copy no longer matches" }`
— 1.0.7 had no such class (our own F46 header cites `UniversalStorageDepotBase`
as the station's depot parent). Repairing the copy in place (adding the two
guards) would keep a 1.0.7 body over a rewritten function, the [[F113]] lesson.
⛔ Neither is done in this session: the brief forbids a train repair without a
control, and that is right.

### ✅ 2026-09-08 16:25 — CONFIRMED LIVE, shipped configuration, owner-attended: the candidate above is the mechanism

**The witness.** Owner's fresh 1.1.0 colony (NASA, BlankBig_02, cheat-provisioned),
**shipped configuration** (0 force-override lines in the log), two stations,
one track, one train assigned. Owner report: *"First train not moving at all"*,
the second train cannot be assigned, the station shows **"Track busy"**. Log:
`Mars.exe-20260908-15.57.09-6a91a190.log` (the unforced-boot log, same session),
manual flush by the owner at ~16:25.

**The throw, verbatim, repeating every 6 s of game time (at least 30 by
16:26, first at Lua `0:25:42` — a FLOOR read off a live file, still accumulating;
the post-exit sweep owns the total):**

```
[LUA ERROR] Mod/SMR_CommunityFixPack/Code/Fix_TrainCargoDumping.lua:89: attempt to index a nil value (field '?')
  Mod/SMR_CommunityFixPack/Code/Fix_TrainCargoDumping.lua(89):  method UnloadAll
  Lua/Units/Train.lua(846):  method TransferCargo
  Lua/Units/Train.lua(260):   <>
  [C](-1):  global sprocall
  CommonLua/Classes/CommandObject.lua(250):   <>
Locals:
  self    | object Train at (127507, 171748, 9997) on map 1
  station | object StationSmall at (128500, 170602, 9997) on map 1
  (for state) | table #21          -- station.storable_resources, 21 entries
  res     | string BlackCube
  carried | number 0
```

Every link predicted above is in the locals: the loop is over 21 storable
resources, `res` is **`BlackCube`** (lock-hidden, not shown in the station's
own UI, which lists 20), and the index that raises is `station.demand[res]`
because the 1.1.0 depot base never registered a demand for it. The stack is
exactly `LoadTrain → TransferCargo → UnloadAll` (`Train.lua:260`, `:846`);
`sprocall` at `CommandObject.lua:250` ends the command, the train idles and
re-enters `LoadTrain`, and the cycle repeats — hence the 6-second cadence and
a train that never moves.

**"Track busy" is a consequence, not a second defect.**
`Station:CanInteractWithObject` (`Station.lua:852-854`) refuses a new train when
`self.track_busy[platform]` is set — the stalled train still occupies the spawn
platform it was placed on, so the second train cannot be added. Removing the
stall removes this.

**What this settles and what it does not.**
- ✅ The reporter's "trains do not move between stations with the pack on" has a
  pinned mechanism in our code, reproduced on demand on a fresh 1.1.0 colony.
- ✅ Vanilla-side control is implicit in the source: 1.1.0's own `UnloadAll`
  guards this exact index (`Train.lua:794-795`), so with the pack off the body
  that runs cannot raise here. The explicit pack-off A/B has not been *watched*
  yet; it is cheap and would make this `tested-attended` rather than
  mechanism-grade.
- ⚠️ Whether any OTHER module also affects trains is NOT excluded by this — one
  cause is pinned, not the only possible one. The other track body copies were
  diffed later the same day by the trains sitting: `Fix_TrackSalvageRefund`
  **cleared** (line-for-line bar its own fix); `Fix_TrackSalvageWipe` is
  [[F116]] (source-read, silent by construction, 1.1.0 added a `ProcessAllElements`
  re-stamp and an orphan safeguard our copy strips — control owed, run it AFTER
  the F114/F115 gates). ⛔ 17 of the pack's 22 full-body replacements are still
  undiffed against 1.1.0, and no instrument we own sees a same-arity body change.
- ⚠️ Also live in the same log: 2 × `F115` (`Fix_LandscapeUnitFilter.lua:63`,
  `Landscapes` nil, from a clear-waste-rock construction site) — the sibling
  session's finding, recorded there.

**Repair, still NOT shipped — decision 106 (owner).** With the control in hand
the recommended shape is the gate from the section above: a `Require` `test`
that declines when `MultiResourceDepotBase` exists, so 1.1.0 players get
vanilla's rewritten, guarded `UnloadAll` back and 1.0.7 players keep F46. A
guard-in-place repair (adding the two nil checks to our copy) would keep a

instead of `task_requests` removal, `MultiResourceDepot.lua:251-290`).
⛔ Code edit only with `Mars.exe` closed.

### ✅ 2026-09-08 (later) — GATE LANDED in `Code/` on the owner's go (decision 106), source-read + parse-swept, NOT yet booted

**Change:** `Code/Fix_TrainCargoDumping.lua` `Require` list gains a `test` spec

next boot: `TrainCargoDumping: inactive (the Station's depot base changed to
MultiResourceDepotBase (game update) — the 1.0.7 UnloadAll copy no longer
matches)`, **16 inactive / 13 named**, no `Fix_TrainCargoDumping.lua:89` line, and

assumed.

1.0.7 players are unaffected. Status stays `filed` until the boot read.
