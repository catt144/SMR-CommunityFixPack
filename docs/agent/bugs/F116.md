### F116 — `Fix_TrackSalvageWipe`'s 1.0.7 `DemolishAndSplitTrack` copy drops 1.1.0's node_idx revalidation AND its orphan safeguard (our own defect, patch-induced) (P1, source-read — filed)  `[filed 2026-09-08: source-read, NOT reproduced]`

- ⛔⛔ **THIS ENTRY IS UNDER CORRECTION (2026-09-08, by its own author). DO NOT
  ACT ON IT AS WRITTEN.** Two of its four claims do not survive re-reading the
  module, and the fix leg `prompts/F116_FIX_LEG.md` must re-derive all four
  before writing any code:
  * ⛔ **"our body never calls `ProcessAllElements` (0 hits in `Code/`)" — the
    GREP IS RIGHT, THE INFERENCE IS WRONG.** The copy calls the underlying
    `ProcessTrackElements(map, ...)` DIRECTLY at four sites near its tail
    (`Fix_TrackSalvageWipe.lua:276-289`). What is actually absent is only the
    **PRE-SORT** revalidation 1.1.0 added; element processing does happen.
  * ⛔ **"strips out the game's new orphan safeguard" — TOO STRONG.** The copy
    has its OWN deliberate, playtest-derived orphan policy (`:256-263`): it
    DELETES elements still carrying `track_obj == false` as unreachable debris,
    where 1.1.0's new loop REHOMES them into fresh tracks. That is a POLICY
    DIVERGENCE, not a missing guard — and the interesting consequence is that
    on 1.1.0 ours may now destroy track pieces vanilla would have saved.
  * Still believed real, but RE-DERIVE: the missing pre-sort revalidation, and
    `skip_track_process` not forwarded to `self.broken:Demolish`.
  ⚠️ **The method failure is the point:** this entry was filed off keyword
  presence (`grep -c ProcessAllElements`) and drew a conclusion the code does
  not support. Same shape as `EF-078`'s path-checked-by-last-segment and the
  name-sweep that missed `F114`. **Check the thing, not its label** — including
  when the thing is our own.
- ⛔ **STATUS: SOURCE-READ. NOT REPRODUCED, NOT SEEN IN ANY LOG.** Found by
  diffing our replacement against the shipped 1.1.0 body after `F114` and
  `F115` established the class. ⛔ Do not move status without a control.
- **The replacement.** `Code/Fix_TrackSalvageWipe.lua` declares itself a copy of
  1.0.7's `Lua\Buildings\TrackElement.lua:448-578`. The shipped 1.1.0 body is
  `TrackGridElement:DemolishAndSplitTrack(mass_delete, skip_track_process)` at
  `Lua/Buildings/TrackElement.lua:467`.
- ⭐ **FOUR THINGS 1.1.0 HAS THAT OUR COPY DOES NOT** (measured by keyword
  presence in the two extracted bodies, not by eye):
  1. **`track_obj:ProcessAllElements()` before the sort**, guarded by
     `if not skip_track_process`. 1.1.0 attaches the reason as a comment:
     *"make sure elements' node_idx is actually valid, since we're assuming it
     represents the distance from the start for the given element."*
     ⛔ **`ProcessAllElements` appears 0 times in the whole of `Code/`** — we
     never call it anywhere. It resolves to
     `ProcessTrackElements(ResolveMap(self), elements)` (`Track.lua:466-469`).
  2. **The orphan-reassign safeguard loop**, whose own comment reads
     *"safeguard: if ExpandTrackFromElement didn't reach an element for some
     reason, create a new track for it and expand from it until all elements
     are assigned."* Our body has the word `orphan` only inside an F44 COMMENT;
     the loop is absent.
  3. **Empty-track cleanup** — `DoneObject(track)` for any track left with no
     `elements` and no `elements_under_construction`.
  4. **`skip_track_process` forwarded** to `self.broken:Demolish(mass_delete,
     skip_track_process)`; ours calls `self.broken:Demolish(mass_delete)`.
- ⭐ **THE STING, AND THE REASON THIS IS P1 RATHER THAN A TIDY-UP.** This
  module's own F44 header defines the failure it repairs as: *"an element
  orphaned by an earlier corrupted split has `track_obj == false`, and the
  shipped body then raises on every click — the debris becomes immune to
  salvage."* 1.1.0's new safeguard loop rehomes precisely those orphans.
  ⇒ **our replacement strips out the game's new guard against the exact
  corruption our fix exists to repair.**
- ⚠️ **TWO BRANCHES FOR THE node_idx GAP, AND WHICH ONE BITES IS UNMEASURED.**
  Our body sorts `all_elements` by `node_idx` and the zone math downstream
  assumes sorted order == physical order.
  * **(a) node_idx unstamped** ⇒ our own F44/F45 guard (`type(el.node_idx) ~=
    "number"` ⇒ bare `return`) fires and the salvage **silently does nothing** —
    the player clicks demolish and nothing happens, with no log line.
  * **(b) node_idx present but STALE** ⇒ the guard passes (it is a number), the
    sort is by a stale index, and the zone math deletes a physically scattered
    set — the corrupted-track outcome F44/F45 were written to prevent.
  ⛔ Which occurs depends on whether 1.1.0 leaves `node_idx` stale at this
  point, which is exactly what `ProcessAllElements` was added to fix. **That
  1.1.0 added the call at all is evidence the value is NOT reliable there** —
  but it is evidence, not a measurement. NEEDS A CONTROL.
- **CONTROL, when a track exists** (cheap, and the rig now has track): salvage a
  MIDDLE element of a multi-element track with the pack on. Expected under (a):
  nothing happens, no log line. Under (b): the track splits wrongly or leaves
  unreachable debris. Pack off ⇒ vanilla splits correctly. ⛔ Run it AFTER the
  `F114`/`F115` gates land, or the train throw will confound the reading.
- **ROUTE.** Same shape as `F113`/`F114`/`F115`: gate the module so it declines
  on 1.1.0, restoring vanilla's (safer, now-guarded) body. ⛔ A repair means
  re-copying a 152-line body and re-applying two fixes to it — not safety-first
  work. ⭐ Note that vanilla 1.1.0 may itself now handle F44's orphan case via
  the safeguard, in which case this module may be **obsolete** rather than
  broken — check before repairing anything.
