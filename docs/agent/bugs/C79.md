### C79 — `SA_RevealTech` ignores exact scenario costs and gives every tech a 20% boost `[cand 2026-09-10: source-read; not reproduced]`

**DIFF-CAUSED. SOURCE contract break:** in 1.0.7,
`Research:ChangeResearchCost(tech_id, points)` validates the technology and
stores `status.cost = points` (`Lua/Research.lua:353-360`). In 1.1.0, the same
method ignores `points` and unconditionally calls `BoostTech(tech_id, 20)`
(`Lua/Research.lua:225-227`). The current helper is explicitly a percentage
reduction and accumulates a per-tech percent (`:245-257`); it is not an exact
research-point setter.

The consumer was not migrated. `SA_RevealTech:SAExec` still forwards
`self.cost` when it is not `-1` (old `Lua/Sequences/SA_Gameplay.lua:1187-1203`,
current `:1196-1214`). Shipped current scenarios retain very different exact
values: Crystalline Frequency Jamming 3,000
(`Data/Scenario/Mystery 10.lua:47-50`), Sol Exploration 5,000
(`Mystery 9.lua:101-104`), Bottomless Pit Research Center 10,000
(`BuriedWonder_Bottomless_Pit.lua:47-50`), and Wildfire Cure 90,000
(`Mystery 8.lua:112-115`). Replacing all four with the literal percentage 20
cannot preserve that authored contract.

**SOURCE ROUTE — R2:** play the corresponding base mystery or buried-wonder
sequence until its `SA_RevealTech` action reveals an undiscovered technology.
The action invokes the changed method directly. This is distinct from C76's
`DiscoverTech`/initiative-only helper route and needs no DLC.

**INFERRED player effect / severity:** P2. Scenario research can require a
materially different number of points than its authored event and balance
expect. The failure is silent; no throw, save damage, or exact observed cost is
claimed.

**Recipe:** start a fresh 1.1.0 fixture with Mystery 10, Mystery 9, Wildfire, or
the Bottomless Pit buried wonder; record the target tech's state and all prior
boosts; trigger the exact reveal action; immediately record displayed required
and remaining research points. The leg is vacuous if the tech was already
unlocked, `cost == -1`, another effect changes the cost/boost in the same step,
or the sequence does not reach this action.

**Falsifier:** another current consumer interprets the scenario's `cost` and
sets the exact authored points after this call, or the immediate in-game value
equals the authored number with no compensating boost/state mutation.

No fix is built. A repair must use the current research model without breaking
intentional percentage boosts or the MysteryTechRevealRemapping step.
