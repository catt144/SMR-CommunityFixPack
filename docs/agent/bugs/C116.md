### C116 — the hub marker outlives the colonist's stay on the hub (P2) `[fixed 2026-09-24 on hubset: desk verified; harm conditional; pending play]`

#### Must_Read_Header

Built on `hubset` at `76d1ed8`, pending attended play and the set audit. Hub audit S3, cleared by the Codex
[resweep](../reports/MIGRATION_CROSSCHECK_HUB_RESWEEP_2026-09-24.md). It ships only as part of the
hub release set below. Its repair shares [C114](C114.md)'s on-hub test, which hubset 04 derives from source.
The 07 sitting held the marker-kept predictions; the marker-clear path was not sampled. See the dated sitting section.

#### Source (archived 1.1.1.405907)

- `passage_hub` is set at `Lua/Passage.lua:1228` on a hub-side exit and cleared only at :1235 on a
  dome-side exit.
- Its readers trust validity, not position: `Unit.lua:468-470` (skips the outside update),
  `Colonist.lua:2963-2966`, `BuildingWayPoints.lua:541-547`, and C99's demolition guard
  (`Passage.lua:1149-1151`).
- So a colonist that is dumped on a hub, then walks off, rides away or dies, keeps the marker until
  its next dome-side passage exit.
- Corrected by the resweep: `Dome_Entrance.lua:71` sets outside regardless of the marker, so "a
  marked colonist never starts the timer" is too strong.

#### Harm

Two directions. A colonist outside with a stale marker is spared the outside update (shelter it
should not have). Stale markers within twice the hub radius keep [C99](C99.md)'s last-exit guard true,
so a hub's last passage waits to be salvaged. Neither harm is measured on its own.

#### Reach

Any interrupted crossing followed by a walk, ride or work elsewhere without another dome-side
passage exit. Measured: markers still set after watched colonists reached work (field findings
ON:661, 663); their physical position relative to the hub is not measured.

#### Control

- **Supports:** `c.passage_hub` valid, `c.outside_start == false`, colonist standing outside well
  away from the hub.
- **Refutes:** every marked colonist is physically on the hub or its ramps.
- **Measured with the hub footprint (handoff item 6, 2026-09-24 sitting — see dated section
  below):** marker readings across two passes on a live save. Departure, dome entry and work are
  covered by the snapshots; death and reload are not — those still want link 07's attended play.

#### Fix shape (owner approved fixing the set, 2026-09-24)

Clear the marker on verified departure from the physical hub or passage, then run the native
outside recomputation. Prefer a synchronous lifecycle hook to a polling thread. Never clear it on
every `ExitHolder`: the native comment protects a real dump onto the hub. Clearing shelter from a
colonist standing on a real ramp falsifies the fix. A load-time cleanup is a separate question.

#### Release (owner ruling, 2026-09-24)

Ships in one release with the rest of the hub set (C114, C115, C116, C117, C42, C111, F127 and the P3
pins in `Fix_VacuumWalks`; F127 and P3 added by the owner the same day), never alone.
The owner's words: "If possible and we are going to fix them all I would like to do the fixes and
get them out all at once. They all touch surfaces near the same thing so if we push one part people
read that as fixed, and then if they hit the surface of one of the other parts then they say 'well
you said it was fixed but X is still borken'".

  `passage_hub`), archived at
  [`docs/archive/logs/hubset04_footprint_Mars.exe-20260924-15.34.30-6aad2d75.log`](../../archive/logs/hubset04_footprint_Mars.exe-20260924-15.34.30-6aad2d75.log).
  - Pass 1 (line 482): 74 marked, by position: 3 DomeInterior, 5 dome, 1 hub1908, 1 hub2026, 7
    hub2692, 3 hub4113, 49 passage. Reconciliation: 74 `SMRMARKU` lines match the 74 total.
  - Pass 2 (line 779): 133 marked: 4 DomeInterior, 4 PassageRamp, 10 dome, 1 hub1908, 9 hub2692,
    1 hub4113, 104 passage. 133 `SMRMARKU` lines match the 133 total.
  - Every dome/DomeInterior row in both passes read `outside false`, at distances of roughly
    14000-19000 (the same scale as the hub-footprint distances above, i.e. genuinely far from the
    hub) — 8/74 (10.8%) in pass 1, 14/133 (10.5%) in pass 2, a steady rate across the two readings.
  - No `SMRMARKU` row, in either pass, read `none`. Every marked colonist was inside a dome
    (dome/DomeInterior), on a hub/passage/PassageRamp hex, or on its own marked hub's hex.
- **Verdict: reach confirmed, harm still conditional.** The marker does outlive the colonist's
  physical stay on the hub at a real, steady rate (~11% of the marked population, both passes) —
  these colonists are inside a dome, working or resting, while still carrying a hub marker from a
  crossing they finished. That is the "Reach" claim in this entry, now measured rather than
  inferred from a single field-findings snapshot. But since every one of them is inside a dome (not
  in vacuum), none demonstrates the "Harm" combination (a marker suppressing the outside-timer
  update on a colonist who is actually exposed): that population — marked, `outside false`, and
  physically outside on open ground rather than inside a dome — was not observed in either pass.
  This does not refute the harm hypothesis; passage hexes are pressurized by design (so `outside
  false` there is expected regardless of marker validity), and open-ground exposure while marked is
  plausibly rarer than indoor drift. It means the harm side still wants link 07's attended
  observation, ideally around a demolition or a colonist that leaves the hub on foot rather than
  through a dome-side passage exit.
- **Drift instance (routed to 99):** the prompt's instruction to rerun the anchor line "when a
  colonist's status shows 'Moving to a new Dome: Brussels'" was operationally infeasible as written
  — that status is a per-colonist info-panel label with no notification, filter or log surface, so
  catching it by eye across ~867 colonists is not a realistic manual task. It also turned out to be
  unnecessary: `Colonist.lua:4647-4649` shows that status string is generated whenever `cmd` is
  `Transport`, `TransportByFoot` or `MigrateStep`, which is exactly the population the anchor query
  already selects (`t.dest_dome == c.dome`) every time it runs — both passes already captured every
  colonist in that state without any extra watching.
- **Correction, 2026-09-24 (hubset chain author, on the owner's review).** (1) The ~11% counts only
  marked colonists at `dome`/`DomeInterior`. `passage` names the building class under a unit, not
  which passage, so the 49 and 104 marked colonists "on passage" were not shown to be on their
  marked hub's passages. The stale share is at least ~11%, not measured at ~11%. (2) The drift
  bullet above is wrong that the anchor line "already selects" everyone showing "Moving to a new
  Dome". That status follows `command` (`Transport`, `TransportByFoot`, `MigrateStep`;
  `Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:4647-4649`, archived 1.1.1). The anchor line selects only own-home rescues
  (`t.dest_dome == c.dome` with `source_landing_site`), and two passes are two instants, so
  rescues that started and ended between them were not seen. (3) This entry's repair shares
  C114's on-hub test, which hubset 04 now derives from source. The owner ruled "One sitting only";
  the harm observation stays with 07, driven from slots.

## 2026-09-24 — hubset 05 build at `76d1ed8`; pending play

- **SOURCE, archived game 1.1.1.405907.** The passage destructor sets the marker on hub arrival
  and clears it only on dome-side exit (`Lua/Passage.lua:1225-1239`). `Unit:UpdateOutside` needs both
  holder and marker absent (`Lua/Units/Unit.lua:468-470`); `IsUnitInDome` uses a present holder
  instead of position (`Lua/Buildings/Dome.lua:159-165`). `Unit:Step` advances the logical position
  and explicitly warns it can lead the visual position (`Lua/Units/Unit.lua:129-141`).
- **BUILD, `hubset` `76d1ed8`.** `Code/Fix_HubMarkerDeparture.lua` synchronously observes
  `Unit:Step`, `Colonist:StopMoving` and `Colonist:SetHolderOnMap`. A valid same-map marker stays
  through an active passage traversal or while either logical or visual position is on the hub.
  After verified departure, it clears the marker, drops a stale hub holder through native
  `SetHolder(false)` bookkeeping, then calls native `UpdateOutside`. It does not alter every
  `ExitHolder`, add a polling thread, persist a field, or copy a blocking body (§3a layer 3).
  The module pins the archived marker lifetime body and declines on an unknown marker/hub layout.
- **DESK, [archived output](../../archive/logs/hubset05_c114_c116_desk_2026-09-24.txt).**
  `python -B tools/desk_c116_hub_marker.py` on `hubset` shows an off-hub walker loses the marker
  and starts the outside timer; a real traversal ramp and a visual position still on the hub keep
  shelter; the next visual step clears it; a stale holder is removed with its holder-list entry;
  an indoor stale holder stays sheltered by the dome. The module-absent walk retains its marker,
  and a changed marker shape declines without installing a wrapper. `python tools/parsecheck.py`
  and `python tools/doccheck.py` were GREEN on `hubset` at the build commit.
- **§3a load disposition and limit.** No load-time cleanup is installed: `OnMsg.LoadGame` timing
  relative to restored traversal state was not established on the desk, so a sweep could strip
  shelter from a real in-flight colonist. Existing stale markers are repaired at the next observed
  movement or holder transition; an idle marked colonist may retain its marker until then. The
  fixture supplies movement and visual coordinates. Link 07 must test a real walk-off, ramp,
  save/reload and outside timer; it also settles whether an overland walk-off occurs in play.
  The harmful open-ground combination remains unobserved. `fixed` means built and desk verified,
