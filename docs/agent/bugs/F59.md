### F59 — Freed housing never notifies homeless (P2, med)  `[fixed*: Code/Fix_FreedHousingNotice.lua — the RemoveResident half; the CancelResidenceReservation site is deliberately not hooked, see below]`
`RemoveResident` (`Residence.lua:83-90`) and `CancelResidenceReservation` (:353-365) never
call `CheckHomeForHomeless`; homeless rely on Idle heavy-update throttled to 12 game hours
at 3600+ pop (`City.lua:118-120`). **Fix:** post-hook `RemoveResident` →
`CheckHomeForHomeless()`.
*Implemented one level up, on better evidence:* the post-hook is on
`Colonist:SetResidence` (`Colonist.lua:2291-2307`), the only caller of `RemoveResident`.
Same event, but the notification then runs when the move is FINISHED. `RemoveResident` is
called from the MIDDLE of `SetResidence`, before `home:AddResident(self)` and before
`self.residence` is updated; waking the homeless there lets one of them take the slot the
colonist in hand is about to occupy, and the next shipped statement is
`assert(self:GetFreeSpace() > 0)` followed by an unconditional insert (`Residence.lua:
74-77`) — assert does not unwind in this engine, so that is an over-capacity residence.
The hook no-ops unless a home was actually left and `GetFreeSpace() > 0` in it.
*Open half:* `Residence:CancelResidenceReservation` is NOT hooked. `Residence:AddResident`
releases the incoming colonist's reservation (`:74`) one line before the same
`assert(self:GetFreeSpace() > 0)`, so a notification from there reintroduces exactly that
race. The reservation case is instead bounded by F58's daily stale-reservation sweep plus
the normal heavy update. Probe: `FreedHousingNotice` in `30_Probes_Wave3.lua`.

## 2026-09-11 — HARMFUL on the 1.1.0 expedition boarding sequence; audit stopped

The original 1.0.7 record above is preserved. **SOURCE**, game 1.1.0.403908:
`Colonist:EnterTransporter` saves `expedition_residence` before
`SetDome(false)` (`Lua/Units/Colonist.lua:5027-5040`). `SetDome` calls
`SetResidence(false)` (`:410-435`), invoking our immediate vacancy notification
after removing the resident (`:2898-2917`). `Residence:CheckHomeForHomeless`
(`Lua/Buildings/Residence.lua:160-168`) can synchronously assign the bed to a
suitable homeless neighbour through `UpdateResidence` (`Colonist.lua:2919-2926`).
Only later does `OnDisappear` try to reserve the expedition home (`:5003-5007`).
A full home fails `CanReserveResidence` (`Residence.lua:283-288`), clearing the hold.

**INFERRED ROUTE, R2:** a housed expedition crew member reaches boarding while
an eligible, command-changeable homeless colonist shares their full residence's
dome. The crew loses the intended return-home reservation because our notification
runs before the larger operation finishes. This is caused by our module, not a
new vanilla defect. Earlier transport may change the home; record it at boarding.

**DESK-CONTROLLED**, `tools/desk_f59_expedition.py`: 6/6 demands held with
extracted shipped `EnterTransporter`, `SetDome`, `SetResidence`, `OnDisappear`,
selection and reservation bodies and whole F59/F58 modules. Module absent:
crew retains its reservation. F59 applied: neighbour takes the bed and the hold
is lost. No competing neighbour: F59 retains the hold. Current F58 cannot
protect or restore a reservation that was never created. The Unit transport
boundary, labels, UI, comfort and geometry are stubs; no engine run or expedition
return was observed. Transcript: `reports/MIGRATION_DESK_RESULTS.txt`.

**Disposition:** HARMFUL on the controlled state; **no fix built**. The audit
stopped under its explicit harmful-module rule. Checklist **151** asks the owner
to prioritize a repair preserving expedition holds; temporary disable/removal is
a separate owner choice. Historical `status`/heading remain the 1.0.7 record;
the generated title exposes current harm alongside the historical status. Mechanism,
unrun numbered A/B recipe and vacuity conditions: `reports/MIGRATION_DEV_REPORT.md` §1.

## 2026-09-11 — resumed audit: repair, not retirement; candidate remains unbuilt

Owner authorized continued checks and findings after the stop. **SOURCE:** the
ordinary notification gap remains in `Residence.lua:119-126`,
`Building.lua:3276-3318`, `Community.lua:361-364`, and `Colonist.lua:2898-2917`.
These update occupancy/cache/own state, not the homeless neighbour. Periodic
housing assignment still runs at `Colonist.lua:2338-2358`; `City.lua:118` caps its
heavy interval at 12 hours at 3,600 colonists. Other events can shorten the delay.

**DESK-CONTROLLED:** expanded harness **12/12**. Ordinary vacancy: vanilla leaves
an eligible neighbour homeless until a later housing update; F59 assigns it
immediately. An **in-memory, unbuilt candidate** captures whether the departing
home equals `self.expedition_residence` before the original setter, then excludes
that exact home from notification. It preserves the expedition hold with F58
loaded, still notifies ordinary departures, and does not suppress an unrelated
home merely because an expedition pointer exists. No `Code/` changes.

**INFERRED recommendation:** repair F59 with a narrowly reviewed expedition
exclusion, rather than retire the ordinary notification. Before a build, review
failed boarding, cancellation, return, and stale expedition intent; skipping the
helper can delay notification about other free beds. Reservation cancellation
remains unhooked. Report §1/§1a contains separate numbered recipes and alternatives.
Audit complete; checklist **151** retains implementation and sitting decisions.
