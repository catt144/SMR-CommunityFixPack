**PASSING. SOURCE intent tell:** misplaced lifetime cleanup plus an exact
sibling contradiction. Old `Lua/Factions/Legislature.lua`

completed-task expiry loop nested inside `if task` at `:1478-1496`, even though
the new comment says the checker runs hourly. Completed Faction Promises are

**SOURCE ROUTE — R1, ordinary base/non-owner politics:**
`FactionTask:Complete`, new `:1437-1447`, clears `active_faction_task`, appends
the completed task and stamps a 15-sol expiration. `FactionDef:EvalApproval`,
new `Lua/Factions/FactionDef.lua:101-109`, consumes every retained completed
task; `FactionTask:Eval`, Legislature `:1462-1468`, adds the temporary `0.6`

`Data/FactionDef/*.lua` supplies many actual opportunity definitions.

**MEASURED desk control, not runtime:** the archived 1.1 checker retains one
expired completed task when `active_faction_task=false`. A harmless active-task
counterfactual makes the same body remove it. The exact output and named shims
are in `PROGRESS_SEAM_DESK.txt` / `tools/desk_progress_seam.py`.

**INFERRED severity:** a temporary approval reward can persist indefinitely
and alter politics until another opportunity happens to be active on an hourly
check. The duration and player-visible approval effect are source-derived, not
observed.

**Independent recipe / falsifier:** complete a normal Faction Opportunity at
`t0`, record its expiration, and pass `t0+15 sols` without another active
opportunity. Inspect `completed_faction_tasks` and the approval explanation;
then activate a harmless opportunity as the removal control. Vacuous if a new
opportunity is active on the first post-expiry hourly tick. An independent
cleanup or an approval evaluator that rejects expired entries would falsify
the player-facing effect; neither appears in the archived Lua. No fix is
proposed.
