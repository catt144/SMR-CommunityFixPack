### ⛔⛔ THE ORGANIC CLICK PRODUCED A NULL READING, AND THE REASON IS A REAL TRAP FOR ANY FUTURE SITTING

The owner answered *"Prisoners of Light"* → *Symbiotic coexistence* at
**day 85, hour 14 — the middle of the afternoon**. Wisps detach and hide in
their sinkhole at 4 AM (`Firefly:GoHome`, `Fireflies.lua:408-424`), so the trap
held **zero** wisps: `wisps=0 | el_prod_modifier{amount=0} | work_state=work_empty`,
and the leg's own guard fired — `PT15-wisp-population = 0 | APPLICABLE=false`.
`Change(0)` is indistinguishable between broken and fixed.

**Two compounding causes, both worth recording:**

1. **⛔ The choice popup is MODAL AND LOCKS THE CONSOLE.** Owner, verbatim:
   *"I cannot do anything until I answer game locks console and all buttons
   until a choice is made."* ⇒ the "call `TrapRead` BEFORE the choice" step that
   this chain's brief and the payload menu both specify is **impossible on this
   beat**. Any future sitting must plan for an after-only read.
2. **⚠️ It also does not appear to hold game time.** `fireflies_caught` was 30
   when the popup fired and **33** at the first read after it, so catches
   continued while it sat open — and the clock ran from night into afternoon
   while the agent was reading source. **The agent's instruction to pause and
   not answer is what created that delay**; the wisp-count precondition was
   raised only afterwards. Recorded as a process failure, not a game defect.

⇒ **The recovery was a better experiment than the one lost**: same trap, same
95 wisps, before/after on a single variable, game paused, all at one
