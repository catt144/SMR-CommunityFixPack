### C106 - C83's arrival fallback can pick a habitat that refuses the colonist (P3) `[cand 2026-09-17: observed once live; no module-off control; owner undecided whether to pursue]`

⚖️ **Owner, 2026-09-17:** *"I am unsure if we are gonna mess with this, its such a niche situation
and really blurring the lines."* Pursue or drop is the owner's call. Do not build from this entry.

#### What was seen

In the C95 reporter's colony, four Naturalist Habitats are the only communities with life support;
the three domes are unpowered (log `:505-511`). Every habitat's filter forbids all specializations
but geologist, which scores +1 (`:587-597`). The owner imported six botanists through a normal
passenger rocket, with SMRTK skipping only the transit time.

- On the first landing, `ArrivalDeaths: C83 rerouted table: 0000023E8A0662B8 from Novo #1 to ` (`:354`).
- After a reload and a second import, all six were assigned habitat **2669**, homeless, each with
  score `-900` and `suitable false` there (`:598-603`).
- Owner, watching: four went inside and did not appear on its residents list; two stood outside.
  Later the habitat ejected them and a shuttle carried them toward a dome. That was not logged, and
  whether that dome was one of the dead ones was not read.

#### Mechanism (SOURCE, game 1.1.0.403908)

Vanilla `ChooseDome` seeds its choice with `safety_dome` at score `CommunityEvalNone` (-700) and
takes any candidate scoring higher that has space (`_GameUtils.lua:486-501`). A forbidden filter
trait costs -1000 (`Filter.lua:6-11`), so vanilla alone sends these botanists to the safety dome,
the nearest dead dome here. Habitats opt out of being a safety dome (`MicroGHabitat.lua:14`).

C83 (`Code/Fix_ArrivalDeaths.lua:455-477`) replaces an unwelcoming destination with the nearest
community passing `is_welcoming_arrival_dome` (`:375-381`): accepting, working, alive, and room or
tourist. That test does not check the colonist's admission (`IsSuitable`), nor
`can_be_safety_dome`, so a habitat that refuses the colonist qualifies and becomes `ChooseDome`'s
seed. The game's own save fix-up `SavegameFixups.HomelessInNaturalistHabitat2`
(`NaturalHabitat.lua:15-24`) treats a homeless colonist roaming in a habitat as a broken state.

The outcome is alive but homeless beside or inside a habitat, where vanilla would have been dead.
It is better than vanilla and still wrong. C102's expedition path does not share it: it admits only
`Dome` candidates (`Fix_ArrivalDeaths.lua:405`).

**The log line** (`Fix_ArrivalDeaths.lua:489-492`) formats `self.name`, a translation table, and
`new_dome.name`, blank for a habitat. It is C83's only evidence line and fires once per launch.

#### Control (not run)

On a copy of the reporter's save, with the filters as shipped, import botanists twice in separate
launches: once with the pack on and once with `ArrivalDeaths` vetoed. The claim holds if the pack-on
arrivals are assigned a habitat with `IsSuitable false` and the vetoed arrivals are assigned a dead
dome. It is refuted if the pack-on arrivals are assigned a dome, or the habitat accepts them.

#### Scope and reach

The case needs a colony whose only live communities are habitats, filters that refuse the
arrivals, and arrivals with no dome that can house them. That is an early aesthetic layout, as this
reporter built. Every platform, ordinary play.
