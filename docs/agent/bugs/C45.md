## What the log says

One block, once, `docs/archive/cb2sitting_Mars.exe-20260810-15.30.16.log:2587-2599`,
timestamped `Lua 1:28:33:535` (wall ≈16:58:49):

The stack is quoted WHOLE (batch-1's one real audit finding was a truncated
stack quote). "Quantum Comet" is a rocket's display name; the shape is: a
rocket generating its departure list asked whether a colonist can reach it by
train, and `ColonistTransport:GetStartingPoint` found a starting position that
is invalid "with no holder" — `Station.lua:1254` then logs the error
deliberately. It is an engine error-LOG, not an unhandled throw: **no
`[LUA ERROR]`, no traceback marker, and execution continued** (the next log
line is a normal leg R volley 4 block).

## When it fired — the window, honestly

Leg R's bombardment window: volley 3 (8 missiles onto `DomeBasic#1243`) ended
`1:23:50`; this line is `1:28:33`; volley 4 launched `1:29:42`. In between the
owner was disabling the MDS lasers for the A/B. So the colony was three forced
volleys into a bombardment when a rocket generated departures. ⚠️ **That is
context, not attribution** — nothing establishes whether bombardment damage
produced the invalid position or whether this fires on ordinary saves too.

## What IS established

- **Vanilla.** Every frame is a shipped `Lua/` file; no pack or TestKit frame
  appears. (Caveat: a tail-called wrapper would not leave a frame; but no pack
  module wraps `GetStartingPoint`, `GetTransportRoute` or `GenerateDepartures`.)
- **First-seen.** Zero occurrences of `invalid pos with no holder` in any other
  archived log on this machine.
- **Why it was unreported upstream:** leg R's banked card claimed "0 engine
  error lines across the whole log" — true for `[LUA ERROR]` (the grep the
  sitting ran), false for `[ERROR]`. The audit's whole-log read found it; the
  never-silently-discount rule is why this entry exists.

## What is NOT established

Cause, recurrence, and harm. One line, one session, a heavily-forced window.
The `Station.lua:1254` context cannot be Src-verified on the sitting machine
(no `ModTools\Src` there — batch-2 ledger S12); the line numbers above are the
stack's own.

## What would settle it

Cheap and passive: grep every future log for `invalid pos with no holder`.
A recurrence OUTSIDE a bombardment window makes it an ordinary-play defect
worth a source read (`Station.lua:1254` and `ColonistTransport.lua:33`, Src
machine); a recurrence only under bombardment makes it collateral of forced
volleys. Until either happens it stays `cand`, one occurrence, nothing built.
