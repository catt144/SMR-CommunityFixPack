### C69 — Number Six's intensified-attack phase collapses to research completion `[cand 2026-09-10: source-read; desk control only]`

**DIFF-CAUSED. SOURCE intent tell:** `SA_WaitResearch` still offers
`"In Progress"` as an authored state (`Lua/Sequences/SA_Gameplay.lua:858-869`),
but its new `SAExec` handles only `Available`; every other value waits for
`TechResearched` (`:871-882`). The old body had a distinct In Progress branch
that returned when the target became the active research (`old :849-864`).

**SOURCE ROUTE — R1, base/non-owner:** the AI mystery retains an In Progress
wait for `NumberSixTracing` (`Lua/Scenario/Mystery 5.generated.lua:369-376`).
It then announces a large intensified Rogue Drone attack and starts `Rogues
Generator (Intensifier)` (`:378-395`), immediately follows with a Researched
wait (`:399-403`), and later stops the intensifier (`:430-432`). In 1.1.0 the
first wait already lasts to completion, so the second is satisfied immediately.
This shipped mystery route has no DLC dependency.

**DESK CONTROL:** `tools/desk_caller_seam.py` executes both archived SAExec
bodies with an active, uncompleted `NumberSixTracing`. Old returns without a
wait; new waits on `TechResearched`. Both return immediately in the already-
researched negative control. Shims are `UIColony` research queries and `WaitMsg`,
which records its first message then completes research to avoid a fake hang.

**Independent recipe / falsifier:** in a fresh 1.1.0 AI-mystery colony, reach
the reveal of Number Six Tracing and begin that research. Observe when **AI: En
Garde** appears and whether the intensified generator/attack persists until
completion. Vacuous if the tech is completed instantly or the active-research
transition is not observed. If En Garde starts when research becomes active and
persists through the research interval, the candidate is false. No fix is
proposed.
