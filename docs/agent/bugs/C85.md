### C85 — "Clogged after a Dust Storm" has no fallback repair (P2) `[fixed 2026-09-12: Fix_CloggedBuildingRelease, the sweep only (ck154); desk-controlled, UNTESTED in play — ck158]`

**Field reports (INHERITED — owner screenshots, Steam "Clogged Extractor", 2026-09-09):** a Rare Metals Extractor
"clogged after a Dust Storm. … I can't seem to fix it"; a second player's Polymer factory "never recovered", and destroy +
rebuild was the only way out; the first player confirms rebuilding too. Triage record:
`reports/FIELD_LEADS_2026-09-11.md` §3.

#### What "Clogged" is (SOURCE, game 1.1.0.403908)

Not a maintenance state: the story bit `BuildingClogged` (`Data/StoryBit/BuildingClogged.lua`).

- It fires at a dust storm's start (`trigger = "DustStormStart"`, category `DustStromStart`, `:9`, `:46-50`), only through
  sol 80 (`IsSolInRange` `Max 80`, `:21-23`), on one Water / Regolith / Metals / Rare Metals Extractor, Polymer Plant,
  Fuel Factory or Fungal Farm (`:24-39`). One-time: `OneTime` defaults to `true` (`Lua/ClassDef-StoryBits.lua:28-29`).
- Its ActivationEffects disable the building with the reason "Clogged after a Dust Storm." (`:4-8`), BEFORE the player
  answers; `Delay = 60000` (`:10`).
- The replies:
  - "Send a colonist" → re-enabled at once, 70% with a colonist lost (`:51-87`).
  - The inventor-commander reply → re-enabled (`:113-127`).
  - "Have the drones replace the entire component" → a 3-Electronics malfunction that a drone repair clears (`:98-112`).
  - **"We'll fix it after the storm" → only enables the follow-up `BuildingClogged_1_FixAfterStorm`** (`:88-97`).
- The follow-up re-enables the building (`Data/StoryBit/BuildingClogged_1_FixAfterStorm.lua:4-8`) on
  `trigger = "DustStormEnd_FollowUps"` with `Delay = 30000` (`:9`, `:16-19`), and inherits the building (`InheritsObject`
  defaults to `true`, `ClassDef-StoryBits.lua:23-24`).
- **One follow-up per firing:** each trigger first tries its follow-ups (`Lua/_StoryBits.lua:838-843`), and
  `StoryBitCategoryState:TryActivateStoryBit` shuffles them and activates only the first whose prerequisites pass, then
  `break`s (`:199-216`).

**ROUTE (INFERRED):** nothing else clears the reason. Ordinary maintenance doesn't (INHERITED:
`RequiresMaintenance.lua:413-417` needs the drone reply's flag), and the building's on/off button drives a separate flag
(INHERITED). After "fix it after the storm", the building waits for a storm-end firing that this follow-up wins.

## 2026-09-11 — mechanism resolved: the story bit has NO fallback, and vanilla's own safety net is one field away

Established after the sections above were written, in a session prompted by a
second field report (Reddit + the owner's Steam pair). All **SOURCE** on
**1.1.0.403908** unless marked. These supersede nothing above; they narrow it.

**(a) The engine ALREADY has the fallback, and this story bit does not use it.**
`SetBuildingEnabledState` (`Lua/ClassDefs/ClassDef-Effects.generated.lua:2770-2785`)
has two branches. With a `Duration` it spawns a game-time thread that sleeps and
then **re-enables the building by itself**; without one it takes the permanent
`else`. Two other shipped events DO pass one —
`DLC/norman/Presets/Event/BugAppetit.lua:26` (3 sols) and
`KitchenRescue_Reopening.lua:44` (5 sols). `BuildingClogged`'s ActivationEffects
pass **only a `Reason`** (`Data/StoryBit/BuildingClogged.lua:4-8`).
⇒ the strongest available evidence that this is an **oversight, not design**, and
the argument to give Paradox.

**(b) Those vanilla durations DO survive a save/load** — checked, because it
decides whether a `Duration` patch would be hollow. `CreateGameTimeThread` threads
are persistable **by default**: `OnMsg.PersistSave` serialises every thread
carrying `threadPersist` together with its sleep state
(`CommonLua/Core/cthreads.lua:481-517`), `PersistLoad` restores them (`:519-524`),
and the `permanents` table registers `Sleep`/`WaitWakeup`/`WaitMsg`/`WaitThread` as
resumable stack functions (`:470-478`). Real-time threads must opt in via
`MakeThreadPersistable`; the engine's own branching shows the asymmetry
(`Libs/Notifications/Notifications.lua:245-251`, `Classes/ActionFX.lua:1186-1191`,
`Libs/TriggersAndEvents/Events.lua:172-177`).

**(c) A lost reply is PERMANENT, not delayed — this is the mechanism H1 lacks.**
In `Lua/_StoryBits.lua` the entire outcome path sits inside `if reply then`. With
no reply it falls through to `ProcessOutcomeEffects(storybit, ...)` and
`Complete()`, and because the bit is `OneTime`, `Complete()` does **not**
re-register it. ⇒ the story bit is **finished and gone** while the building stays
disabled and no follow-up was ever armed. H1 predicts a *delay*; both reporters
describe *permanence* ("never recovered", destroy-and-rebuild the only way out).
⚠️ INFERRED that this is the route the reporters took — the routes into a nil
reply are still H2's (a save/load or quit-to-menu with the popup open).

**(d) REFUTED, so nobody re-derives it: the player cannot Escape the popup.**
`disallow_escape = #choices > 1` (`Lua/MarsStoryBits.lua:79`), and all four replies
are always present — none carries `HideIfDisabled`
(`BuildingClogged.lua:51/88/98/113`) — so `#choices` is 4.

**(e) The stuck state is two saved fields, so detection is exact and the cure is
one call.** `exceptional_circumstances = true` and
`exceptional_circumstances_reason` = that `T`, both declared on
`Lua/Buildings/BaseBuilding.lua:30-31`. Read the loc id with `TGetID`
(`CommonLua/Core/localization.lua:48`), which handles the packed-userdata and
table forms. The repair is the game's own setter,
`building:Setexceptional_circumstances(false)` (`:470-480`) — it clears the reason,
runs `UpdateWorking`/`UpdateConsumption`/`AttachSign`, and its `:474` already
guards the reason-clear behind `exceptional_circumstances_maintenance`.

### Fix shape — PROPOSED, owner has not chosen (supersedes the older sketch above)

**A read-only sweep, on load + daily**, keyed on `exceptional_circumstances` and
`TGetID(...) == 789863173059`, repairing via `Setexceptional_circumstances(false)`,
enumerating with the shipped idiom `AllMapsForEach` (as the devs' own fixups do,
`Residence.lua:634-642`). **⛔ Two interlocks, both `GameVar`s and both read-only
to us — never unstick a building that is legitimately waiting:** `g_StoryBitActive`
(the bit is running now, popup unanswered) and `g_StoryBitStates`
(`BuildingClogged_1_FixAfterStorm` armed and pending for it).

**Why the sweep over a `Duration` DataPatch:** the Duration is spawned at trigger
time, so it cannot help a player **already** stranded — and both reporters are in
exactly that state. The sweep also writes **nothing** new to the save, patches no
shipped preset, adds no UI, and no-ops if Paradox fixes the bit. The Duration's one
advantage is that it is vanilla's own idiom. ⚠️ A Duration also fires
`AddEventOnScreenNotification`, i.e. a visible countdown — a UI addition, which is
`FIX_POLICY` §4 and the **owner's** call.

## 2026-09-12 — BUILT: the sweep, `Code/Fix_CloggedBuildingRelease.lua` (owner ruling, checklist 154 — sweep only)

Built from `prompts/C85_C88_BUILD.md` (now consumed). The owner ruled the **sweep alone**: no `Duration`
DataPatch, no UI. The trade above is not re-litigated — it is settled by that ruling.

**Shape.** `OnMsg.LoadGame` + `OnMsg.NewDay`, both through `SMRFixPack.WhenActive`. Detection is the two
saved fields (`exceptional_circumstances` true **and** `TGetID(exceptional_circumstances_reason) ==
789863173059`); the cure is the game's own `building:Setexceptional_circumstances(false)`; enumeration is
`AllMapsForEach("map", "BaseBuilding", ...)`, the idiom the developers' own `ReconcileNotWorkingNotifications`
uses (`BaseBuilding.lua:413-421`). Save footprint **none** — no thread, no new field, no GameVar; the only
writes are to two fields the game already saves and already writes with this setter.

**The setter is called with ONE argument, deliberately.** With `reason` nil the shipped body clears the reason
only when `exceptional_circumstances_maintenance` is false (`:474`) — which is what vanilla's own re-enable
does (`RequiresMaintenance:Repaired` `:417`, the Duration thread at
`ClassDef-Effects.generated.lua:2779`). Passing an explicit `false` would clear a LIVE maintenance reason,
because `reason ~= nil` is true for `false`. Where a maintenance state coexists the infopanel reads it first
anyway (`Building.lua:2930-2935` precedes the `exceptional_circumstances` branch at `:2942`), so no stale text
is shown.

### ⭐ The brief's three desk checks, answered — and one of them overturned an interlock

**(1) Does the reason survive save/load as a `T`?** `TGetID` handles the packed-userdata form and both table
forms (`CommonLua/Core/localization.lua:47-64`), so any round-trip shape reads back. ⚠️ The packed-userdata
form goes through `LightUserdataToLocId`, a C export with **no desk equivalent** — the harness says so rather
than faking it, so that one form is covered by reading the shipped branch, not by a leg.

**(2) The pending `g_StoryBitStates` shape.** `g_StoryBitStates` is keyed by story-bit **id** →
`StoryBitState` (`_StoryBits.lua:129`, `:300`), so at most ONE follow-up is armed at a time; the state carries
`.id`, `.object`, `.map` (`:274-285`) and the follow-up inherits the building (`:758-768`). `g_StoryBitActive`
is by contrast an **array** of running states (`:130`). Both shapes are now keyed on exactly.

**(3) The narrow key holds.** Keyed on this one reason id, never on "story-bit-disabled". Other routes into
`exceptional_circumstances` carry their own reasons and are untouched — `LawEffectTurnOffBuildings` uses
`T(374137718365, ...)` and restores its own `{ec, reason}` snapshot on repeal
(`Lua/Factions/LawDef.lua:657-682`): a building our sweep released *before* the law was enacted is snapshotted
as released, and one released *after* repeal is released again on the next pass — no interaction either way.

**⚠️ THE BRIEF'S TWO INTERLOCKS DO NOT COVER THE OPEN POPUP, and a third was added.** `OnStopRunning()` runs
**before** `OpenPopup()` (`_StoryBits.lua:515-521`), so by the time the player is looking at the popup the
state has already been removed from `g_StoryBitActive`, and it unregistered from `g_StoryBitStates` when it
activated (the comment at `:130` says so). In that window the building is disabled and **neither GameVar
knows**. The popup dialog does pause the game (`PopupNotification` declares `dont_pause = false`, so `Init()`
adds an `XPauseLayer` — `Lua/UI/PopupNotification.lua:1-13`), which keeps `NewDay` from firing while it is on
screen — but a context can sit in `g_PopupQueue` **unopened** while `ArePopupsEnabled()` is false (`:383`), and
then nothing is paused. ⇒ **interlock 3: a whole-pass stand-down if any queued context carries `is_storybit`**,
the flag `WaitStoryBitPopup` sets (`MarsStoryBits.lua:71-79`). A queued context carries no object reference, so
it cannot be attributed to one building — fail closed, whole pass. An OPEN popup is still in the queue: a
context is removed only when its dialog closes (`:82-94`).

**The drones reply needs no interlock** (checked, because it looked like one).
`SetBuildingBreakdownState:__exec` calls `Setexceptional_circumstances(not self.EnableBuilding, self.Reason or
nil)` with `EnableBuilding` defaulting **true** and the outcome not overriding it
(`Data/StoryBit/BuildingClogged.lua:103-111`; `Data/ClassDef-Effects.lua:4307` and its generated twin) ⇒ the
building is left with `exceptional_circumstances` **false** and the electronics reason `T(149427596640, ...)`.
The first detection test already excludes it, and the 3,000 Electronics it owes is untouched.

**⚠️ Why the daily pass is not redundant — and the brief's implied route is REFUTED.** An expired notification
does **not** lose the reply: `:606-607` returns true either way and the popup still opens. The real
mid-session route is the dialog closing without a choice — `Msg(context.async_signal, reason)`
(`Lua/UI/PopupNotification.lua:89-94`) — and only a choice index indexes `replies`, so any other close (a UI
teardown, a mode change, any non-numeric reason) yields a nil reply on the spot (`_StoryBits.lua:645-653`) with
the game running on. A `reason == "suspend"` close posts nothing and is not this case (`:78-80`).

**And the load route is now mechanically explained, which the entry only had as H2.** The story-bit popup is
**async**, not sync: `WaitStoryBitPopup` calls `WaitPopupNotification(false, context)` so
`context.async_signal = {}` (`ShowPopupNotification`), and `OnMsg.PersistSave` keeps **only** `sync_popup_id`
contexts (`:390-397`). A save taken with the popup up therefore drops it; on load `g_PopupQueue` is restored
from that sync-only list (`:400`) and the run thread waits on a signal nobody will post. Building disabled, no
follow-up armed, nothing in either GameVar ⇒ exactly the reporters' state, and exactly what the load sweep
releases. ⭐ This closes H2's mechanism. It remains INFERRED that this is the route the two reporters took.

**⚠️ NOT REPAIRED, ON PURPOSE — H1 stays open as a residual.** A follow-up that is armed but never wins its
`DustStormEnd_FollowUps` pick (one per trigger, `:838-843` + `:199-216`), or a colony where terraforming has
stopped dust storms (`TerraformingDisasters.lua:15-19`), leaves the building waiting forever — and interlock 2
deliberately leaves it alone, because the player accepted "the building will be turned off" and releasing it
would overrule their own choice. Filed here, not fixed.

### Branch guard (`FIX_POLICY` §2a) — two halves, no version check anywhere

* `apply()` carries a **behaviour probe on the cure**: it drives the shipped setter on a closed stub and
  requires one call to clear BOTH fields. Stub contract: the body reads/writes three of its own fields and
  calls exactly three methods on `self` (`UpdateWorking`, `UpdateConsumption`, `AttachSign`), touches no
  global, allocates nothing and cannot yield — synchronous and side-effect-free, shown from `:470-480`.
* The **data** half can only be read where it exists (`FIX_POLICY` §2's F110/F75 rules: a preset absent at the
  menu proves nothing), so the sweep re-checks the shipped story bit every pass and **latches the module
  inactive** when the reason id moves or a `Duration` appears — the day the developers ship the fix we are
  asking them for, this module stands itself down and logs "already correct, RETIRE candidate" for
  `tools/logscan.py`.
