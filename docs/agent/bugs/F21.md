### ⛔ The `nil` was the instrument, not the game — the reader gap is CLOSED by reading (2026-08-10, Src 1.0.7.396349)

The batch-1 reader asked each train for `t.ts_spent_time or t.spent_time`.
**Neither field exists on any class in the tree.** `TransportStatistics`
(`Lua\TransportStatistics.lua:1-11`) declares exactly:
`spent_time_sum` (number), `spent_time_values` (array, capped at 20 samples —
`:31-37`), `visitors_per_day`, `visitors_lifetime`, `full_today`,
`full_yesterday`, `trip_time`, `last_reset`. So `spent_time=nil` was a
guaranteed nil against a name the engine never had: a reader that could not
have printed anything else, on any save, in any state. Not a verdict about
this build, and not evidence that the statistic is absent.

**Where the penalty actually lives, all three spends, all readable live:**

| what | where to read it |
|---|---|
| the single source of all three spends | `colonist.transport_ticket.start_wait` (a `GameTime()` stamp) — `ColonistTransport.lua:493` stamps it on reaching the platform; **our wrapper restamps it at boarding** |
| the rolling average the train's UI shows | `train:GetSpentTimeAverage()` / `:GetSpentTimeAverageInHours()` over `train.spent_time_values` (`TransportStatistics.lua:39-46`) |
| the same statistic on the track | `vehicle.track:GetSpentTimeAverage()` — `TrainTrack` reaches it via `StationsLink` (`StationsLink.lua:5`) |
| the station's side (the WAIT, which is *supposed* to keep the full figure) | `station:GetSpentTimeAverage()` — `Station.lua:52` |
| the Comfort charge itself | computed at `ExitVehicle` from `GameTime() - ticket.start_wait` (`ColonistTransport.lua:551`), spent at `:555-557` (skipped once `LuxuriousTrains` is researched) and again at `:568-569` |

Three parents of `TransportStatistics` in the whole tree — `Station`,
`StationsLink`, `Train` — so the class list is closed, not sampled.

⭐ **The sharp read this makes possible, and it witnesses the wrapper directly
rather than inferring from an average.** `transport_ticket.stage` moves
`"Inbound"` → `"Waiting"` (`:496`) → `"Traveling"` (set in `BoardVehicle`'s
destructor, `:515`). So: pick a colonist in `station.waiting_for_train`, record
`ticket.start_wait`, poll until `ticket.stage == "Traveling"`, read
`start_wait` again.

- **restamped (changed, ≈ `GameTime()` at boarding) ⇒ the wrapper fired** — the
  train, the track and the Comfort charge will all measure the ride only.
- **unchanged ⇒ the wrapper did not fire** on that boarding, and F21's defect is
  live for that rider.

Both outcomes are recorded off one subject; neither can be produced by an absent
fixture, because the subject is only chosen once they are *in* the waiting list.
⚠️ **Still not a `tested` re-earn on its own** — that stays what the rider says
it is. What this closes is the reader gap that made the 2026-08-05 half
unmeasurable.
