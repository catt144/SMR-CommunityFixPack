- ⛔⛔ **PARADOX MODS HAS NO COMMENT CHANNEL AT ALL, and its in-game "Report" is a
  MODERATION FLAG — telling a player to use it for a bug report would flag our
  own mod for Paradox QA** (source-read 2026-08-20, prompted by the owner asking
  whether they had missed a comment route on the live page).
  * **What the in-game Paradox browser actually offers a player, exhaustively:**
    1. **A star rating, text-free.** `ModsUIRateMod` → `AsyncPdxRateMod(mod_id,
       rating)` (`CommonLua/UI/ModManager.lua:383-396`), template
       `Lua/XTemplates/ModsUIRate.lua`. It carries a number and nothing else.
    2. **A "Report <ModName>" flag.** `ModsUIChooseFlagReason` (`:336-344`) →
       `ModsUIFlagMod` → `AsyncPdxReportMod{ModID, Reason, Description}`
       (`:346-366`), template `Lua/XTemplates/ModsUIFlag.lua`.
  * ⛔ **The flag is NOT a channel to the author, and the dialog says so in its
    own words** (`ModsUIFlag.lua:49`): *"If you believe this mod violates our
    Terms and Conditions, help us review it by flagging it for moderation.
    Please fill in the details below and we'll submit it to our QA team for
    analysis."* The reasons it offers are **Nudity · Spam · Copyright
    infringement · Inappropriate** (`ModManager.lua:1979-1985`).
    ⇒ **A player who used it to report a bug would be reporting our mod for
    moderation.** ⛔ No player-facing surface of ours may ever suggest it.
  * **Whole-layer control:** `grep -i "comment|review|rating|feedback|report"`
    over `CommonLua/Libs/Paradox/ParadoxMods.lua` returns **nothing** — the
    upload/download layer has no comment concept to expose.
  * **Website side (weaker evidence, recorded as such):** the live mod page
    fetches 200 and its markup contains no `comment`/`discussion`/`review`
    string. ⚠️ It is a JS-rendered page, so absence in the fetched HTML is
    suggestive, not conclusive; the owner's own reading of the live page agrees,
    and the in-game source above is what actually settles it.
  * ⇒ **CONSEQUENCE FOR EVERY PLAYER SURFACE WE WRITE.** Steam has comments;
    Paradox has none — and **Paradox is the console route**, so *"leave a comment
    on the mod page"* is a route that does not exist for Xbox and PlayStation
    players. The site's FAQ asserted exactly that until 2026-08-20 and it was
    corrected the same day. The only reporting route that works for a
    non-Steam player is the issue tracker, from any browser.
